## Epistemic status

Quantum tunnelling in some enzymatic hydrogen-transfer reactions is well supported, but its quantitative contribution varies by reaction. Enzyme catalysis is not explained by tunnelling alone. The RPE65 “quantum cliff” is a recent preprint requiring independent validation. The Platonic interpretation is philosophical and does not follow experimentally from tunnelling data.
