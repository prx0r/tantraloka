# life crosses barriers it cannot climb

## Tradition/domain

Quantum biology, enzymatic hydrogen transfer, and a carefully bounded Platonic-biology interpretation.

## Central thesis

Living systems do not violate classical barriers; enzymes shape molecular geometry, electric fields, and conformational ensembles so that quantum tunnelling or other proton-transfer mechanisms become functionally reliable. The essay uses this to explore how form selects latent physical possibilities without claiming that quantum biology proves Platonism or consciousness-based causation.

## Suggested visual motifs

- a proton wave decaying through a finite barrier;
- donor and acceptor atoms moving closer as an enzyme “breathes”;
- hydrogen versus deuterium crossing probabilities;
- a triangular form inheriting mathematical relations;
- water-wire proton gates inside cytochrome c oxidase;
- a wall becoming thin through architecture rather than force.

## Production notes

- Distinguish proton, hydrogen-atom, and hydride transfer visually where possible.
- Do not depict tunnelling as a tiny ball travelling through a secret tunnel.
- Treat the 2026 RPE65 “quantum cliff” as an unvalidated preprint hypothesis.
- Do not imply that enzymatic tunnelling validates quantum consciousness, intention-driven physics, or medical treatment claims.
