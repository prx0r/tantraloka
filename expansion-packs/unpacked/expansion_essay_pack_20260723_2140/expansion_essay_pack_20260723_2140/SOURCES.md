# SOURCES

## Essay 1 — your voice existed before language

### Primary texts
- *Śiva Sūtras*, Section II, especially 2.1: `cittaṃ mantraḥ`.
- Kṣemarāja’s commentary tradition on the *Śiva Sūtras*.
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrāloka* and *Parātrīśikā* commentarial traditions concerning mantra, phonemes, levels of speech, and the power of consciousness.
- André Padoux and Mark S. G. Dyczkowski on mantra, *mātṛkā*, phonemic cosmology, and Kashmir Śaivism.

### Epistemic status
Historical-philosophical and contemplative. Claims about phonemes as ontological powers belong to the Śaiva tradition and are not presented as modern acoustic science.

## Essay 2 — an organism survives by predicting itself

### Current scientific research
- Pio-Lopez, L., Kuchling, F., Tung, A., Pezzulo, G., and Levin, M. “Active inference, morphogenesis, and computational psychiatry.” *Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience* 16 (2022): 988977. DOI: 10.3389/fncom.2022.988977.
- Friston and collaborators on active inference, variational free energy, agency, perception, and action.
- Seifert, G. et al. “From reinforcement learning to agency: Frameworks for understanding basal cognition.” *BioSystems* 235 (2024): 105107.
- Research on collective behavior, surprise minimization, and active-inference models of biological systems.

### Epistemic status
Active inference is an influential formal framework, not a universally accepted final theory of cognition or life. Extensions to morphogenesis are active research and should not be read as proof that cells possess humanlike awareness.

## Essay 3 — music is medicine for the invisible body

### Primary texts
- Marsilio Ficino, *Three Books on Life* (*De vita libri tres*), especially Book III.
- Ficino’s writings and letters on music, hymn, melancholy, health, astrology, and *spiritus*.

### Scholarship
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Marsilio Ficino.”
- Scholarship on *musica humana*, Ficino’s spiritus theory, natural magic, prayer, song, and Renaissance medicine.
- Scholarly work emphasizing sound, music, hymn, and invocation as major vehicles of spiritual influence in Ficino’s system.

### Epistemic status
Historical analysis of Renaissance natural philosophy and medicine. Ficino’s astrology, humoral theory, and spiritus are not established biomedical mechanisms.

## Essay 4 — you are only one of your possible selves

### Esoteric/channeled source texts
- Jane Roberts and Robert F. Butts, *The Early Sessions*.
- Jane Roberts, *The Unknown Reality* and related Seth books.
- Seth Center glossary and publication descriptions on probable realities, probable selves, psychological time, and the “point of power.”

### Epistemic status
The Seth Material is treated as a historically situated channeled corpus and psychological-philosophical text. The independent existence of “Seth,” probable universes, and metaphysical mechanics are not presented as verified facts or as consequences of quantum physics.

## Essay 5 — the future can move you without existing yet

### Scientific sources
- Active-inference literature on generative models, prior preferences, expected free energy, action selection, and morphogenesis.
- Pio-Lopez et al. (2022), “Active inference, morphogenesis, and computational psychiatry.”
- Levin and collaborators on target morphology, biological goal spaces, and multiscale competency.

### Philosophical sources
- Aristotle on final causation.
- Plotinus and Proclus on procession, return, participation, and the Good.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entries on Plotinus, Proclus, causation, and Neoplatonism.

### Epistemic status
The bridge is structural. Active-inference preferences are not identical to Aristotelian or Neoplatonic final causes, and neither body of work is evidence for the other.
