## Essay 1 — the goddess is not the alphabet but the path through it

### Primary texts and scholarship
- *Mālinīvijayottara Tantra*, Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies edition.
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrāloka* and *Parātriṃśikāvivaraṇa*, on Mālinī, Mātṛkā, mantra, phonemic manifestation, and initiation.
- Jaideva Singh, *Parā-trīśikā-vivaraṇa: The Secret of Tantric Mysticism*.
- André Padoux, studies of mantra, phonemes, and Tantric language.
- Alexis Sanderson, scholarship on Trika scripture and ritual.

### Current complexity and assembly research
- Siebert, G., Chowdhury, R., Slocombe, L., and Walker, S. I. “Assembly Spaces: Formal Definitions and Fast Methods for Approximating Assembly Indices.” arXiv:2606.15499 (2026).
- Walker, S. I. “Metrology of Complexity and Implications for the Study of the Emergence of Life.” arXiv:2602.18203 (2026).
- Jirasek, M. et al. “Quantifying the Emergence of Selection Prior to Biological Evolution.” arXiv:2512.18752 (2025).
- Sharma, A. et al. “Assembly theory explains and quantifies selection and evolution.” *Nature* 622 (2023): 321–328.

### Epistemic status
The phonemic assembly comparison is structural. Mantras are not molecules, and ritual efficacy is not measured by assembly index.

## Essay 2 — life survives by never becoming fully stable

### Primary texts and scholarship
- Vasugupta/Bhaṭṭa Kallaṭa, *Spandakārikā*.
- Kṣemarāja, *Spandanirṇaya* and related commentarial traditions.
- Mark S. G. Dyczkowski, *The Stanzas on Vibration* and *The Doctrine of Vibration*.
- Jaideva Singh, *Spanda-Kārikās*.

### Complexity and dynamical systems
- Muñoz, M. A. “Colloquium: Criticality and dynamical scaling in living systems.” *Reviews of Modern Physics* 90 (2018); arXiv:1712.04499.
- Mediano, P. A. M. et al. “Integrated information as a common signature of dynamical and information-processing complexity.” arXiv:2106.10211.
- Htet, A. et al. “Allostatic Control of Persistent States in Spiking Neural Networks for perception and computation.” arXiv:2503.16085 (2025).
- Suzuki, R. and Adachi, T. “Theoretical Framework for Temporal Changes in Interactions among Adaptive Components of Living Systems.” arXiv:2504.20418 (2025).
- Roli, A. et al. “Dynamical criticality: overview and open questions.” arXiv:1512.05259.

### Epistemic status
Spanda is a metaphysical and contemplative doctrine, not a measurable oscillation. Metastability and criticality are scientific concepts with system-specific evidence and ongoing debate.

## Essay 3 — the crooked body can reach forms the straight body cannot

### Primary texts and scholarship
- *Kubjikāmatatantra*, critical edition by T. Goudriaan and J. A. Schoterman.
- Mark S. G. Dyczkowski, scholarship on the Kubjikā tradition and the Western Transmission.
- Cambridge/Bulletin of SOAS scholarship on the critical edition and textual history of the *Kubjikāmatatantra*.

### Morphospace, topology, and active matter
- Lucas, M. et al. “Minimal branching and fusion morphogenesis approaches biological multi-objective optimality.” arXiv:2601.03877 (2026).
- Singh, A. et al. “TopoSPAM: Topology grounded Simulation Platform for morphogenesis and biological Active Matter.” arXiv:2509.24905 (2025).
- Paparini, S. et al. “Morphogenesis driven by nematic defects in active biological networks.” arXiv:2606.04256 (2026).
- Leroux, R. et al. “Density and shape govern the dynamical self-organization of active matter on a droplet.” arXiv:2603.23735 (2026).
- Angheluta, L. et al. “Topological defects in polar active matter.” arXiv:2504.03284 (2025).

### Epistemic status
Kubjikā’s crooked form is ritual and theological imagery. It is not evidence that the tantra anticipated topological active matter or developmental morphospace.

## Essay 4 — a collective becomes one when its desires stop cancelling each other

### Primary texts and scholarship
- *Kaulajñānanirṇaya*, attributed within tradition to Matsyendranātha.
- P. C. Bagchi, edition and study of the *Kaulajñānanirṇaya* school.
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam materials on the eightfold chakra system and sixty-four yoginīs.
- Scholarship on Yoginī Kaula traditions, kula, embodied collectivity, and ritual centres.

### Multiscale agency and valence
- Levin, M. “Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere.” arXiv:2201.10346 / *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience* (2022).
- Burini, D. and Knopoff, D. A. “Multiscale Kinetic Structures for Living Systems.” arXiv:2604.19659 (2026).
- Pezzulo, G. and Levin, M. “Bootstrapping Life-Inspired Machine Intelligence.” arXiv:2602.08079 (2026).
- Pattisapu, C. et al. “Free Energy in a Circumplex Model of Emotion.” arXiv:2407.02474 (2024).
- Levin and collaborators on multiscale competency, goal scaling, and collective biological intelligence.

### Epistemic status
Functional valence at cellular or collective scales does not establish humanlike felt emotion. The yoginī network comparison is structural and philosophical.

## Essay 5 — fear is a precision setting before it is a story

### Primary texts and scholarship
- *Netra Tantra*, Kashmir Series edition with Kṣemarāja’s commentary.
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam, “The Netra Tantra.”
- Scholarship on the *Netra Tantra* as a Kashmir Śaiva compendium of protection, healing, ritual danger, Mṛtyuñjaya/Mṛtyujit, and royal or communal safeguarding.

### Valence, interoception, and allostasis
- Balar, P. and Kapila, S. “Integrative Model for Interoception and Exteroception: predictive coding, points of modulation, and testable predictions.” arXiv:2511.13668 (2025).
- Candia-Rivera, D. “Interoceptive machine framework: Toward interoception-inspired regulatory architectures in artificial intelligence.” arXiv:2604.24527 (2026).
- Htet, A. et al. “Allostatic Control of Persistent States in Spiking Neural Networks for perception and computation.” arXiv:2503.16085 (2025).
- Pattisapu, C. et al. “Free Energy in a Circumplex Model of Emotion.” arXiv:2407.02474 (2024).
- Established affective-neuroscience and predictive-processing work on threat precision, interoception, anxiety, and allostasis.

### Epistemic status
Protective ritual can organize attention, expectation, social support, and action. The essays do not claim that neuroscience verifies invisible attackers or that ritual protection replaces clinical care.
