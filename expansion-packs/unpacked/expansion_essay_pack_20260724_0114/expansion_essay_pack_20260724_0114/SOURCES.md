## 1. knowledge binds by making the world small enough to use

### Primary text and scholarship
- *Śiva Sūtras*, especially I.1–2 and II.1–9.
- Kṣemarāja, *Śivasūtravimarśinī*.
- Jaideva Singh, *Śiva Sūtras: The Yoga of Supreme Identity*.
- Mark S. G. Dyczkowski, scholarship on contracted and awakened knowledge in Kashmir Śaivism.
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam, “The Śiva Sūtras.”

### Scientific and formal sources
- Tishby, N., Pereira, F. C., and Bialek, W. “The Information Bottleneck Method.”
- Wang, Y. et al. “Task-Oriented Lossy Compression with Data, Perception, and Classification Constraints.” arXiv:2405.04144 (2024).
- Yu, F. “Memorization-Compression Cycles Improve Generalization.” arXiv:2505.08727 (2025).
- John, R. et al. “Combined Representation and Generation with Diffusive State Predictive Information Bottleneck.” arXiv:2510.09784 (2025).
- Current predictive-processing and precision-weighting literature.

### Epistemic status
Information bottleneck research formalizes task-relative compression. The Śaiva claim that contracted knowledge obscures universal consciousness is metaphysical and contemplative.

## 2. you cannot manufacture the state the whole must become

### Primary text and scholarship
- *Akulavīra Tantra*, attributed within its colophon to Macchendrapāda/Matsyendranātha.
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam, “Akulavira Tantra.”
- P. C. Bagchi, *Kaulajñānanirṇaya of the School of Matsyendranātha*.
- Scholarship on Akula, Kula, *sahaja*, and *samarasa* in early Nātha and Yoginī Kaula materials.

### Complexity and emergence
- Hoel, E. “Causal Emergence 2.0: Quantifying emergent complexity.” arXiv:2503.13395 (2025).
- Sule, S., Mehta, A., and Cameron, M. K. “Learning collective variables that preserve transition rates.” arXiv:2506.01222 (2025).
- Yuan, J. et al. “Learning collective variables that respect permutational symmetry.” arXiv:2507.00408 (2025).
- Xu, Y. and Sun, F. “Universal classification of continuous phase transitions with two order parameters.” arXiv:2605.24061 (2026).
- Haken, H., foundational synergetics work on control parameters, order parameters, and collective organization.

### Epistemic status
Order parameters provide a structural analogy for system-level reorganization. They do not validate the Akulavīra’s theological claims or eliminate the need for evidence when evaluating spiritual authority.

## 3. the universe changes scale without changing its grammar

### Primary text and scholarship
- *Siddha Siddhānta Paddhati*, attributed to Gorakṣanātha.
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam, “Siddha Siddhanta Paddhati.”
- Kalyani Mallik, *Siddha Siddhānta Paddhati & Other Works of the Nātha Yogis*.
- Gopinath Kaviraj, introduction to the *Siddha Siddhānta Saṅgraha*.
- Scholarship on Nātha Piṇḍa doctrine and microcosm–macrocosm correspondences.

### Multiscale science
- Hoel, E. “Causal Emergence 2.0.” arXiv:2503.13395 (2025).
- Wang, Z. et al. “Causal Emergence of Consciousness through Learned Multiscale Neural Dynamics in Mice.” arXiv:2509.10891 (2025).
- Gabriel, N. A., Johnson, N. F., and Karniadakis, G. E. “Connecting the geometry and dynamics of many-body complex systems with message passing neural operators.” arXiv:2502.15913 (2025).
- Artime, O. and De Domenico, M. “From the origin of life to pandemics: Emergent phenomena in complex systems.” arXiv:2205.11595.
- Current work on coarse-graining, renormalization, and multiscale biological causation.

### Epistemic status
Multiscale causal descriptions are scientific models. The six Piṇḍas and bodily cosmic geography are Nātha metaphysics and contemplative anatomy, not literal modern physiology.

## 4. a symbol becomes real when several worlds compile to the same form

### Primary text and scholarship
- *Śrī Vidyā Ratna Sūtras*, attributed in tradition to Gauḍapāda.
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam, “Śrī Vidyā Ratna Sūtras.”
- Gopinath Kaviraj and Narayana Shastri Khiste, historical edition and introduction.
- Śrīvidyā scholarship on the claimed identity of vidyā, deity-form, yantra, lineage, and ritual enactment.

### Compositional and genotype–phenotype research
- Agmon, E. “Foundations of a Compositional Systems Biology.” arXiv:2408.00942 (2024).
- Nitash, C. G. and Adami, C. “Information-theoretic characterization of the complete genotype-phenotype map of a complex pre-biotic world.” arXiv:2107.03522.
- Liu, M. et al. “G2PDiffusion: Genotype-to-Phenotype Prediction with Diffusion Models.” arXiv:2502.04684 (2025).
- General literature on compositionality, interfaces, commuting diagrams, redundant coding, and developmental genotype–phenotype maps.

### Epistemic status
Compositional systems biology supports the importance of translation across interfaces. It does not demonstrate that mantra, deity, and yantra are ontologically identical.

## 5. the same state can feel like birth from one direction and death from the other

### Primary text corpus
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam, “The Fifteen Nityās.”
- Shiva Shakti Mandalam, “The Kālī Nityās.”
- *Tantrarāja Tantra*, *Dakṣiṇāmūrti Saṃhitā*, *Jñānārṇava Tantra*, and related Śrīvidyā materials.
- Kālī traditions associated with the *Śaktisaṅgama Tantra* and related Nityā descriptions.
- Scholarship on waxing and waning lunar cycles in Śrīkula and Kālīkula ritual time.

### Affective and computational research
- Pattisapu, C. et al. “Free Energy in a Circumplex Model of Emotion.” arXiv:2407.02474 (2024).
- Narayana, S. et al. “Mood as a Contextual Cue for Improved Emotion Inference.” arXiv:2402.08413 (2024).
- Ying, L., Michal, A., and Zhang, J. “A Bayesian Drift-Diffusion Model of Schachter-Singer’s Two Factor Theory of Emotion.” arXiv:2406.11086 (2024).
- Established literature on opponent-process theory, affective adaptation, path dependence, and hysteresis.

### Epistemic status
Dynamic models support the claim that valence depends on context, expectation, and trajectory. The Nityā cycles are ritual-cosmological systems, not neuroscientific emotion models.
