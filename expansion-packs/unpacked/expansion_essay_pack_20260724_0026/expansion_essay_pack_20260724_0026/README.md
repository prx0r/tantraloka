# It from Bit from Śiva — Expansion Essay Pack

Five production-ready essays developed from the supplied synthesis and deepened through current arXiv research, primary Buddhist/Śaiva texts, and recognized scholarship.

## Essays

1. **a bit begins when a difference can wound something**  
   Semantic information, viability, normativity, multiscale agency, and the five *kañcukas*.  
   Visual motifs: identical light striking stone and bacterium; counterfactual scrambling; a hierarchy of nested centres of concern.

2. **matter remembers by becoming easier to change again**  
   Material memory, hysteresis, trained solids, self-reference through time, and *saṃskāra* as modified readiness.  
   Visual motifs: bent paperclip, trained amorphous material, bodily trace rewriting future response.

3. **you are what remains recognisable after the parts have changed**  
   Ñāṇavīra’s invariance through transformation, persistent nonequilibrium patterns, homeostasis, and layered personal identity.  
   Visual motifs: flame losing fuel while preserving form; nested invariants; identity revealed by perturbation and repair.

4. **self and world hold each other upright**  
   Ñāṇananda’s reeds and vortex, enactive cognition, active inference, environment-centred modelling, and grasping as self-evidencing.  
   Visual motifs: two reed bundles; self/world predictions forming a reciprocal whirlpool; action revealing the world it expects.

5. **śakti draws the line that turns physics into information**  
   Wheeler’s “it from bit,” semantic information, biological interpretability, *prakāśa–vimarśa–svātantrya*, and the limits of scientific derivation.  
   Visual motifs: physical difference becoming signal, value, self-model, and recognition; Śakti as line-drawing rather than supernatural intervention.

## Production notes

- Each essay is between 1,400 and 2,200 words.
- Scientific claims are separated into established frameworks, current proposals, and metaphysical interpretation.
- Functional normativity is not equated with phenomenal consciousness.
- Material memory is not treated as subjective recollection.
- Buddhist dependent arising and Śaiva universal consciousness are kept distinct rather than forced into agreement.
- “It from Bit from Śiva” is presented as a philosophical architecture, not a scientific derivation from *Tantrāloka*.
