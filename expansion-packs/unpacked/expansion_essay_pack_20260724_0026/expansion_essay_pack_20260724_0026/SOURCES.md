# Sources

## Supplied synthesis

- User-supplied essay beginning “It from bit from Śiva?” used as the conceptual starting map. Its claims were expanded and tested rather than adopted as scientific conclusions.

## 1. a bit begins when a difference can wound something

### Scientific research
- Kolchinsky, A. and Wolpert, D. H. “Semantic information, autonomous agency, and nonequilibrium statistical physics.” arXiv:1806.08053; *Interface Focus* 8 (2018).
- Wilson, P. et al. “Active Inference: A method for Phenotyping Agency in AI systems?” arXiv:2604.23278 (2026).
- Pio-Lopez, L., Bischof, J., LaPalme, J. V., and Levin, M. “The scaling of goals via homeostasis: an evolutionary simulation, experiment and analysis.” arXiv:2211.08522.
- Levin, M. “Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere.” arXiv:2201.10346; *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience* 16 (2022).

### Śaiva sources
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrāloka* and *Tantrasāra* on Māyā and the five *kañcukas*.
- Kṣemarāja, *Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam*.
- Mark S. G. Dyczkowski and Raffaele Torella on Kashmir Śaiva metaphysics.

### Epistemic note
Semantic information is defined functionally through viability-affecting correlations. This does not establish phenomenal consciousness in every norm-governed system.

## 2. matter remembers by becoming easier to change again

### Scientific research
- Keim, N. C., Paulsen, J. D., Zeravcic, Z., Sastry, S., and Nagel, S. R. “Memory formation in matter.” arXiv:1810.08587; *Reviews of Modern Physics* 91 (2019).
- Paulsen, J. D. and Keim, N. C. “Mechanical memories in solids, from disorder to design.” arXiv:2405.08158.
- Agoritsas, E. and Morse, P. K. “Memory formation in dense persistent active matter.” arXiv:2403.11701.
- Abramsky, S. et al. “Open Questions about Time and Self-reference in Living Systems.” arXiv:2508.11423.
- Tzelios, P. M. and Bishop, K. J. M. “Patterns that persist: Heritable information in stochastic dynamics.” arXiv:2101.01469.

### Buddhist and Śaiva sources
- Ñāṇavīra Thera, *Fundamental Structure*, Static and Dynamic Aspects.
- Primary and scholarly discussions of Sanskrit *saṃskāra* and Pali *saṅkhāra*.
- Utpaladeva and Abhinavagupta on memory, traces, and first-person recognition.

### Epistemic note
Material memory means readable, history-dependent change. It does not imply subjective recollection.

## 3. you are what remains recognisable after the parts have changed

### Scientific research
- Tzelios and Bishop, “Patterns that persist,” arXiv:2101.01469.
- Susman, L. et al. “Individuality and slow dynamics in bacterial growth homeostasis.” arXiv:1805.05058.
- Pio-Lopez et al., “The scaling of goals via homeostasis,” arXiv:2211.08522.
- Roth, Y. “Identity and Self as Physical Signatures of Life in Dictyostelium and Multicellular Systems.” arXiv:2607.00923 (recent preprint; interpret cautiously).

### Philosophical sources
- Ñāṇavīra Thera, *Fundamental Structure*, especially the Dynamic Aspect.
- Utpaladeva’s *Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā* and *Vṛtti* on recognition and continuity.
- Raffaele Torella, *Utpaladeva: Philosopher of Recognition*.

### Epistemic note
Persistent organization, biological individuality, autobiographical identity, and phenomenal continuity are distinct layers and should not be collapsed.

## 4. self and world hold each other upright

### Scientific and cognitive research
- Rafiee, B. and Sutton, R. “Toward Enactive Artificial Intelligence.” arXiv:2605.24238.
- Esaki, K. et al. “Environment-Centric Active Inference.” arXiv:2408.12777.
- Baltieri, M. and Buckley, C. L. “Nonmodular architectures of cognitive systems based on active inference.” arXiv:1903.09542.
- van de Laar, T. et al. “Active Inference and Epistemic Value in Graphical Models.” arXiv:2109.00541.

### Buddhist sources
- Bhikkhu Ñāṇananda, *Nibbāna: The Mind Stilled*, especially the reciprocal relation of *viññāṇa* and *nāma-rūpa* and the vortex imagery.
- Early Buddhist dependent-arising discourses concerning consciousness and name-and-form.

### Epistemic note
Enactive and active-inference models are compared structurally with dependent arising. They do not scientifically verify Buddhist liberation claims.

## 5. śakti draws the line that turns physics into information

- Fields, C. “Building the Observer into the System.” arXiv:1411.3405.

### Śaiva sources

- Abhinavagupta and Utpaladeva on *prakāśa*, *vimarśa*, *svātantrya*, and *ābhāsa*.
- Raffaele Torella, Mark S. G. Dyczkowski, and Isabelle Ratié on Recognition philosophy.

### Buddhist comparative sources
- Bhikkhu Ñāṇananda on dependent arising and appropriation.
- Madhyamaka sources on the refusal to establish an intrinsic metaphysical ground.

### Epistemic note
“It from Bit from Śiva” is a philosophical ordering of explanatory dependence, not a scientific cosmology or evidence that *Tantrāloka* predicted information theory.
