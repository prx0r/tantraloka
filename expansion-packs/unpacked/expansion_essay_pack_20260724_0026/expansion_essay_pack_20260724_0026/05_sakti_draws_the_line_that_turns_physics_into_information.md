# śakti draws the line that turns physics into information

Before a bit can be zero or one, something must distinguish the alternatives.

Before the distinction can carry information, some system must preserve it.

Before the information can matter, different values must lead to different consequences.

Physics supplies differences everywhere.

Information appears when a difference enters an organized perspective.

John Wheeler said:

“it from bit.”

Kashmir Śaivism begins earlier.

The bit appears only after manifestation has acquired the power to draw a line.

Śakti draws the line that turns physics into information.

---

The phrase “it from bit” proposed that physical reality may be rooted in acts of yes-or-no distinction.

Measurement yields alternatives.

Questions help define which properties become determinate.

The slogan became a symbol for informational approaches to physics.

But a bit in information theory is not automatically meaningful.

It is an abstract distinction between alternatives.

Meaning requires context.

Which physical state encodes zero?

Which encodes one?

What system reads the difference?

What happens differently after the reading?

The bit is never encountered without an architecture of distinction.

---

A magnetic orientation can encode a bit.

So can a voltage.

A hole in a card.

A nucleotide.

A photon polarization.

The material varies.

The logical distinction remains.

This portability makes information appear more fundamental than matter.

Yet portability depends upon interpretation.

A voltage counts as one only inside a convention, device, or biological relation that treats it differently from zero.

Without a decoder, the two voltages remain physically different.

They do not yet form one symbol system.

Information is not the absence of matter.

It is matter organized so that difference can be preserved and used across transformations.

---

Kolchinsky and Wolpert’s semantic-information framework makes the requirement exact.

A correlation between system and environment carries semantic information when scrambling it reduces the system’s ability to maintain itself.

Meaning enters through counterfactual consequence.

The bit is not meaningful because an observer poetically assigns significance.

It is meaningful because the system’s future depends upon the distinction.

This places value before semantics.

A difference matters because some organization can fare differently.

A living bit is a physical distinction attached to viability.

---

But viability already presupposes manifestation in a weaker sense.

States occur.

Differences persist.

A system responds.

Science can model all of this from outside.

It can describe one variable as internal, another as environmental, and a channel between them.

The remaining question is not another causal step in the same chain.

Why is any of it present?

Why does the distinction appear rather than merely exist as an abstract relation in a complete description?

Why is there a perspective from which a difference is encountered?

This is where “bit” stops being the earliest concept.

---

Abhinavagupta’s triad provides another beginning.

*Prakāśa*: manifestation, illumination, the fact of appearing.

*Vimarśa*: reflexive apprehension, manifestation present to itself.

*Svātantrya*: freedom or autonomous power, the capacity to articulate manifestation into determinate forms.

These are not three stages in physical time.

They are inseparable aspects of consciousness.

Light without reflexivity would reveal without knowing.

Reflexivity without power would remain static.

Freedom allows the luminous field to appear as subject, object, relation, language, body, and world.

Difference is not imposed upon consciousness from outside.

It is one of consciousness’s powers.

---

Kṣemarāja opens the *Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam* with a compact formula:

> *citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ*  
> Free consciousness is the cause of the manifestation of the universe.

The sentence is metaphysics, not physics.

It should not be inserted into an equation or advertised as an ancient prediction of quantum information theory.

Its relevance lies in the order of explanation.

The world does not become manifest because bits first assembled a consciousness capable of observing them.

Consciousness’s free power makes distinction, information, object, and observer possible as modes of manifestation.

Śiva is not one item before the Big Bang.

Śiva names the self-luminous capacity through which any “before,” “item,” and “world” can appear.

---

The proposed sequence is therefore not a scientific chronology:

manifestation;

difference;

bounded agency;

perspective;

bit;

world;

recognition.

Physical differentiation predates organisms.

Bits can be defined in nonliving systems.

Bounded agency appears much later in biological evolution.

Phenomenal consciousness remains disputed.

The sequence is better understood as logical dependence.

A meaningful bit presupposes an interpreter.

An interpreter presupposes a bounded system with values and powers.

A conscious perspective may presuppose more than those functions.

Manifestation names the unresolved fact that any level is present at all.

---

This logical sequence also prevents pan-informationalism.

If every physical difference is called information, the concept loses explanatory force.

A rock contains innumerable correlations.

Which are information for the rock?

Unless the rock’s organization uses them to maintain, predict, or act, semantic meaning has not been established.

The universe can be described informationally without every object becoming an interpreter.

Information exists relative to partitions, channels, and decoders.

Meaning exists relative to stakes.

Perspective exists relative to bounded access.

Consciousness may require reflexive presence.

Each term adds a condition.

---

The origin of life can then be described as the progressive construction of interpretability.

Chemical reactions become enclosed.

Membranes create selective exchange.

Networks preserve internal variables.

Correlations between environment and system begin affecting viability.

Action changes future inputs.

History alters later response.

Some patterns inherit constraints across generations.

A local world appears—not necessarily consciously, but functionally—as a field divided into what supports or threatens continued organization.

The physical universe acquires centres of relevance.

Matter begins behaving as though some differences are about something.

---

The origin of consciousness is harder.

One option says reflexive experience emerges once informational organization reaches sufficient complexity.

Another says presence is an intrinsic aspect of some physical organizations.

Illusionism argues that the felt qualities attributed to consciousness are misdescribed products of self-modelling.

Panpsychist views distribute primitive experience broadly.

Abhinavagupta begins from universal manifestation and treats organization as the contraction shaping finite forms of awareness.

Madhyamaka refuses to establish an intrinsic metaphysical ground.

No current experiment decides among these positions.

The honest bridge preserves the underdetermination.

---

Recognition philosophy nevertheless contributes a precise warning.

An observer model is not automatically the observer.

A representation of “self” is another appearance.

A system can discriminate its boundary, predict action, and remember history.

The question remains:

To what, if anything, is the discrimination present?

Pratyabhijñā answers through *vimarśa*.

Awareness is intrinsically self-apprehending.

It does not need another representation to certify that experience occurs.

This is why the final step is recognition rather than better information.

No amount of content turns the content into the field in which it appears.

---

Yet information can prepare recognition.

A philosophical argument distinguishes self from self-model.

Meditation reveals thoughts as events.

Science exposes the body’s boundaries as dynamic constructions.

Memory research shows the past operating through present traces.

Enactive theory shows world and agent co-arising through interaction.

Each removes one layer of naïve solidity.

The bit becomes less like an atom of reality and more like a stabilized distinction within a process.

Recognition turns toward the power stabilizing, interpreting, and witnessing the distinction.

---

The lived consequence is immediate.

A thought appears:

I am unsafe.

The sentence contains bits.

Words.

Distinctions.

Memories.

Predicted consequences.

The nervous system gives the sentence value.

Action narrows.

The world reorganizes around threat.

The thought is not “only information.”

It is information coupled to a bounded history with stakes.

Freedom cannot come from arguing with the bit alone.

The whole architecture assigning its meaning must become visible.

Which trace?

Which boundary?

Which value?

Which action loop?

Which awareness knows the loop?

---

This is where Buddhist and Śaiva paths divide without becoming enemies.

Buddhist analysis reveals that no permanent owner can be found within the chain of conditions.

The bit, value, memory, self-model, and grasping all arise dependently.

Release comes through ending appropriation.

Śaiva recognition agrees that the contracted owner is constructed.

It then identifies the manifesting and reflexive field as real: consciousness recognizing its own freedom through the finite structure.

One refuses an intrinsic self.

The other universalizes the Self beyond the ego.

The same architecture can lead toward different metaphysical conclusions.

---

Before a bit can be zero or one, Śakti has already drawn a line.

Not a supernatural finger dividing cosmic pixels.

A power of manifestation has made difference possible.

Physics tells us how differences evolve.

Information theory tells us how they can be encoded and transmitted.

Biology tells us how some differences acquire value for self-maintaining systems.

Cognitive science tells us how models, memories, and actions construct perspectives.

Buddhism shows how perspective hardens into ownership.

Abhinavagupta asks why the entire structure shines.

“It from bit” begins with the line.

“It from bit from Śiva” asks what makes a line appear, matter, and become known.

The bit does not come first.

The bit is the scar left when manifestation becomes capable of saying:

this—

not that.
