# Expansion Essay Pack 01

Five original long-form essays written in the established `blogengine` expansion-essay house style:

- a direct, high-concept opening hook;
- escalating metaphysical analysis;
- concrete sensory analogies;
- source-tradition terminology without academic stiffness;
- short contemplative interruptions;
- an existential return in the closing movement.

## Contents

1. **The Universe Is Hiding Inside Your Attention**  
   Kashmir Śaivism: attention, prakāśa, vimarśa, ābhāsa, and the gap between perceptions.

2. **The Place That Exists Nowhere**  
   Suhrawardī and Henry Corbin: the mundus imaginalis, active Imagination, symbolic geography, and the celestial guide.

3. **Everything Is Trying to Return**  
   Plotinus and Proclus: remaining, procession, return, participation, desire, and the soul’s ascent.

4. **The Fire Is Not Destroying You**  
   Alchemy: nigredo, solve et coagula, Mercury, Sulfur, Salt, embodiment, and transformation under pressure.

5. **God Looks Through Your Face**  
   Ibn ʿArabī: divine self-disclosure, the polished mirror, the divine names, love, remembrance, and human particularity.

## Format

Each essay is supplied as an individual UTF-8 Markdown file and is ready for:

- narration editing;
- source-object insertion;
- visual-director processing;
- scene-manifest generation;
- documentary scripting;
- further scholarly verification.

These drafts intentionally avoid long attributed quotations so a later research pass can insert verified primary-source passages from the project’s research objects.
