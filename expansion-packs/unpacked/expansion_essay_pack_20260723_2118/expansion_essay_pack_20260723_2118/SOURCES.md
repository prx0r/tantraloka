# Sources and epistemic notes

This pack separates primary philosophical texts, scholarly interpretation, current scientific research, and channeled/revelatory source corpora. The essays are interpretive documentary prose, not substitutes for critical editions or scientific reviews.

## 1. The world arrives before you name it

### Primary texts
- *Śiva Sūtras*, especially 1.1 (`caitanyam ātmā`), 1.2 (`jñānaṃ bandhaḥ`), and 1.5 (`udyamo bhairavaḥ`).
- Utpaladeva, *Īśvarapratyabhijñā Kārikā*.
- Abhinavagupta, *Īśvarapratyabhijñāvimarśinī* and *Tantrāloka*.
- *Vijñānabhairava Tantra*.

### Scholarship
- Mark S. G. Dyczkowski, studies of Spanda and Kashmir Śaivism.
- Raffaele Torella, translation and study of Utpaladeva.
- Alexis Sanderson and secondary scholarship on nondual Śaivism.

### Epistemic status
Historical-philosophical interpretation. Sanskrit aphorisms are established textual formulations; broader metaphysical claims belong to the tradition.

## 2. A bird may see the Earth’s magnetic field

### Current scientific research
- Hore, P. J. and Mouritsen, H. “The Radical-Pair Mechanism of Magnetoreception.” *Annual Review of Biophysics*.
- Xu, J. et al. “Magnetic sensitivity of cryptochrome 4 from a migratory songbird.” *Nature* 594 (2021).
- Denton, M. C. J. et al. “Magnetosensitivity of tightly bound radical pairs in cryptochrome is enabled by the quantum Zeno effect.” *Nature Communications* 15 (2024).
- Cao, J. et al. “Quantum biology revisited.” *Science Advances* (2020).
- Kim, Y. et al. “What is quantum biology?” *PNAS* (2026).
- Reviews of quantum effects in enzymes, photosynthesis, olfaction, and magnetodetection.

### Epistemic status
Magnetic sensitivity of cryptochrome and behavioral magnetoreception are supported research areas. The exact in-vivo receptor, radical pair, signaling pathway, and phenomenal character of the avian compass remain unresolved. The essay avoids treating speculative models as settled fact.

## 3. Matter remembers the gods

### Primary texts
- Iamblichus, *Reply to Porphyry* / *De Mysteriis*.
- Proclus, fragments of *On the Hieratic Art* and *Platonic Theology*.
- Plotinus, *Enneads*.
- Marsilio Ficino, *Three Books on Life* and *Platonic Theology*.

### Scholarship
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entries on Iamblichus, Proclus, Plotinus, and Ficino.
- Modern scholarship on theurgy, cosmic sympathy, ritual, and late antique Platonism.

### Epistemic status
Historical and philosophical analysis. Claims about divine participation describe Neoplatonic metaphysics, not established physical mechanisms.

## 4. The universe speaks in borrowed voices

### Channeled/revelatory source corpora
- Jane Roberts, the Seth books and session material.
- L/L Research, *The Law of One* / Ra Contact transcripts and Relistening Project.
- Cassiopaea Experiment session transcripts associated with Laura Knight-Jadczyk.
- Comparative background: ancient oracle, inspiration, daimonic, and Orphic traditions.

### Method
The essay treats these as claimed communications and cultural-philosophical texts. It does not verify the existence or identity of the claimed speakers. Empirical claims within the corpora require independent evidence.

### Epistemic status
Comparative esotericism and psychology of authorship. No channeled cosmological claim is presented as scientifically established.

## 5. Your cells may be solving a problem called you

### Current scientific research
- Levin Lab, Tufts University: research programs on bioelectricity, morphogenesis, basal cognition, and diverse intelligence.
- Durant, F. et al. “Long-Term, Stochastic Editing of Regenerative Anatomy via Targeting Endogenous Bioelectric Gradients.” *Biophysical Journal* (2017).
- Pezzulo, G. and Levin, M. work on goal-directedness and morphogenesis.
- Levin, M. “Bioelectric networks: the cognitive glue enabling evolutionary scaling from physiology to mind.” (2023).
- Work on early bioelectric signals in planarian anterior/posterior polarity and bistable somatic pattern memories.

### Philosophical sources
- Kashmir Śaiva accounts of *saṃkoca*, *kalā*, *vidyā*, *svātantrya*, tattvas, and recognition.

### Epistemic status
The planarian and bioelectric findings are empirical. “Memory,” “preference,” “goal,” and “intelligence” can be used functionally at nonhuman scales but remain conceptually debated. The comparison with Kashmir Śaivism is explicitly structural and does not claim scientific confirmation of the metaphysics.
