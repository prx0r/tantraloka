# SOURCES

## Essay 1 — desire is not proof that you are incomplete

### Primary texts and scholarship
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrāloka*, *Tantrasāra*, and Pratyabhijñā commentarial traditions on *icchā-śakti*, *svātantrya*, manifestation, and contraction.
- *Vijñānabhairava Tantra*, contemplations using desire, fulfillment, pleasure, fear, and perceptual thresholds.
- Paul Eduardo Muller-Ortega, *The Triadic Heart of Śiva*.
- Gavin Flood, “Techniques of Body and Desire in Kashmir Śaivism.”
- Raffaele Torella and Mark S. G. Dyczkowski on nondual Śaiva metaphysics.

### Epistemic status
Historical-philosophical and contemplative. No claim is made that every human desire is morally good or metaphysically authoritative.

## Essay 2 — a tumor may be a cell that forgot the body

### Scientific research
- Chernet, B. and Levin, M. “Endogenous Voltage Potentials and the Microenvironment: Bioelectric Signals that Reveal, Induce and Normalize Cancer.” *Journal of Clinical & Experimental Oncology* (2013).
- Lobikin, M. et al. “Resting Potential, Oncogene-induced Tumorigenesis, and Metastasis: The Bioelectric Basis of Cancer in vivo.” *Physical Biology* 9 (2012).
- Chernet, B. and Levin, M. “Transmembrane voltage potential is an essential cellular parameter for the detection and control of tumor development.” *Disease Models & Mechanisms* 6 (2013).
- Payne, S. L., Levin, M., and Oudin, M. J. “Bioelectric Control of Metastasis in Solid Tumors.” *Bioelectricity* 1 (2019).
- Levin Lab, Tufts University, cancer and spatial-information research pages.
- Reviews on bioelectric dysregulation, ion channels, tumor microenvironment, and metastasis.

### Epistemic status
The foundational normalization and long-range bioelectric experiments discussed are largely from amphibian and other preclinical models. They do not establish a human cancer therapy. Cancer is heterogeneous and bioelectricity is one interacting control layer among genetics, epigenetics, immunity, metabolism, tissue context, and evolution.

## Essay 3 — part of you never entered the body

### Primary texts
- Plotinus, *Enneads*, especially IV.3, IV.8, V.1, and VI.4.
- Plotinus, “On the Three Primary Hypostases” and related treatises on soul, Intellect, and descent.

### Scholarship
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Plotinus.”
- Lloyd P. Gerson et al., translation and study of *Plotinus: The Enneads*.
- Paul Kalligas, commentary on the *Enneads*.
- Scholarship on Plotinus’s doctrine of the undescended soul and later criticism by Iamblichus and Proclus.

### Epistemic status
Historical metaphysics and contemplative interpretation. The undescended soul is not presented as an empirically verified entity or a substitute for neuroscience.

## Essay 4 — the warning can become the trap

### Esoteric/channeled source corpus
- Cassiopaea.org, official presentation of the Cassiopaean Experiment and its claimed origin.
- Cassiopaean session transcripts, FAQs, and methodological claims concerning “critical channeling,” research, deception, and disinformation.
- Comparative study of channeling, automatic writing, revelation, and self-sealing belief systems.

### Epistemic status
The existence of the sessions, texts, authors, websites, and communities is historically verifiable. The claimed source, superluminal communication, future-human identity, densities, and conspiracy claims are not presented as established facts. The essay studies the epistemic structure and psychological effects of the corpus.

## Essay 5 — a body may remember an idea

### Scientific research
- Levin, M., Pezzulo, G., and Finkelstein, J. M. “Endogenous Bioelectric Signaling Networks: Exploiting Voltage Gradients for Control of Growth and Form.” *Annual Review of Biomedical Engineering* 19 (2017).
- Levin Lab research on planarian target morphology, regenerative polarity, bioelectric pattern memory, morphospace, and anatomical reprogramming.
- Research on developmental regulation, regeneration, gene networks, bioelectricity, biomechanics, and target morphology.

### Philosophical sources
- Plato on Forms and recollection.
- Plotinus on Intellect, Forms, Soul, and the sensible cosmos.
- Proclus on participation, causality, and form.

### Epistemic status
The comparison is structural. Morphospace and target morphology are scientific modeling concepts; Platonic Forms are metaphysical principles. Neither body of work is evidence for the other.
