# Sources

## 1. the self appears wherever two moments belong to one life

### Primary texts
- Utpaladeva, *Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā* and the author’s *Vṛtti*, especially the section on the power of memory.
- Abhinavagupta, *Īśvarapratyabhijñāvimarśinī* and surviving materials related to Utpaladeva’s *Vivṛti*.

### Scholarship
- Raffaele Torella, *Utpaladeva: Philosopher of Recognition* (2016), especially “What is Memory?”
- Raffaele Torella, critical edition and translation of Utpaladeva’s *Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā* and *Vṛtti*.
- David Allport, “Utpaladeva’s doctrine of recognition,” University of Oxford DPhil thesis.
- Yōhei Kawajiri, studies of self-awareness, memory, and appearance in Pratyabhijñā.
- Isabelle Ratié, “Utpaladeva and Abhinavagupta on the Freedom of Consciousness.”

### Epistemic note
The permanent/self-luminous subject is a philosophical conclusion of Pratyabhijñā, not an empirical result established by neuroscience.

## 2. a markov blanket is not the skin of the self

### Foundational and review literature
- Kirchhoff, M. et al. “The Markov blankets of life: autonomy, active inference and the free energy principle.” *Journal of the Royal Society Interface* 15 (2018): 20170792.
- Parr, T., Pezzulo, G., and Friston, K. J. “Active inference on discrete state-spaces: A synthesis.” *Journal of Mathematical Psychology* 99 (2020): 102447.
- Friston and collaborators on Markov blankets, non-equilibrium steady states, and active inference.

### Critical literature
- Bruineberg, J. et al. “The Emperor’s New Markov Blankets.” *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*.
- Raja, V. et al. “The Markov blanket trick: On the scope of the free energy principle and active inference.” *Physics of Life Reviews* 39 (2021).
- Aguilera, M. et al. “Knitting a Markov blanket is hard when you are out-of-equilibrium.” arXiv:2207.12914.
- Beck, J. and Ramstead, M. J. D. “Dynamic Markov Blanket Detection for Macroscopic Physics Discovery.” arXiv:2502.21217.
- Possati, L. M. “Markov Blanket Density and Free Energy Minimization.” arXiv:2506.05794.

### Epistemic note
The existence of a Markov blanket in a chosen probabilistic model does not by itself establish a unique physical, biological, mental, or conscious boundary.

## 3. awareness is not inside the system that predicts the world

### Active inference and consciousness
- Whyte, C. J. et al. “On the Minimal Theory of Consciousness Implicit in Active Inference.” arXiv:2410.06633.
- Kirchhoff et al. (2018), Markov blankets and autonomous organization.
- Friston, K. et al., current work on hierarchical and temporally deep active inference.

### Pratyabhijñā
- Utpaladeva and Abhinavagupta on *prakāśa*, *vimarśa*, *ābhāsa*, recognition, and finite subjectivity.
- Yōhei Kawajiri, “Pratibhā in the Pratyabhijñā School.”
- Raffaele Torella and Isabelle Ratié on self-luminosity and the freedom of consciousness.

### Epistemic note
The essay compares a scientific-functional theory of mediated access with a metaphysical theory of self-luminous awareness. Neither is evidence for the other.

## 4. a mutation may begin when a proton changes the alphabet

### Primary scientific research
- Slocombe, L., Sacchi, M., and Al-Khalili, J. “An open quantum systems approach to proton tunnelling in DNA.” *Communications Physics* 5 (2022): 109.
- Slocombe, L. et al. “Proton transfer during DNA strand separation as a source of mutagenic guanine-cytosine tautomers.” *Communications Chemistry* 5 (2022): 144.
- “Multiscale simulations reveal the role of PcrA helicase in protecting against spontaneous point mutations in DNA.” *Scientific Reports* 13 (2023).
- Abma, G. L. et al. “Preparation of tautomer-pure molecular beams by electrostatic deflection.” arXiv:2403.16617 / related experimental publication.

### Speculative or developing models
- Ghasemi, F. and Tirandaz, A. “Environment assisted quantum model for studying RNA-DNA-error correlation created due to base tautomery.” *Scientific Reports* 13 (2023): 10788.
- Hossieni, H. “Mutation in DNA: A quantum mechanical non-adiabatic model.” arXiv:2512.23094.

### Epistemic note
Rare tautomers and proton-transfer pathways are real subjects of quantum chemistry. Their exact contribution to spontaneous mutation rates in vivo remains unsettled, and adaptive-mutation interpretations are speculative.

## 5. a leaf uses noise to keep light from getting lost

### Primary and major research
- Collini, E. et al. and related foundational 2D-spectroscopy studies of photosynthetic coherence.
- Romero, E. et al. “Quantum coherence in photosynthesis for efficient solar-energy conversion.” *Nature Physics* 10 (2014): 676–682.
- Duan, H.-G. et al. “Nature does not rely on long-lived electronic quantum coherence for photosynthetic energy transfer.” *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* 114 (2017).
- Zhu, R. et al. “Quantum phase synchronization via exciton-vibrational energy dissipation sustains long-lived coherence in photosynthetic antennas.” *Nature Communications* 15 (2024): 3171.
- Johnson, M. P. “Structure, regulation and assembly of the photosynthetic electron transport chain.” *Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology* 26 (2025): 667–690.

### Reviews
- Cao, J. et al. “Quantum biology revisited.” *Science Advances* / open review literature on photosynthetic coherence and dissipation.
- Scholes, G. D. et al. “Photosynthetic light harvesting: excitons and coherence.” *Journal of the Royal Society Interface* 11 (2014).

### Epistemic note
Short-lived electronic coherence and quantum excitonic structure are established. The functional role of longer-lived coherence is system- and mechanism-dependent. The essay rejects the oversimplified claim that photosynthetic energy performs a long coherent search through all possible pathways.
