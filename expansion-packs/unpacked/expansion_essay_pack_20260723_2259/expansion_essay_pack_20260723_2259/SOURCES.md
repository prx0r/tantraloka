# Sources

## 1. grace breaks the law that brought you here

### Primary and traditional sources
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrāloka*, Chapter 13, on *śaktipāta*, divine grace, karma, impurity, initiation, and Śiva’s freedom.
- Jayaratha’s commentary on *Tantrāloka* Chapter 13.
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrasāra*, related discussions of initiation and grace.

### Scholarship and research resources
- Shaman Hatley / academic contextual work on initiation and Śaiva traditions.
- Anuttara Trika Kula, *Tantrāloka* Chapter 13 course overview based on Mark S. G. Dyczkowski’s translation and teaching materials.
- University of Tokyo repository: annotated study/translation of *Tantrāloka* Chapter 13.1–52.

### Epistemic status
Historical-philosophical and theological. The doctrine presents liberation as dependent on Śiva’s free grace rather than a mechanically sufficient karmic equilibrium. The essay does not present this metaphysics as scientifically testable.

## 2. the embryo flashes before it has a brain

### Primary scientific research
- Lee, H. C. et al. “Regulation of long-range BMP gradients and embryonic polarity by propagation of local calcium-firing activity.” *Nature Communications* 15 (2024): 1463. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-45772-4.
- Tung, A. et al. “Embryos assist morphogenesis of others through calcium and ATP signaling mechanisms in collective teratogen resistance.” *Nature Communications* 15 (2024): 535. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-023-44522-2.
- Chevalier, N. R. et al. “Calcium wave dynamics in the embryonic mouse gut mesenchyme: impact on smooth muscle differentiation.” *Communications Biology* 7 (2024): 1277. DOI: 10.1038/s42003-024-06976-y.

### Reviews
- Slusarski, D. C. and Pelegri, F. “Calcium signaling in vertebrate embryonic patterning and morphogenesis.” *Developmental Biology* 307 (2007): 1–13.
- Whitaker, M. “Calcium at fertilization and in early development.” *Physiological Reviews* 86 (2006/archival review).
- Nunes, C. O. and Barriga, E. H. “Bioelectricity in Morphogenesis.” *Annual Review of Cell and Developmental Biology* 41 (2025): 187–208.

### Epistemic status
The calcium mechanisms described are empirical in specific embryo and organ-development models. They do not imply subjective awareness before the nervous system or establish universal embryo-to-embryo coordination in humans.

## 3. the soul needs a second body

### Primary philosophical sources
- Plato, *Timaeus*, background to the soul’s vehicle.
- The Chaldean Oracles, fragments and later Neoplatonic interpretations.
- Iamblichus, *De Mysteriis* and related fragments.
- Proclus, *Elements of Theology*, *Commentary on the Timaeus*, and related discussions of the soul’s vehicles.

### Scholarship
- Simon Cox, *The Subtle Body: A Genealogy*, Chapter 1, “Vehicles of the Soul from Plato to Philoponus” (Oxford University Press, 2022).
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Iamblichus.”
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Proclus.”
- Stéphane Toulouse, research on the *ochēma-pneuma* as mediator between soul and body.
- Scholarship on Proclus’s distinction between luminous and pneumatic vehicles.

### Epistemic status
Historical metaphysics and religious philosophy. No claim is made that a luminous or pneumatic soul vehicle has been empirically demonstrated.

## 4. the dead were buried with instructions

### Primary corpus
- The Orphic/Bacchic gold tablets from Hipponion, Petelia, Thurii, Pelinna, Pherai, Crete, Macedonia, and other burial contexts.
- Short formulae concerning Earth and starry Heaven, Mnemosyne, Persephone, initiatory identity, and underworld passwords.

### Scholarship
- Fritz Graf and Sarah Iles Johnston, *Ritual Texts for the Afterlife: Orpheus and the Bacchic Gold Tablets*.
- Radcliffe G. Edmonds III, ed., *The “Orphic” Gold Tablets and Greek Religion: Further Along the Path*.
- Cambridge excerpt, “The ‘Orphic’ gold tablets and Greek religion.”
- Richard Janko, “Forgetfulness in the Golden Tablets of Memory.”
- *Bryn Mawr Classical Review* reviews of *Instructions for the Netherworld* and recent scholarship on memory and performance in the Bacchic tablets.

### Epistemic status
The tablets are archaeological and textual evidence for diverse initiatory and funerary practices. They do not verify the underworld geography or afterlife privileges they describe.

## 5. the whole can act before any part understands it

### Current scientific research
- Lee, H. C. et al. (2024), calcium-firing activity, BMP gradients, and embryonic polarity.
- Tung, A. et al. (2024), embryo-group calcium/ATP signaling and collective teratogen resistance.
- Nunes, C. O. and Barriga, E. H. (2025), bioelectricity in morphogenesis.
- Current work on self-organized tissue mechanics, developmental regulation, bioelectric networks, and multiscale competency.

### Philosophical sources
- Plato on Forms and participation.
- Proclus, *Elements of Theology*, on causes remaining present in effects and higher unities organizing lower multiplicity.
- Contemporary philosophy of biology on downward causation, constraints, emergence, and biological individuality.

### Epistemic status
The essay uses “downward causation” to describe physically realized higher-level constraints that alter local behavior. Platonic and Proclean comparisons are conceptual; no developmental experiment demonstrates transcendent Forms.
