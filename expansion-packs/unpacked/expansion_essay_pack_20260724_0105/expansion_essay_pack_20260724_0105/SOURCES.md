### Tantric and aesthetic sources
- Abhinavagupta, *Abhinavabhāratī*, especially the analysis of rasa, universalization, and aesthetic relish.
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrāloka* and Pratyabhijñā writings on *sāmarasya*, *camatkāra*, *prakāśa*, and *vimarśa*.

- Pollock, Sheldon and other major scholarship on Sanskrit rasa theory.

### Epistemic status
The association between phenomenal symmetry and positive valence remains a QRI hypothesis. Consonance research supports narrower auditory effects but does not validate STV across all experience. Rasa supplies a philosophical counterexample and refinement, not scientific confirmation.

## Essay 2 — the brain has an alphabet before it has a thought

### Connectome harmonics
- Atasoy, Selen, Isaac Donnelly, and Joel Pearson. “Human brain networks function in connectome-specific harmonic waves.” *Nature Communications* 7 (2016): 10340. DOI: 10.1038/ncomms10340.

### Tantric sources
- Abhinavagupta, *Parātriṃśikāvivaraṇa*, on *mātṛkā*, phonemic manifestation, and reflexive consciousness.
- *Mālinīvijayottara Tantra* and Abhinavagupta’s use of its phonemic and mantra system.
- Vasugupta/Bhaṭṭa Kallaṭa, *Spandakārikā*, with Kṣemarāja’s and other classical commentaries.
- Padoux, André. Major studies of Tantric mantra, phonemic power, and language.
- Dyczkowski, Mark S. G. *The Doctrine of Vibration* and *The Stanzas on Vibration*.

### Epistemic status
Connectome harmonics are a mathematically defined basis for representing cortical activity. Their relation to phenomenal primitives is unproven. Mātṛkā and spanda are ritual-metaphysical concepts, not early descriptions of graph Laplacian eigenvectors.

### Tantric sources
- Abhinavagupta, *Tantrāloka*, Chapter 13, on *śaktipāta* and the degrees of grace.

### Epistemic status
Neural annealing is an integrative QRI model, not a validated unitary biological mechanism. Increased entropy or criticality is not equivalent to therapeutic benefit or spiritual realization. Śaktipāta is a theological doctrine and is not established by altered-state neuroscience.

### Tantric sources
- Classical 36-tattva accounts in *Tantrāloka*, *Tantrasāra*, and Pratyabhijñā literature.
- The five *kañcukas*: *vidyā, kalā, rāga, kāla,* and *niyati*.
- Dyczkowski, Mark S. G., scholarship on contraction, māyā, and the structure of finite consciousness.
- Torella, Raffaele and Isabelle Ratié, scholarship on Utpaladeva, Abhinavagupta, finite subjectivity, and reflexive consciousness.

### Epistemic status
Topological segmentation is a hypothesis-and-theory proposal. No electromagnetic pocket has been empirically established as the boundary of a conscious subject. The kañcuka comparison analyses dimensions of finite perspective and does not validate an EM theory.

- Krama traditions concerning cognition as an ordered goddess cycle and the internalization of ritual sequence as *saṃvit-krama*.

### Epistemic status
The pseudo-time arrow is a toy formal model of phenomenal time, not a demonstrated neural mechanism. The IIT and active-inference papers are competing theoretical accounts. Krama is a historical ritual-metaphysical phenomenology rather than scientific evidence.
