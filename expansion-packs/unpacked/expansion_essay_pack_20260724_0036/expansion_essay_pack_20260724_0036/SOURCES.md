## Essay 1 — your body is the diagram your mind keeps redrawing

### Tantric sources
- *Bhāvanopaniṣad* and Bhāskararāya’s ritual interpretation.
- *Yoginīhṛdaya* and Śrīvidyā literature on the identity of body, mantra, yantra, and goddess.

### Scientific research
- Balar, P. and Kapila, S. “Integrative Model for Interoception and Exteroception: predictive coding, points of modulation, and testable predictions.” arXiv:2511.13668 (2025).
- Seth, A. K. and Friston, K. J., foundational work on interoceptive inference and embodied selfhood.
- Le Jeune, F. et al. “Experiencing an elongated limb in virtual reality modifies the tactile distance perception of the corresponding real limb.” arXiv:2401.12601 (2024).

### Epistemic status
Predictive body models and body-schema plasticity are empirical and computational research areas. They do not establish the sacred identity of body and Śrīcakra.

## Essay 2 — touch can move the border of the self

### Tantric sources
- General nyāsa materials on Shivashakti.com.
- Śrīcakra nyāsa from *Nityotsava*.
- Mātṛkā nyāsa and the placement of letters, deities, sacred sites, and cosmic powers on the body.

### Scientific research
- Zhao, Y., Lu, E., and Zeng, Y. “Brain-inspired bodily self-perception model for robot rubber hand illusion.” arXiv:2303.12259.
- Center, E. G. et al. “Body Ownership Affects the Processing of Sensorimotor Contingencies in Virtual Reality.” arXiv:2509.01420.
- Song, K.-W. et al. “Finger Tendon Vibration: Finger Movement Illusions for Immersive Virtual Object Interaction.” arXiv:2602.08201.
- Wu, S., Wahle, J. P., and Mohammad, S. M. “The Language of Interoception.” arXiv:2505.16189.

### Epistemic status
Multisensory integration can alter functional and experienced body ownership. This does not verify ritual claims that divine entities literally enter body parts.

## Essay 3 — a diagram becomes alive when it is given a world

### Tantric sources
- “Yantra and Prāṇapratiṣṭha,” drawing on *Nityotsava*.
- Śrīvidyā ritual descriptions of installing tattvas, elements, senses, organs of action, the internal instrument, and mātṛkās into a yantra.

### Scientific and computational research
- Pezzulo, G. and Levin, M. “Bootstrapping Life-Inspired Machine Intelligence: The Biological Route from Chemistry to Cognition and Creativity.” arXiv:2602.08079 (2026).
- Murray-Smith, R. et al. “Active Inference and Human–Computer Interaction.” arXiv:2412.14741.
- Esaki, K. et al. “Environment-Centric Active Inference.” arXiv:2408.12777.
- Kirchhoff, M. et al. on Markov blankets, autonomy, and active inference.

### Epistemic status
The comparison concerns the architecture of agency: boundaries, sensory states, active states, preferences, and world relations. It does not establish consciousness in consecrated objects.

## Essay 4 — the first point becomes a body by losing symmetry

### Tantric sources
- *Yoginīhṛdaya*, especially its account of bindu, the five Śaktis and four fires, nine triangles, letters, subject/object/means of knowledge, and the identity of mantra, yantra, body, and cosmos.
- Padoux, André, with Roger-Orphe Jeanty, *The Heart of the Yogini*.

### Scientific research
- Aguilar-Hidalgo, D. et al. “Self-organization drives symmetry-breaking, scaling, and critical growth transitions in stem cell-derived organoids.” arXiv:2507.18887.
- Miotto, M. et al. “Rosette formations as symmetry-breaking events: theory and experiment.” arXiv:2505.20543.
- Ishihara, K. and Tanaka, E. “Spontaneous symmetry breaking and pattern formation of organoids.” arXiv:1807.00192.

### Epistemic status
The organoid studies concern physical self-organization, reaction–diffusion, mechanics, scaling, and boundaries. The bindu comparison is structural and aesthetic, not evidence of historical prediction.

## Essay 5 — breath is the clock the brain cannot ignore

### Tantric sources
- The fifteen Nityās and the traditional division of 21,600 daily breaths.
- Jvālāmukhī breath nyāsa.
- *Haṃsa Śodha Upaniṣad*, *Niruttara Tantra*, and related ajapā-mantra traditions.
- *Subhagodaya* and the Śrīvidyā mapping of breath, lunar time, and mantra.

### Scientific research
- Mowla, M. R. et al. “Human forebrain neural synchronization and entrainment to breathing during wakefulness, sleep, and external mechanical ventilation.” *Nature Communications* (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-73828-0.
- Schreiner, T. et al. “Respiration modulates sleep oscillations and memory reactivation in humans.” *Nature Communications* 14 (2023): 8351.
- Nakamura, N. H. et al. “Hippocampal ensemble dynamics and memory performance are modulated by respiration during encoding.” *Nature Communications* 14 (2023): 4391.
- Nakamura, N. H. et al. “Respiratory phase alignment across memory encoding and retrieval improves task efficiency.” *Scientific Reports* 16 (2026): 19152.
- Yahalom, E. et al. “The Evaluation of Breathing 5:5 effect on resilience, stress and balance center measured by Single-Channel EEG.” arXiv:2507.10175.
- Keitel, A. et al. “Brain rhythms in cognition — controversies and future directions.” arXiv:2507.15639.

### Epistemic status
Respiration measurably couples to neural activity and can modulate cognition. The fifteen Nityās and 21,600-breath system are ritual-cosmological mappings, not established neurophysiological divisions.
