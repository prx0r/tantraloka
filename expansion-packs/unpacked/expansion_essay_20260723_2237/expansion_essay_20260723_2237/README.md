# the body can remember a shape it is not wearing

## Domain

Frontier biology: developmental bioelectricity, planarian regeneration, and somatic pattern memory.

## Central thesis

Planarian experiments show that transient changes to bioelectric networks can establish persistent, rewriteable target anatomies that remain hidden in currently normal-looking animals until later regeneration reveals them.

## Suggested visual motifs

- an ordinary one-headed worm containing a ghosted two-headed silhouette;
- voltage gradients replacing a conventional anatomical blueprint;
- a switch moving between head-tail and head-head attractors;
- injury as an examination that reveals a hidden answer;
- a message persisting while the cells carrying it change.

## Production notes

- Keep the visuals biological rather than supernatural.
- Distinguish current anatomy from latent regenerative target state.
- Do not depict the result as a human medical treatment.
- The terms “memory,” “goal,” and “preferred form” are used functionally and do not imply demonstrated subjective experience.
