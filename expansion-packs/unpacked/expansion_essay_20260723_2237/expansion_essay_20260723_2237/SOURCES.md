## Epistemic status

The core phenomenon is empirical in planarian models: transient bioelectric perturbation can produce persistent and rewriteable changes in regenerative anatomy. “Memory,” “goal,” and “preferred anatomy” describe functional properties of the regenerative system. They do not establish humanlike consciousness in cells or clinical applicability to human regeneration.
