The Mantra of Ratiśekhara (consists of) Fire (R), Air (Y), Earth (L) and Water (V), along with the sixth vowel (Ū) and the Point. His faces and limbs (are formed) with the short vowels (A I U E O) and the long ones (Ā I Ū AI AU), respectively. (10cd-11ab) Ratiśckhara is the Bhairava of Parāparā. His mantric form is: RYLVŪṀ. This Mantra, as is generally the case, is presented in the Mālinī code in the MV. There we read:


TANTRĀLOKA
----------------------------------------


Fire is R, Air is Y, Earth is L, Water is V, and the sixth vowel is Ū (thus the mantra of Ratiśekhara is RYLVŪMṀ). The five short (vowels) are A I U F and O. The six long ones are Ā Ī Ū AI AU and AH. He is indicating in this way by saying this, that (the letters) are those of the (corresponding) faces and limbs (of the deity) coming down (serially) in this way.
