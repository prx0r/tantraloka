# The Directions and the Abode of Sacrifice

**Tantrāloka 15.179cd–232ab | Lines 5551–6100**

## Overview

The worship of the abode (gṛha) of sacrifice and its directions (dik) is not a superstition about cardinal points but a profound meditation on the structure of perceptual space. The directions are the fundamental orientation of consciousness — the way awareness deploys itself as objectivity.

## Key Verses

- **179cd–183ab:** Worship outside the abode (bahirarcā).
- **183cd–191ab:** Worship of the door (dvārārcana) — the threshold between inner and outer.
- **194–232ab:** The true nature of the quarters (diksvabhāva) and their guardians.
- **203cd–209ab:** The five Brahmās (Īśāna, Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Vāmadeva, Sadyojāta) distributed in the directions.
- **217cd–221ab:** Direction in relation to the sun and the sacrificer.
- **229cd–232ab:** Śiva is always in the centre.

## Philosophical Meaning

1. **Directions as noetic categories:** East, West, North, South are not arbitrary conventions. They are the way consciousness structures its field. The sun rises in the East because consciousness "rises" as manifestation (sṛṣṭi). The West is where it sets — withdrawal (saṃhāra).

2. **The door as threshold:** The worship of the door (dvārārcana) is the contemplation of the boundary between the unmanifest and the manifest. To cross the door is to enter the sacred space — which is the heart.

3. **Five Brahmās = five faces of Śiva:** The five Brahmās distributed in the directions are the five faces (pañcavaktra) of Sadāśiva, corresponding to the five energies: creation, preservation, destruction, concealment, and grace.

4. **Śiva in the centre:** All directions radiate from and return to the centre, which is Śiva. The centre is the Heart (hṛdaya). No matter where one stands, the centre is always HERE.

## The Sun of Consciousness

The sun is the symbol of self-luminous consciousness (prakāśa). Its rising and setting are the pulse of manifestation — the expansion and contraction of awareness. The directions are its rays.
