# Deep-Dive: Ananta in the Boat on the Cosmic Waters Below the Egg

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 20cd–22ab  
**Catalog Entry:** #294  
**Lines:** 5601–5900+

---

## Topic Overview

The cosmic geography proper begins with what lies below the Egg of Brahmā (brahmāṇḍa). The serpent lord Ananta (also called Śeṣa) floats on a boat on the cosmic waters, supporting the entire universe. This is the foundation of the manifest cosmos.

---

## Key Verses

### Verses 20cd–22ab (lines 5601–5700+)

Ananta, the thousand-headed serpent, floats on the cosmic waters (garbhodaka) below the Egg of Brahmā. His hoods support the shell of the Egg. Below Ananta is the world of Kālāgni — the Fire of Time — which is the lowest limit of the manifest cosmos.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Ananta as Foundation:** The serpent Ananta (the "Endless") is the support of the entire cosmic Egg. His name signifies his role as the infinite ground of finite manifestation.

2. **The Cosmic Boat:** Ananta is described as floating on a boat — symbolizing the support of the universe across the undifferentiated cosmic waters.

3. **Kālāgni Below:** Below Ananta is the Fire of Time (Kālāgni), beyond which is the non-manifest.
