# Deep-Dive: The Power of Time, Breath, Pulse of Consciousness and the Void

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 7, verses 62cd–65ab  
**Catalog Entry:** #288  
**Lines:** 3801–4028

---

## Topic Overview

This short but dense section bridges Chapter 7 (Cakrodaya — the cycles of mantras emerging in the Time of the breath) and Chapter 8 (Deśādhvan — the Path of Space). Abhinava establishes that the six paths (ṣaḍadhvan) — Varṇa, Mantra, Pada (phonemes, mantras, words — subjective) and Bhuvana, Tattva, Kalā (worlds, principles, forces — objective) — are all established within consciousness, mediated by the void (śūnya), intellect (dhī), and vital breaths (marut).

---

## Key Verses

### Verses 62cd–63ab (lines 3801–3820+)
> varṇamantrapadā bhūyo bhuvanādhvatattvakalātmakam
> ṣaḍdhvānam anutiṣṭhanti prāṇa eva vyavasthitaḥ
> *"The six paths — phonemes, mantras, words, worlds, principles and forces — all operate within the vital breath itself."*

### Verses 63cd–65ab
> saṁviddvāreṇa tatsṛṣṭe śūnye dhiyi marutsu ca
> nāḍīcakrānucakreṣu bahirdehe 'dhvasaṁsthitiḥ
> *"Through and mediated by consciousness, the Path is established in that which it has emitted: in the void (śūnya), in the intellect (dhī), in the vital breaths (marut), in the configurations of the channels (nāḍīcakra), in the secondary wheels of the senses, and in the external body."*

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **The Six Paths (Ṣaḍadhvan):** The totality of manifestation is divided into six paths:
   - **Subjective:** Varṇa (phonemes), Mantra (mantras), Pada (words/parts)
   - **Objective:** Bhuvana (worlds), Tattva (principles), Kalā (forces)
   - All six operate within the breath

2. **The Body as Microcosm:** The Path is established in the void (deep sleep state), intellect (waking cognition), vital breaths (life force), channels (nāḍīs), sense-wheels, and the physical body.

3. **Consciousness as Mediator:** The Path is not separate from consciousness — it is emitted by consciousness and established within it.

4. **Bridge to Chapter 8:** This teaching prepares for the extensive cosmic geography of Chapter 8 by establishing that all worlds exist within the body and consciousness.
