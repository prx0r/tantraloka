# Deep-Dive Video Essay: Navātmā Bhairava — The Nine-Souled Mantra

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 11, Chapter 30, Verses 11cd-16ab (~lines 1500-1860)
**Duration:** 10 minutes

## Opening Hook (0:00-0:45)
Navātmā — the Nine-Souled One — is the most important Bhairava mantra in the Trika system. His mantric body encodes the five elements, the five faces of Śiva, and the seven stages of cosmic pervasion. As the Bhairava of Aparā (the lowest of the three goddesses), Navātmā is the sonic form through which the practitioner ascends from the gross to the subtle.

## Part 1: The Mantric Structure of Navātman (0:45-3:00)
- Verse 11cd-12ab (~line 1504): The composition of Navātmā
- **Elements encoded:**
  - Fire (R) — agni
  - Vital Breath (H) — prāṇa
  - Fire (R) — agni
  - Withdrawal (KS) — saṃhāra
  - Time (M) — kāla
  - Indra (L) — the lord of senses
  - Water (V) — ambhas
  - Air (Y) — samīraṇa
  - Sixth vowel (Ū)
  - Point (bindu) and Half-Moon (candrārdha)
- Full mantra: **RHRKṢMLVYŪṀ**
- This is extracted using the Mālinī code in MV 8/22-23
- Abhinava presents the same in the easier Śabdarāśi code
- Navātmā is the sonic body of Bhairava in His aspect as Aparā's consort

## Part 2: The Great Pervasion — Seven Stages of Sound (3:00-5:30)
- Verses 12cd-14ab (~line 1565): From the Traiśirasa Mata
- The "Point, Sound and the rest" refer to the seven stages of sonic manifestation:
  1. **Kṣepa (Casting Forth)** = the Point (bindu) — outward projection
  2. **Ākrānti (Entering Within)** = Sound (nāda) — interiorization
  3. **Cidudbodha (Awakening of Consciousness)** = End of Sound (nādānta)
  4. **Dīpana (Energization)** = Power (śakti)
  5. **Sthāpana (Establishment)** = the Pervasive One (vyāpinī)
  6. **Saṁvitti (Consciousness)** = the Equal One (samanā)
  7. **Tadāpatti (Attainment of That)** = the Transmental (unmanā)
- This "Great Pervasion" (mahāvyāpti) is the body (mūrti) of the deity
- Each stage corresponds to a level of consciousness
- The mantra contains all these stages within its sonic structure

## Part 3: The Faces and Limbs of Navātman (5:30-7:30)
- Verse 15-16ab (~line 1737): Formation of the six limbs and five faces
- **Six Limbs** (formed by omitting last three letters: V, Y, Ū):
  - Heart (Hṛdaya): **RĀṀ**
  - Head (Śiras): **HĪṀ**
  - Topknot (Śikhā): **RŪṀ**
  - Armour (Kavaca): **KṢAIṀ**
  - Eye (Netra): **MAUṀ**
  - Weapon (Astra): **LAḤ**
- **Five Faces** (energized seeds KS, Y, R, V, L with short vowels):
  - Upper (Ūrdhvavaktra): **KṢAṀ**
  - East (Pūrvavaktra): **YIṀ**
  - South (Dakṣiṇavaktra): **RUṀ**
  - North (Uttaravaktra): **VEṀ**
  - West (Paścimavaktra): **LOṀ**
- Each face corresponds to an element: Space, Air, Fire, Water, Earth

## Part 4: The Vitality (Vīrya) of Navātman (7:30-9:30)
- Lakṣmaṇagupta's interpretation of the energized seed-syllables
- Normally the letters KṢ, Y, R, V, L represent Space, Air, Fire, Water, Earth
- They are "energized" by the short vowels (E, O, A, I, U) which are fiery by nature
- Lakṣmaṇagupta adds: they have the letter R added (Fire element) which gives them power
- Scriptural quotation (~line 1804): "The supreme seed-syllable is the twenty-sixth... conjoined with R and having a Point"
- This demonstrates that mantras are not arbitrary — their power derives from the phonetic/phonemic structure of reality itself
- The mantra is a sonic cosmos — to recite it is to recreate the universe within oneself

## Conclusion (9:30-10:00)
Navātmā is the key to the Trika's sonic technology. Through its phonemes, the practitioner embodies the five elements, the five faces of Śiva, and the seven stages of consciousness.
