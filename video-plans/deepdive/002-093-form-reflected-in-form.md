# Topic 93: Only Form is Reflected in a Medium with Form

## Catalog Reference
- **Topic**: 93 of 161
- **Section**: Volume 2 — Chapter 3: Paropāya (The Supreme Means)
- **Verses**: TĀ 3/5-7

---

## 1. Core Teaching

Only form (rūpa) is reflected in a medium with form. A mirror reflects visible forms but not touch, taste, or sound. The purity (nairmalya) of a reflective medium consists of the compact coming together of parts all of the same type. A mirror made of uniform material, free of cracks and dust, reflects form perfectly. Similarly, each sense has its corresponding reflective medium.

## 2. Key Verses

**TĀ 3/5** (lines 5646-5713): *sadṛśaṁ bhāti nayanadarpaṇāmbaravāriṣu | tathā hi nirmale rūpe rūpam evāvabhāsate*
That which manifests reflected in the eye, mirror, sky, or water is similar in nature. In this way, only form manifests in a medium with form that is free of impurities.

**TĀ 3/6** (lines 5734-5744): *pracchannarāgiṇī kāntapratibimbitasundaram | darpaṇaṁ kucakumbhābhyāṁ sprśanty api na tṛpyati*
She who is full of passion yet hides it, although touching with her breasts the mirror beautiful with the reflected image of the beloved, is not satisfied.

**TĀ 3/7** (lines 5787-5795): *na hi sparśo 'sya vimalo rūpam eva tathā yataḥ | nairmalyaṁ cātiniviḍasajātīyaikasaṅgatiḥ*
Because it can only reflect form, it is not capable of reflecting touch. Purity consists of the single, compact coming together of parts all of the same type.

## 3. Jayaratha's Commentary

**On the example of the lover and the mirror** (lines 5766-5781): "When a woman full of passion hides it due to some obstacle and cannot see her lover directly, she considers herself satisfied seeing his reflection in a mirror. But when she tries to experience his touch also, she cannot, because touch is not projected into the mirror.

## 5. Philosophical Context

This teaching is part of Abhinavagupta's systematic exposition of the Supreme Means (Śāmbhavopāya) in Chapter 3 of the Tantrāloka. It integrates the Doctrine of Reflection with the alphabet mysticism of the Tantric traditions, showing how the structures of language embody the structures of consciousness.

## 6. Visual Concepts

- **The mirror of consciousness** with all phenomena reflected within it
- **The fifty letters** arranged as a maṇḍala of energies
- **AHAṀ** as the encompassing cycle of self-awareness
- **The five-fold bindu** as the pentad of divine activities
- **The four levels of speech** from transcendent silence to uttered sound
