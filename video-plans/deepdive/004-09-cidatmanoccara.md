## Central Thesis
Cidātmanoccāra — the Utterance Through Conscious Nature — is the practice of tracing the breath's movement not merely through the physical subtle body but as a movement entirely within consciousness itself. It represents the transition from the Individual Means toward the Empowered Means, where the outer supports of breath and body are recognized as modalities of pure awareness.

## Script Outline

### 1. From Outer to Inner Utterance (0:00–2:00)
- The progression: from bodily breath utterance → to utterance through consciousness — lines 4622–4635
- The yogi's nature is the Absolute (anuttarasvabhāvena) — line 4627
- Present in the activity of Speech itself — lines 4627–4629
- The reflective awareness of consciousness as the Supreme Ego — lines 4630–4632

### 2. The Sun of Knowledge (2:00–4:00)
- "Consisting of the twelve phases of Being" — lines 4622–4635
- The Sun = the power of knowledge that illumines all phenomena
- Manifests, fills, and contemplates phenomenal existence — line 4635
- These twelve phases correspond to the dvādaśānta stations — lines 4622–4623

### 3. The Moon with Sixteen Phases (4:00–6:00)
- The Moon enters the fire of consciousness — lines 4646–4650
- The sixteen phases = the sixteen kalās of the lunar cycle projected into the breath
- The four vivifying energies are emitted into the fire — lines 4648–4651
- The undifferentiated state of emission (viṣarga) is attained — lines 4650–4652

### 4. The Garment of Energies (6:00–8:00)
- "The garland of the ladder of the inner energies" — lines 4662–4670, 4687–4692
- The energies are: consciousness, vital breath, qualities, body — lines 4688–4690
- The plane of the Belly of the Fish = the supreme life-giving energy — lines 4691–4696
- Expanding and contracting, the goddess ever creates and destroys phenomena — lines 4717–4726

### 5. Penetration of the Heart (8:00–9:30)
- The five gates of entry into the Heart — lines 6460–6560
  1. With a Tantric partner (śakti)
  2. Without a partner (kulāveśa) — through imagination
  3. At the tip of all channels (the End of the Twelve)
  4. At bodily extremities (armpits, etc.)
  5. During pervasion (vikāsasamādhi)
- All these gates lead to the same plane of emission — lines 6460–6560

### 6. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- Cidātmanoccāra internalizes the entire breath practice into consciousness
- The outer utterance of the breath becomes the inner utterance of Self-awareness
- The yogi recognizes: "I am he who possesses Śiva's attributes" — lines 6550s

## Visual Suggestions

- The five gates of entry as doorways on the subtle body
- The garland of energies as a ladder ascending through consciousness levels
