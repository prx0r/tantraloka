# Deep-Dive: Identifying with Bhairava by Oneness with the Path

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 15cd–19ab  
**Catalog Entry:** #293  
**Lines:** 5229–5600

---

## Topic Overview

This section extends the Triśirobhairava teaching by declaring that the yogi who contemplates the Path as his own body — as his own conscious nature — becomes Bhairava. The Path is not something to traverse; it is something to recognize as oneself.

---

## Key Verses

### Verses 15cd–19ab (lines 5229–5450+)

Abhinava explains that the thirty-six principles are the body of Bhairava. Earth is His feet, water His legs, fire His navel, air His chest, space His head, and so on up to Śiva as His crown. The yogi who contemplates himself as identical with this cosmic body attains Bhairava's state (bhairavatā).

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **The Path as Body:** The entire hierarchy of worlds and principles is the body of Bhairava. The yogi who knows this recognizes his own body as the cosmos.

2. **Non-Separation:** The Path is not an external reality to be traversed — it is one's own conscious nature.

3. **Quick Attainment:** This recognition is not gradual but immediate — "quickly attains Bhairava's state" (drāg yogī bhairavatāṁ vrajet).
