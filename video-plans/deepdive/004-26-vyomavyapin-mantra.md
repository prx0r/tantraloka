# 004-26: The Vyomavyāpin Mantra

## Video Essay Metadata
- **Duration:** 10:00
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Prerequisite Videos:** 004-25 (Varṇodaya)
- **Core Texts:** Tantrāloka 6.228–230; Viveka commentary; Svacchandatantra

## Key Verses (with line numbers)
- TĀ 6.228–230: The Vyomavyāpin Mantra — pages 431–437, line 507
- Vyomavyāpin = "Pervading Space" — the mantra that pervades all vyomas
- 368 syllables = 360 days of the year + 8 — lines 212–213
- Distributed into 12 spokes (months) with 8 in the nave (hub) — lines 212–213

## Central Thesis
The Vyomavyāpin ("Space-Pervading") mantra is the supreme mantra of Kālopāya — a 368-syllable mantra that embodies the entire year (saṁvatsara). Its syllables correspond to the days of the year, distributed into the 12 months (spokes) and the 8 intercalary days (nave). Reciting this mantra in the breath is to breathe the entire year in a single cycle.

## Script Outline

### 1. Opening: What Is Vyomavyāpin? (0:00–1:30)
- Vyomavyāpin = "that which pervades space (vyoman)" — line 507
- The mantra that pervades all voids (vyomas) of the dvādaśānta
- 368 syllables = the days of the year — lines 212–213
- The supreme mantra of the Temporal Means

### 2. Structure of the Mantra (1:30–3:30)
- 360 syllables for the 360 days of the Indian calendar year
- 8 additional syllables for the intercalary days — lines 212–213
- Distributed in a wheel:
  - 12 spokes = 12 months (30 syllables each = 360)
  - Nave (hub) = 8 syllables for the extra days
- The wheel of the year is the body of Śiva as Time — lines 212–213

### 3. The Mantra in the Breath (3:30–5:30)
- The Vyomavyāpin mantra is recited within a single extended breath
- Each syllable corresponds to a subtle movement of the breath
- The complete recitation = one complete breath = one complete year
- The yogi breathes the year in a single exhalation and inhalation
- The 368 syllables are the phonemic structure of cosmic time

### 4. The Ritual Context (5:30–7:00)
- The Kālayāga (Worship of Time) uses the Vyomavyāpin mantra — lines 212–213
- Śiva is worshipped as the Year (saṁvatsarātmā)
- The maṇḍala of the year is drawn with 12 spokes and a nave
- The mantra is recited while meditating on the wheel
- This is described in the Svāyambhuvasūtrasaṁgraha and Niśvāsaguhya — lines 212–213

### 5. The 368 Syllables and Consciousness (7:00–9:00)
- Each syllable is a form of consciousness (citi)
- The 368 syllables = 368 forms of the goddess
- The mantra is not just uttered — it is the structure of awareness itself
- The "pervasion of space" means that the mantra fills every void of the dvādaśānta
- To recite Vyomavyāpin is to become the year, to become Time itself

### 6. Conclusion (9:00–10:00)
- Vyomavyāpin is the most comprehensive mantra of the Trika system
- It integrates phonemes, breath, time, and space
- The yogi who masters it embodies the entire cosmos
- The final state: beyond the mantra, beyond the year, beyond time
