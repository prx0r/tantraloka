# Combined Deposition of Mātṛkā and Mālinī

**Tantrāloka 15.139cd–145 | Lines 449–450**

## Overview

The combination of the Mātṛkā and Mālinī depositions produces a third, higher state — the union of the outgoing and returning currents of consciousness. This syncretic deposition is said to bestow both worldly benefits (bhoga) and liberation (mokṣa) simultaneously.

## Key Verses

- **139cd–145 (lines 449–450):** The combination of the deposition of Mātṛkā and Mālinī for both worldly benefits and liberation.

## Philosophical Meaning

1. **Mātṛkā as pravṛtti, Mālinī as nivṛtti:** The two orders of the alphabet — the outgoing (Mātṛkā) and the returning (Mālinī) — correspond to the two movements of consciousness: manifestation and withdrawal. To combine them is to embody both movements simultaneously.

2. **The third state — sahaja:** When the two currents are united, the practitioner abides in the "co-emergent" (sahaja) state — the natural, effortless condition of non-dual awareness that includes both poles.

3. **Bhoga and mokṣa are not opposed:** The combination of emanatory and dissolutory depositions demonstrates that worldly enjoyment and liberation are not enemies. Enjoyment experienced as the play (līlā) of consciousness IS liberation.

4. **The body as the site of both:** The practitioner's body now contains both alphabets — the entire universe of phonemic energies in both their active and restful modes. The body is no longer a site of bondage but the field of Śiva's play.

## The Two Orders of the Alphabet

The esoteric teaching is that the alphabet is not a linear sequence but a circle. Mātṛkā goes from A to KṢ; Mālinī goes from KṢ to A. Together they form the cycle AHAṀ — "I am" — the self-reflexive awareness that is the essence of Śiva.
