# Deep-Dive: The World of the Fire of Time (Kālāgnirudra)

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 22cd–24ab  
**Catalog Entry:** #295  
**Lines:** 5901–6000+

---

## Topic Overview

The world of Kālāgni (the Fire of Time) is the lowest world in the manifest cosmos. It is the realm of Rudra as the Fire of Time — the devouring power that consumes all things at the end of each cosmic cycle. From here, the ascent through the 224 worlds begins.

---

## Key Verses

### Verses 22cd–24ab (lines 5901–6000+)

Kālāgnirudra resides in this world, which is located below the Egg of Brahmā and above Ananta's world. His nature is pure devouring fire — the power of time that consumes all creation at the end of each kalpa.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Kālāgni as Time-Fire:** This is not a hell of punishment but the cosmic fire of temporal destruction. It represents time's power to consume all forms.

2. **The First World of Ascent:** Although it is the lowest manifest realm, it is the starting point of the yogi's ascent. By purifying identification with time, the yogi rises.

3. **Connection to Kālāgnirudrakālī:** This world is related to the Krama goddess Kālāgnirudrakālī, who governs the persistence of the subject (see Chapter 4).
