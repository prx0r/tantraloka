# Deep-Dive: The Seven Worlds (Saptaloka) — Bhūr, Bhuvar, Svar, Mahar, Jana, Tapa, Satya

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 118cd–165ab  
**Catalog Entries:** #313–318  
**Lines:** 11701–13000+

---

## Topic Overview

Above the Earth, the cosmic geography ascends through the seven worlds (saptaloka) — from Bhūrloka (the Earth) to Satyaloka (the World of Truth). These are the heavens where beings experience progressively subtler states of consciousness and bliss.

---

## Key Verses

### 1) Bhūrloka — Earth World (verses 118cd–121ab)
The Earth, including Jambudvīpa and all the islands and oceans. This is the realm of embodied existence, karma, and sensory experience.

### 2) Bhuvarloka — Atmospheric World (verses 121cd–142)
The space between Earth and the sun, inhabited by Siddhas, Gandharvas, and Yakṣas. The paths of the winds (pravaha, āvaha, etc.) carry beings through this realm. The seven winds move in patterns corresponding to the movement of consciousness.

### 3) Svarloka — Heaven (verses 144–149ab)
The realm of Indra and the gods, where the celestial city Amarāvatī is located. Heavenly pleasures and divine status are experienced here, but this is still within the sphere of karma and thus impermanent.

### 4) Maharloka — The Great World (verses 149cd–151)
The abode of great sages (bhṛgus, aṅgirases) who survive the cosmic dissolution. A transitional realm between the lower heavens and the higher worlds.

### 5) Janaloka — The World of People (verses 152–153ab)
The realm of Brahmā's mind-born sons (Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanātana, Sanatkumāra). Beings here are free from rebirth but still within the impure path.

### 6) Tapoloka — The World of Austerities (verses 153cd–154ab)
The realm of perfected yogis and ascetics. Tapas (austerity) has purified these beings to a high degree.

### 7) Satyaloka — The World of Truth (verses 154cd–155ab)
The highest of the seven worlds, the abode of Brahmā. Truth (satya) shines here without admixture of falsehood. Above Satyaloka begin the pure worlds of Viṣṇu, Rudra, and beyond.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Ascent Through the Lokas:** The seven worlds mark the stages of the soul's ascent from embodied existence to the threshold of the pure path.

2. **Impermanence of Heavens:** All seven lokas are within the domain of Māyā — even Svarloka is impermanent. Beings return from heaven when their merit is exhausted.

3. **Maharloka as Transition:** At the end of each cosmic cycle (kalpa), the lower three worlds are destroyed, but Maharloka survives — it is the threshold to the imperishable realms.

4. **The Three Higher Lokas:** Jana, Tapa, and Satya are the "higher three" — progressively subtler realms where beings are free from the cycle of rebirth but have not yet attained liberation.
