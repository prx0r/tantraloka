## Central Thesis
The entire cosmic time-cycle — days, nights, lunar months, eclipses, planetary transits — is projected into the breath. Each complete breathing cycle of 21,600 breaths contains all astronomical and astrological phenomena in microcosm. By meditating on these projections, the yogi experiences the cosmos as his own body.

## Script Outline

### 1. Opening: The Universe in a Breath (0:00–1:30)
- The breath contains all cosmic cycles — days, months, years, planetary periods
- This is not symbolic — it is a real correspondence — lines 432–444
- The 21,600 breaths per day = the cosmic number in microcosm — lines 3789–3792
- All time-cycles are present simultaneously in each breath

### 2. Day and Night in the Breath (1:30–3:00)
- Day = the active phase of the breath (prāṇa dominant) — lines 432–434
- Night = the restful phase (apāna dominant)
- The transits of the Sun through the zodiac = the breath's movement through the 12 dvādaśānta stations
- Sunrise = the breath at the Heart; noon = the breath at the Throat; sunset = the breath at the End of Twelve

### 3. The Planets and Their Stations (3:00–5:00)
- TĀ 6.66cd–75 — lines 435
- Each planet has a watch (prahara) in the breath cycle
- First watch: Sun from Heart to Throat — line 20709
- Second watch: Moon at the Palate — line 20711
- Third watch: Mars on the Forehead — line 20713
- Fourth watch: Mercury in Śakti (End of Twelve) — line 20714
- The planetary watches repeat in different orders according to the day

### 4. The Lunar Month (5:00–6:30)
- TĀ 6.76–77ab, 92cd–95 — lines 437, 440
- The waxing and waning of the Moon = the breath's increasing and decreasing
- The bright fortnight (śuklapakṣa) = inhalation phase
- The dark fortnight (kṛṣṇapakṣa) = exhalation phase
- The full moon = the breath fully retained at the End of Twelve

### 5. Eclipses in the Breath (6:30–8:00)
- Solar eclipse (TĀ 6.100cd–109ab) — line 443
  - The Sun (prāṇa) is obscured — the breath is suspended
  - Rahu (the ascending node) = the obstruction of the breath
- Lunar eclipse (TĀ 6.109cd–114ab) — line 444
  - The Moon (apāna) is obscured — inhalation is blocked
  - Ketu (the descending node) = the release of the breath
- Eclipses = the moments of greatest yogic opportunity

### 6. The Constellations (Nakṣatras) (8:00–9:30)
- TĀ 6.66cd–75 — lines 435
- The 27 nakṣatras = the 27 subtle movements within the breath's 21,600 cycles
- Each nakṣatra governs a specific quality and duration
- The serpent gods (nāgas) = the channels (nāḍīs) through which the breath flows — lines 435

### 7. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- Cosmic time is not outside — it is the rhythm of one's own breath
- The yogi who knows this carries the entire universe within
- Projection (vikalpana) becomes recognition (pratyabhijñā)
