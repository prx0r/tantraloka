# Deep-Dive: The Worlds of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, and the Hundred Rudras

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 155cd–193  
**Catalog Entries:** #317–321  
**Lines:** 13001–14200+

---

## Topic Overview

Above the seven lokas begin the "pure path" (śuddhādhvan) — the worlds of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Rudra, followed by the extensive system of the Hundred Rudras, the Eleven Rudras, the Hundred Eggs, and finally the principles from Water to Śiva as stages to liberation.

---

## Key Verses

### The Worlds of Brahmā and Viṣṇu (verses 155cd–158)
Brahmā's world is the creative aspect of the godhead, the source of the impulse to manifest. Viṣṇu's world is pervasive sustenance — the power that maintains the cosmic order.

### Rudra's World (verses 158–162ab)
Rudra's world (Śivaloka or Rudraloka) is the realm of withdrawal and reabsorption. Rudra here is not the destructive Rudra of the hells but the benevolent Lord who guides souls toward liberation.

### Elements of the Egg (verses 162cd–165ab)
The Egg of Brahmā is fashioned from the five gross and five subtle elements in a specific cosmic architecture.

### The Hundred Rudras (verses 168–184ab)
The Hundred Rudras are powerful manifestations of Śiva's sovereignty. Each governs a specific domain of cosmic function. They are governed by the Eleven Rudras (the ekādaśarudra).

### The Eleven Rudras (verses 180–184ab)
The classic eleven Rudras of Purāṇic tradition — headed by Śiva as the eleventh. They oversee the Hundred Rudras.

### The Hundred Eggs (verses 184cd–186ab)
According to the Siddhātantra, there are one hundred Eggs of Brahmā (universes), each with its own Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Rudra.

### The Sizes of Principles (verses 186cd–193)
The principles (tattvas) are stages to liberation. Their "sizes" — from the gross element Water (āpas) up to Śiva — represent the scope of consciousness each principle encompasses. The yogi who ascends through these principles expands his awareness correspondingly.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **The Pure Path:** Above the seven worlds, the realms are no longer governed by karma and Māyā. These are the worlds of pure consciousness.

2. **Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Rudra as Cosmic Functions:** Creation, sustenance, and withdrawal — the three fundamental operations of consciousness.

3. **The Hundred Rudras:** A complex system of divine sovereignty. Each Rudra rules a specific aspect of cosmic and yogic experience.

4. **Multiple Universes:** There are one hundred Eggs of Brahmā — we inhabit just one of many universes, each with its own divine hierarchy.

5. **Principles as Stages:** The tattvas from Water (āpas) through Earth, the senses, mind, ego, intellect, prakṛti, puruṣa, kalā, etc., up to Śiva — each is a stage of expansion of consciousness.
