## Central Thesis
The breath contains not just daily and monthly cycles but the largest cosmic time-periods: the year, the zodiacal ages, the twelve-year cycle of Jupiter, the sixty-year cycle, and even the lifetimes of Brahmā and the cosmic lords. Each breath is the Year (saṁvatsara) — Prajāpati himself — and beyond it, the timeless consciousness of Śiva.

## Script Outline

### 0. Opening: Scaling Up (0:00–1:30)
- From the micro-breath to the macro-cosmos: all time scales are present
- The year = the complete cycle of the breath through all 12 zodiacal signs — line 446
- Prajāpati = the Year (saṁvatsara) = the breath — lines 15540–15560
- The breath spans from the smallest moment to the largest cosmic age

### 2. The Zodiac in the Breath (1:30–3:30)
- TĀ 6.114cd–123 — line 446
- The 12 signs of the zodiac = the 12 dvādaśānta stations
- Aries = the Heart (beginning of exhalation)
- Taurus-Throat; Gemini-Palate; Cancer-Forehead; Leo-Crown; Virgo-End of Twelve
- Libra = the turn (beginning of inhalation); Scorpio-Sagittarius-Capricorn-Aquarius-Pisces = descent
- Each sign governs a specific breath-quality and duration

### 3. The Twelve-Year and Sixty-Year Cycles (3:30–5:30)
- TĀ 6.124–126ab: Jupiter's 12-year cycle = the 12 months amplified — line 447
- TĀ 6.126cd–130ab: The 60-year cycle = the complete cycle of Jupiter and Saturn — line 448
- These are projected into the breath as macro-rhythms
- The breath of a lifetime = one complete cosmic cycle
- The yogi who breathes consciously lives through all cycles in a single breath

### 4. The Egg of Brahmā (5:30–7:00)
- TĀ 6.135–140 — line 458
- The Egg of Brahmā (brahmāṇḍa) = the total cosmic egg
- It is contained within the breath's field
- The egg is the 36-finger span of the breath from Heart to End of Twelve
- Within this egg are all worlds, all beings, all time

### 5. The Night and Day of Brahmā (7:00–8:30)
- TĀ 6.141–147ab — line 459
- Brahmā's day = 4.32 billion human years
- Brahmā's night = equal period of dissolution
- These are one complete breath of the cosmic person
- The yogi's breath participates in this cosmic breathing

### 6. Lifespans of the Lords (8:30–9:30)
- TĀ 6.147cd–179 — line 463
- The lifetimes of cosmic lords: from Brahmā through Viṣṇu, Rudra, Sadāśiva, up to Śiva
- Each lifespan is a breath of the next higher being
- Śiva alone is timeless — beyond all breath, all cycle
- The yogi who reaches Śiva transcends time entirely

### 7. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- All cosmic cycles are present in the breath
- The breath is the Year, Prajāpati, Brahmā, and beyond
- The yogi who breathes consciously participates in cosmic time
- The ultimate goal: timeless awareness within the temporal breath
