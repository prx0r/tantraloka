## Central Thesis
Brahmānanda (Bliss of Brahman) and Mahānanda (Great Bliss) represent the first major integration of the subject-object polarity. In Brahmānanda, all objects fuse into unity through breath retention (kumbhaka). In Mahānanda, the individual subject itself is consumed in the fire of the rising breath (udāna) that flows through the central channel (suṣumnā).

## Script Outline

### 1. Brahmānanda: The Fusion of Objects (0:00–3:30)
- Arises on the plane of samāna (equalizing breath) — lines 3462–3463
- Occurs in breath retention (kumbhakavṛtti) — lines 3762–3763
- "Countless manifest objects have fused" — lines 3526–3528, 3764–3768
- The Bliss of Brahman is more intense than Parānanda — lines 3800–3807
- Brahman derives from √bṛh = "to augment, intensify" — lines 3800–3801
- Swami Lakshmanjoo: breath comes to a complete halt — lines 4172–4180

### 2. The Mechanism of Brahmānanda (3:30–5:30)
- Samāna equalizes prāṇa and apāna — lines 3767–3769
- The dualities of exhale/inhale, subject/object are suspended
- The yogi becomes "equal" (sama) — all objectivity made his own — line 3767
- The uvula (lambikā) centre is where breath passages are blocked — lines 4337–4339
- The breath feels as if rotating very fast around this centre — lines 4338–4340

### 3. Mahānanda: The Fire of Subjectivity (5:30–8:00)
- Arises in udāna (rising breath) through the Middle Way (suṣumnā) — lines 3532–3537, 3794–3796
- "Devouring the differentiated manifestation of the flux of means and object of knowledge" — lines 3532–3534
- This "devouring" = assimilating all breaths (21,600 daily) into consciousness — lines 3789–3792
- The yogi rests in the "Great Bliss, considered to be the most excellent perceiver" — lines 3797–3799
- Swami Lakshmanjoo: the yogi feels he is dying — individual ego is ending — lines 4347–4354

### 4. The Transition Between (8:00–9:30)
- "Having attained a state of rest there, he becomes tranquil within this great fire" — lines 3830–3833
- The fire of udāna burns out having consumed its fuel (objectivity) — lines 3873–3876
- This tranquillity is the gateway to Cidānanda — lines 3844–3846
- The yogi must not let attention slacken even slightly — lines 4182–4183, 4204–4213

### 5. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- Brahmānanda = oneness with all objects (universal content)
- Mahānanda = oneness with the supreme perceiver (universal subject)
- Together they prepare the dissolution of the subject-object framework itself
