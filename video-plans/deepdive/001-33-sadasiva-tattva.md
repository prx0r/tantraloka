# SADĀŚIVA TATTVA — The I-This
## 10-Minute Deep-Dive Video Essay — Tantrāloka Vol. 1, Chapter 1

---

### Core Teaching

Sadāśiva tattva is the 34th principle, the first emergence of subject-object polarity within the unity of Śakti. It is defined as "I am This" (*aham idam*) — a state in which subjectivity (*ahaṁ*) predominates while objectivity (*idam*) is still faint and subsumed. Abhinavagupta locates Sadāśiva as the first of the three "pure" principles above Māyā where consciousness experiences the universe as one with itself. In the phonemic cosmology, Sadāśiva corresponds to the vowel Ī, the third phase of the expansion of consciousness.

---

### Key Verse — The Trika Triad of Pervasion (TĀ 1.188cd-189ab)

**Sanskrit:**
```
puṅvidyāśaktisaṁjñaṁ yat tat sarvavyāpakaṁ yataḥ |
avyāpakebhyastenedaṁ bhedena gaṇitaṁ kila || 188 ||
aśuddhiśuddhyamānatvaśuddhitastu mitho 'pi tat || 189 ||
```

**Dyczkowski Translation:**
"The three called the Person, Knowledge and Power pervade the entire (series of thirty-six metaphysical principles). Thus, they have been reckoned apart from the other (principles), which are not pervasive. They are also mutually (related and distinguished) as (the Person spans) the impure (principles), (Knowledge spans those) in the process of being purified, and (Power spans the one that) is pure."

**Explanation:**
Knowledge (Vidyā) spans from Śuddhavidyā to Sadāśiva — the principles in transition from impurity to purity. Sadāśiva tattva sits at the apex of this transitional zone, where the "I" still dominates the "This."

---

### Jayaratha's Commentary

On TĀ 1.189 (line ~28683): Jayaratha explains that Knowledge is "in the process of being purified" because the principles from Śuddhavidyā up to Sadāśiva consist of *bhedābheda* (unity-in-diversity). Sadāśiva is the highest of these, where the unity of the subject nearly overwhelms the nascent object — "I" is prominent, "This" is barely manifest.

Jayaratha notes that Sadāśiva's pervasion extends from above Māyā, and its perceivers are the *Mantramaheśvaras* — the lords of mantra at the threshold of pure subjectivity.

---

### Philosophical Significance

1. **I-This Structure**: Sadāśiva is the first tattva where the Self (*aham*) begins to relate to an other (*idam*). Unlike Śakti tattva where power is still undifferentiated, here the universe begins to appear as a faint "this" within the "I." The emphasis remains on the subject.

2. **Threshold of Manifestation**: Sadāśiva marks the transition from pure nonduality (*abheda* in Śiva-Śakti) to what will become the balanced duality of Śuddhavidyā and then the full ob jectivity of the impure path. It is the cosmic "yes" to manifestation.

3. **Phonemic Identity**: In the Mālinīvijayottara system, Sadāśiva corresponds to Ī — the long Ī that extends the energy of I-consciousness outward while retaining its subjective core.
