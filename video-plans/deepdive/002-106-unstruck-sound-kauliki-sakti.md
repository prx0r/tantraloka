# Topic 106: Unstruck Sound and Kaulikīśakti

## Catalog Reference

- **Verses**: cf. TĀ 3/66cd-67ab and SvT 7/59

## 1. Core Teaching

The Unstruck Sound (anāhata) is the spontaneous mantra HAṀSA that every living being repeats with each breath. This is the ajapa-mantra — the mantra not recited but perpetually uttered by the breath itself. Inhaling HAṀ, exhaling SA — 21,600 times each day and night. Kaulikīśakti is the energy that pervades both Kula (the immanent universe) and Akula (the transcendent). She is the mistress of the body, vital breath, and pleasure — the vibrant effulgence of consciousness within all.

## 2. Key Verses

**Śvacchandatantra quotation** (lines 10686-10691): "The god who resides in the chest of living beings spontaneously utters the mantra not uttered by anybody and that none obstructs."

## 3. Kṣemarāja's Commentary (lines 10741-10761): "HAṀSA is the supreme formless Lord. He utters spontaneously, pulses radiantly, having transcended the entire procession of denotators and all they denote. There is no one who utters it or obstructs it. He abides within the heart as the very life of life itself.

---

## 5. Philosophical Context

This teaching is part of Abhinavagupta's systematic exposition of the Supreme Means (Śāmbhavopāya) in Chapter 3 of the Tantrāloka. It integrates the Doctrine of Reflection with the alphabet mysticism of the Tantric traditions, showing how the structures of language embody the structures of consciousness.

## 6. Visual Concepts

- **The mirror of consciousness** with all phenomena reflected within it
- **The fifty letters** arranged as a maṇḍala of energies
- **AHAṀ** as the encompassing cycle of self-awareness
- **The five-fold bindu** as the pentad of divine activities
- **The four levels of speech** from transcendent silence to uttered sound
