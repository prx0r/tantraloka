# Topic 105: The Letter A as Anuttara, Anākhya, Kula and Akula

## Catalog Reference
- **Topic**: 105 of 161
- **Section**: Volume 2 — Chapter 3: Paropāya (The Supreme Means)
- **Verses**: TĀ 3/66cd-67ab

---

## 1. Core Teaching

The letter A is Anuttara (the Absolute), Anākhya (the Inexplicable). It is Akula (transcendent) and its power is Kaulikīśakti (the energy of universality). A is the first letter of the alphabet, the first vibration of consciousness, the seed of all sound and manifestation. It contains within itself, in potential form, all the other letters and thus all the energies of consciousness.

## 2. Key Verses

**TĀ 3/66cd-67ab** (lines 10471-10474): *akulasya parā yeyaṁ kaulikī śaktir uttamā*
The supreme power Kaulikī, which is Parā, of Akula (the transcendent, A).

## 3. Jayaratha's Commentary

**On Kaulikīśakti** (lines 10611-10641): "Kaulikī is the supreme power whose nature it is to be endowed with the unfolding perception of the universe — Kula. This is the expansion of Akula's energy. The Lord is never separated from this power."

## 4. Dyczkowski's Notes

**On the letter A** (lines 10311-10343): "The letter A is a measure of the power of consciousness (kalā), which does not belong to the domain of Māyā. It transcends hearing, is spontaneous, the wonder of one's own nature, the great sea of consciousness that, without waves, rests in the Great Light.

## 5. Philosophical Context

This teaching is part of Abhinavagupta's systematic exposition of the Supreme Means (Śāmbhavopāya) in Chapter 3 of the Tantrāloka. It integrates the Doctrine of Reflection with the alphabet mysticism of the Tantric traditions, showing how the structures of language embody the structures of consciousness.

## 6. Visual Concepts
