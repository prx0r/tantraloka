# 347 — Various Sequences of the Kañcukas
**Tantrāloka 9.45cd–48ab** | Chapter 9: Kāryakāraṇa (Causality)

## Hook
The five coverings (*kañcukas*) — time, necessity, attachment, limited knowledge, limited agency — are the architecture of bondage. But their order reveals their function.

## Core Teaching
Each *kañcuka* is a modality of the one limiting power of Māyā. Their different sequences in scripture teach different aspects of the binding process.

## The Five Kañcukas
1. **Kāla** (Time) — divides the eternal now into past, present, future
2. **Niyati** (Necessity) — the law of karmic consequence
3. **Rāga** (Attachment) — desire that drives action
4. **Vidyā** (Limited Knowledge) — partial cognition
5. **Kalā** (Limited Agency) — restricted power to act

## Two Key Orders

### Order of Emanation (ascent of limitation)
Māyā → Kāla → Niyati → Kalā → Vidyā → Rāga
(The universe contracts from subtle to gross limitation)

### Order of Withdrawal (descent to freedom)
Rāga → Vidyā → Kalā → Niyati → Kāla → Māyā
(The soul ascends by shedding each layer)

## Philosophical Significance
The *kañcukas* are not separate entities but aspects of a single act: consciousness limiting itself. Therefore:
- They can be enumerated differently without contradiction
- Each enumeration highlights a different aspect of the binding
- The true order is the one that leads to liberation for a particular aspirant

## Connection to the Pure Path
Above Māyā lie the Pure Principles (Śiva, Śakti, Sadāśiva, Īśvara, Śuddhavidyā) which have no *kañcukas*. The coverings only operate in the impure universe (31 tattvas from Māyā down to Earth).

## Bridge to Pure Principles (9.49cd–55ab)
Having described the coverings' sequences, Abhinava now turns to what lies above them: the five pure principles that constitute the *śuddhādhvan* (pure path), where consciousness operates without the limiting factors of time, necessity, attachment, limited knowledge, or limited agency.
