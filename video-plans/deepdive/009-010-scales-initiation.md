# The Scales Initiation (Tulādīkṣā)

**Tantrāloka 20.1–16ab | Lines 546–564**

## Overview

The initiation validated by the scales (tulādīkṣā) is a unique form of initiation where the purification of the disciple's karma is objectively demonstrated by a physical change in weight. The disciple is weighed on a scale before and after the rite; if he has become lighter, the purification is deemed effective.

## Key Verses

- **2 (line 548):** The inner purification (antaḥsaṃskāra) that prepares the soul.
- **2–7 (lines 550–551):** The burning seed and the burning away of the three impurities — mala, karma, māyā.
- **8–11ab (lines 554–555):** Examination of the attainment of competence (adhikāraparīkṣā).
- **11cd–12ab (lines 556–557):** The procedure concerning the scales (tulāvidhi).
- **11cd–16ab (line 558):** Initiation in which the purification is apparent by a loss of weight.

## Philosophical Meaning

1. **The visible sign of the invisible:** The weight change is not a "miracle" but a natural consequence of purification. The three impurities (malatraya) are real ontological contractions of consciousness. When they are removed, the soul — though weightless — is no longer "weighted down" by karma.

2. **The burning seed:** The "seed" (bīja) that is burned is the latent karmic potential (saṃskāra). As it burns, its "heaviness" — its tendency to pull consciousness downward into embodiment — is removed.

3. **Competence is measurable:** The scales do not measure physical mass in the ordinary sense but the density of the soul's contraction (saṅkoca). A lighter soul is a more expanded one — closer to the fullness (pūrṇatā) of Śiva.

4. **Objective validation:** The Tulādīkṣā provides an objective, intersubjective validation that purification has occurred. This is not private, subjective feeling — it is publicly observable.

## The Philosophical Problem of Weight

How can spiritual purification affect physical weight? The non-dual answer: because the physical body is not separate from consciousness. The body is a "form" (mūrti) of Śiva, and its apparent materiality is a function of contracted awareness. When awareness expands, the body's density is transformed.
