## Central Thesis
The three seed-syllable mantras (bījamantras) — SAUḤ, KHPHREṀ, and KṢMRYŪM — are the condensed sonic forms of the three goddesses of the Trika: Parā, Parāparā, and Aparā. Each mantra encodes a complete ontology and practice, and their utterance within the breath is the key to accessing the corresponding levels of reality.

## Script Outline

### 1. Opening: The Trika Seed Mantras (0:00–1:30)
- The three goddesses: Parā (supreme), Parāparā (supreme-nonsupreme), Aparā (nonsupreme) — lines 4514–4518
- Their seed mantras are the sonic essences of their respective domains
- These mantras are not invented — they are the natural sounds of consciousness at each level
- They are recited within the breath, not as external repetitions

### 2. SAUḤ — The Heart Seed of Parā (1:30–4:00)
- SAUḤ is the seed of the supreme goddess Parā — lines 4493–4496
- S = the Great Root (Māyā / the Square) — lines 4823–4832
- AU = the Trident (the three pure principles) — lines 4830–4832
- Ḥ = emission (visarga) into pure consciousness at the End of Twelve — lines 4831–4832
- SAUḤ pervades from the genital region to the crown — lines 4520–4528
- It is the mantra of the Heart (hṛdayabīja) — the core of all Trika practice — lines 4792–4793

### 3. KHPHREṀ — The Seed of the Fierce One (4:00–6:30)
- KHPHREṀ is the seed of Parāparā — lines 398–399
- KHPH = the gathering of the energies
- RE = the fire of consciousness
- Ṁ = the bindu, the point of completion
- Associated with the Krodha (fierce) aspect of the goddess
- Used in practices of transformation and purification
- The "crossing over" of obstacles

### 4. KṢMRYŪM — The Seed of the Lower Goddess (6:30–9:00)
- KṢMRYŪM is the seed of Aparā — lines 400–401
- KṢ = the final compound letter, encompassing the alphabet
- MRY = the gathering of the semivowels
- ŪM = the prolongation of the vowel Ū with the nasal
- Associated with the Vāmeśvarī goddesses
- Used for worldly benefits and the transformation of embodied existence

### 5. The Integration in Practice (9:00–10:00)
- The three seeds correspond to the three levels of utterance:
  - SAUḤ: supreme, through the Heart
  - KHPHREṀ: empowered, through the throat
  - KṢMRYŪM: individual, through the palate
- Together they encompass the entire range of consciousness
- Their recitation in the breath is the natural mantra of the Trika yogi
