# Deep-Dive: The Supreme Group of Eight Bodies, Twelve Suśivas, and Conclusion of Bhuvanādhvan

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 245–249cd  
**Catalog Entries:** #334–335  
**Lines:** 17001–17600+

---

## Topic Overview

The final passages of Chapter 8 present the pinnacle of the cosmic ascent. The Supreme Group of Eight Bodies (mitryaṣṭaka) represents the most subtle level of divine embodiment. The Twelve Suśivas (Auspicious Ones) governed by Vīrabhadra, and the Eight Mahādevas, complete the hierarchy of the Path of Worlds, leading to the transcendent Śiva who is beyond all worlds.

---

## Key Verses

### The Supreme Group of Eight Bodies (verses 245–249ab)
The mitryaṣṭaka at the supreme level consists of:
1. **Bhadrakālī** — the auspicious goddess
2. **Vīrabhadra** — the heroic form
3. **Śrīkaṇṭha** — the beautiful-throated Lord
4. **Umā** — the Goddess
5. **Sadāśiva** — the eternal benefactor
6. **Īśvara** — the sovereign Lord
7. **Śuddhavidyā** — pure knowledge
8. **Anāśrita Śiva** — the transcendent Śiva beyond all support

### The Twelve Suśivas (verse 249cd)
The Twelve Suśivas are governed by Vīrabhadra. They are:
- Twelve manifestations of Śiva's auspicious (śiva) nature
- Each governs a specific aspect of divine function
- They serve as intermediaries between Vīrabhadra and the Eight Mahādevas

### The Eight Mahādevas (verse 249cd)
The Eight Mahādevas (Great Śivas) are the highest expressions of divine embodiment before the formless absolute. They represent Śiva's eightfold sovereignty in the pure path.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **From Worlds to Gods:** The ascent through the worlds culminates in the deities who embody those worlds. The final realization is that all worlds are the body of the divine.

2. **The Supreme Eight (Mitryaṣṭaka):** These eight forms are the most subtle embodiment of the divine. They span from Bhadrakālī (power) to Anāśrita Śiva (the supportless absolute).

3. **The Twelve Suśivas:** These are called "su-śiva" (very auspicious) — they represent the complete perfection of Śiva's grace, twelve aspects of the divine's compassionate outpouring.

4. **Beyond All Worlds:** Above the Twelve Suśivas and Eight Mahādevas is the Anāśrita Śiva — the Lord who has no support, no world, no location. This is the ultimate goal of the ascent described throughout Chapter 8.

5. **The Complete Ascent:** The 224 worlds are not an exhaustive inventory of external places — they are a map of consciousness. The yogi who ascends through them recognizes them as aspects of his own awareness.

## Summary of the Bhuvanādhvan (Chapter 8)

The 224 worlds (bhuvanas) of the Bhuvanādhvan are arranged from lowest to highest:

| Level | Worlds | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Kālāgni | Fire of Time |
| 2 | Ananta's World | Serpent foundation |
| 3–37 | 35 Hells | Avīci, Kumbhīpāka, etc. |
| 38–45 | 8 Pātālas | Atala to Rasātala |
| 46 | Earth (Jambudvīpa) | Bhārata, etc. |
| 47–53 | 7 Lokas | Bhūr to Satya |
| 54–118 | Worlds of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, etc. | Including Hundred Rudras |
| 119–217 | Elemental and Subtle Body Worlds | Bhadrakālī through Umā |
| 218–224 | Supreme Eight Bodies, Suśivas, Mahādevas | Approach to Anāśrita |
