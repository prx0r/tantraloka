# ŚAKTI TATTVA — Reflexive Power of Consciousness
## 10-Minute Deep-Dive Video Essay — Tantrāloka Vol. 1, Chapter 1

---

### Core Teaching

Śakti tattva is the 35th principle — the first vibration within the unity of Śiva. It is *vimarśa* (reflective awareness), the power by which Śiva knows Himself as "I." Abhinavagupta identifies it as the *spanda* (pulsation) of consciousness, the dynamic aspect without which the Light of Śiva would be inert. Jayaratha explains that Śakti is not separate from Śiva but is His inherent power of self-awareness (*ahaṁvimarśa*). From this principle, the entire universe of subject-object distinction unfolds.

---

### Key Verse — The Goddess as Thirty-six Principles (TĀ 1.195)

**Sanskrit:**
```
yāvān ṣaṭtriṁśakaḥ so 'yaṁ yad anyad api kiñcana |
etāvatī mahādevī rudraśaktir anargalā || 195 ||
```

**Dyczkowski Translation:**
"As is this entire group of thirty-six (principles) and so too whatever else (may exist), such is the extent of the Great Goddess, Who is Rudra's power free of (all) restrictions."

**Explanation:**
The entire hierarchy of tattvas is Rudra's Śakti. She is *anargalā* — unrestricted, unobstructed. This verse identifies the totality of manifest reality with the Goddess, who is the power of consciousness itself. Śakti tattva is her first emanation — pure power before any qualification.

---

### Jayaratha's Commentary

On TĀ 1.195 (line ~29139): Jayaratha glosses "whatever else" as world-orders and other existents. The Goddess is "free of restrictions" because She pervades all things without obstruction. He quotes a Krama text: "This universe is Your Wheel of Energies as the perceived, perceiver and perception."

On the Trika Triad (1.189, line ~28679): Jayaratha explains that the Person (*puṁ*) spans the impure principles (Earth to Māyā), Knowledge (*vidyā*) spans the pure-cum-impure (Śuddhavidyā to Sadāśiva), and Power (*śakti*) spans the pure (Śakti to Śiva). Śakti tattva is thus co-extensive with the highest sphere of unity-in-difference (*bhedābheda*).

---

### Philosophical Significance

1. **Śakti as Vimarśa**: Without Śakti, Śiva would be *prakāśa* without self-awareness — a light that illumines nothing. Śakti is the reflexive turn by which consciousness knows itself. She is *citi* (consciousness-in-action), *spanda* (vibration), and *svātantrya* (freedom).

2. **First Differentiation**: Śakti tattva is the thinnest veil between pure nonduality and the appearance of distinction. She contains all subsequent principles as potentials. The transitions from Śiva → Śakti → Sadāśiva are not temporal but ontological — degrees of self-manifestation.

3. **Unity-in-Difference**: Unlike Śiva tattva (pure *abheda*) and the lower principles (pure *bheda*), Śakti tattva is the sphere of *bhedābheda* — a state where difference is present but not yet alienated from unity. It is the creative tension from which the universe arises.
