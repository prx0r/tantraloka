## Central Thesis
Prāṇavicāra — the investigation of the breath through recaka (exhalation), pūraka (inhalation), kumbhaka (retention), and śūnyāntara (void-spaces) — is the core yogic technology of both Āṇavopāya and Kālopāya. Each phase of the breath corresponds to a specific state of consciousness, a specific bliss, and a specific cosmic function. The void-spaces between breaths are the gates to the timeless.

## Script Outline

### 0. Opening: The Four Phases of Breath (0:00–1:30)
- The breath has four phases: out, in, hold, and the gap between
- Recaka = exhalation, prāṇa, the means of knowledge — lines 3680–3683
- Pūraka = inhalation, apāna, the object of knowledge — lines 3692–3696
- Kumbhaka = retention, samāna, the fusion of subject-object — lines 3762–3769
- Śūnyāntara = void-spaces, the intervals where consciousness rests — lines 4136–4156

### 2. Recaka — The Exhaled Breath (1:30–3:00)
- Prāṇa rises from Heart to End of Twelve — lines 3680–3683
- This is Nirānanda — the Bliss of Stillness — lines 3678–3688
- The movement of manifesting consciousness outward
- Associated with the Sun (solar breath)
- Duration: one HAṀ from Heart to End of Twelve

### 3. Pūraka — The Inhaled Breath (3:00–4:30)
- Apāna descends from End of Twelve to Heart — lines 3692–3696, 3755–3756
- This is Parānanda — the Bliss of the Other — lines 3690–3696
- The movement of consciousness returning to its source
- Associated with the Moon (lunar breath)
- Duration: one SAḤ from End of Twelve to Heart

### 4. Kumbhaka — Retention (4:30–6:00)
- The breath is held, prāṇa and apāna are equalized — lines 3762–3769
- This is Brahmānanda — the Bliss of Brahman — lines 3758–3775
- Objects fuse into unitary consciousness — lines 3762–3768
- Samāna (equalizing breath) is the presiding breath
- Swami Lakshmanjoo: the breath passages are blocked; the breath feels as if spinning — lines 4336–4341

### 5. Śūnyāntara — The Void Spaces (6:00–8:00)
- The spaces BETWEEN breaths — the pause after exhalation before inhalation
- These are the "voids" — the gates to the timeless — lines 4136–4156
- The yogi practices "centring" — attention to the gap — lines 4097–4101
- Nijānanda is the bliss of resting in these void-spaces — lines 3440–3469
- The void-spaces are the Fourth State (turīya) manifesting between states — lines 4084–4098

### 6. Integration (8:00–9:30)
- Recaka + Pūraka = the pulse of the universe (spanda)
- Kumbhaka = the moment of recognition between pulses
- Śūnyāntara = the ground from which pulses arise
- All four phases together constitute the complete cycle of consciousness
- The 21,600 daily breaths = 21,600 opportunities for realization — lines 3789–3792

### 7. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- Prāṇavicāra transforms ordinary breathing into contemplative practice
- Each phase of the breath is a phase of consciousness
- The void-spaces are the most important — they reveal the timeless
- Mastery of the breath's four phases leads to mastery of time
