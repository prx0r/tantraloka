# Deep-Dive: Beyond Jambūdvīpa — Islands, Seas, and Mount Lokāloka

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 102–118ab  
**Catalog Entries:** #309–312  
**Lines:** 10500–11700+

---

## Topic Overview

Beyond Jambudvīpa and its surrounding ocean of salt lie six concentric island-continents (dvīpas), each separated by a sea of a different substance. At the outermost edge of the cosmos is Mount Lokāloka — the ring-mountain where light meets darkness.

---

## Key Verses

### The Six Islands and Seas (verses 102–107ab)

| Island | Sea | Substances |
|--------|-----|------------|
| Jambūdvīpa | Salt Ocean | Salt water |
| Plakṣadvīpa | Sugarcane Juice Sea | Sugarcane |
| Śālmali | Wine Sea | Wine |
| Kuśadvīpa | Clarified Butter Sea | Ghee |
| Krauñca | Milk Sea | Milk |
| Śākadvīpa | Curd Sea | Curd |
| Puṣkaradvīpa | Fresh Water Sea | Water |

### Mount Lokāloka (verses 107cd–109ab)

The outermost mountain ring of the cosmos, made of gold. Beyond it is total darkness. Inside it, the light of the sun, moon, and stars circulates. The Rudras of the Eight Directions guard this boundary.

### Ajavīthī and Suvīthī (verses 109cd–110ab)
- **Ajavīthī** (Goat Path): The northern path of the sun, the path of the fathers (pitṛyāna)
- **Suvīthī** (Good Path): The path of the gods (devayāna)

### Rising and Setting of the Sun (verses 110cd–113ab)

The sun rises and sets daily around Mount Meru and Lokāloka, creating the cycles of day and night across the different regions.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Concentric Geography:** The Earth is composed of concentric island-continents separated by seas of different substances, following Purāṇic cosmology.

2. **Lokāloka as Cosmic Boundary:** Lokāloka means "World-Non-World" — it is the limit of the manifest cosmos. Beyond it is the realm of non-manifestation.

3. **Ajavīthī and Suvīthī:** These two paths represent the two destinies after death — the pitṛyāna (path of the fathers, rebirth) and devayāna (path of the gods, liberation).

4. **Cosmic Architecture:** The entire system — from the hells below to Lokāloka above — is a single, integrated structure within the Egg of Brahmā.
