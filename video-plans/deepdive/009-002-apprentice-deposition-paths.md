# Apprentice Initiation: Deposition of the Paths

**Tantrāloka 16.73–163ab | Lines 1301–1800**

## Overview

The teacher projects the six paths (ṣaḍadhvan) onto the disciple's body. These are: the path of principles (tattva), worlds (bhuvana), mantra-parts (pada), phonemes (varṇa), and forces (kalā). The disciple's body becomes a living map of the entire cosmos.

## Key Verses

- **73–76:** Propitiation of the fire.
- **77–94ab (lines 655–656):** Inflaming the teacher's own essential nature (svabhāva) — the teacher must first realize within himself the paths he will deposit.
- **94cd–96 (line 656):** Deposition of a path on the disciple's body.
- **97–99 (line 657):** The variety of paths to be purified.
- **100–113 (line 658):** Projection of the 36 tattvas onto the body.
- **114–131 (line 659):** Projection of the path of worlds (bhuvanādhvan) — from earth to the abode of Śiva.
- **132–139ab (line 660):** Deposition of the path of mantra-parts (padādhvan).
- **139cd–146 (line 661):** Deposition of the path of phonemes (varṇādhvan).
- **147–150ab (line 662):** Deposition of the path of forces (kalādhvan).
- **150cd–163ab (line 663):** Deposition of the seventy purifying mantras.

## Philosophical Meaning

1. **The body as all paths:** The body is not a physical object among other objects but the intersection of ALL paths of reality. By depositing the paths on the disciple's body, the teacher reveals what has always been true: the body is the cosmos.

2. **The teacher inflames his own nature:** Before depositing anything on the disciple, the teacher must "inflame" (prajvālana) his own essential nature. This means entering deep samādhi on each path — because one can only transmit what one IS.

3. **Seventy purifying mantras:** The 70 mantras correspond to the 70 divisions of reality across the six paths. Each mantra purifies a specific aspect of the disciple's contracted consciousness.

4. **The variety of paths means variety of disciples:** Not every disciple needs all paths deposited. The teacher selects the path appropriate to the disciple's competence (yogyatā). Some need the gross paths (tattva, bhuvana); some need the subtle (varṇa, kalā).

## Corporeal Soteriology

The deposition of paths on the body means that salvation is not escape FROM embodiment but the RECOGNITION of embodiment as the full expression of Śiva. The body becomes the Veda, the temple, and the goal.
