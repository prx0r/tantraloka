# BUDDHI TATTVA — The Intellect
## 10-Minute Deep-Dive Video Essay — Tantrāloka Vol. 1, Chapter 1

---

### Core Teaching

Buddhi tattva is the 23rd principle — the intellect, the first evolute from Prakṛti. It is the capacity of determination (*adhyavasāya*), the faculty of certainty that discriminates, decides, and knows. In the Sāṁkhya evolution, Buddhi is *mahat* (the Great One) — the first cosmic product, from which all subsequent principles unfold. It contains in seed form the entire objective universe. In the Trika analysis, Buddhi is the first veil of objectivity — the intellect functioning as both the instrument of discernment and the repository of karmic impressions (*vāsanās*).

---

### Key Verse — The Impure Principles (TĀ 1.189cd)

**Dyczkowski notes (Vol. 1, line ~28876):**
"Then come the three inner mental senses: ego (ahaṁkāra), mind (manas), and intellect (buddhi). Above them is Nature (prakṛti) and the individual soul (puruṣa)."

**On the Kañcukas (line ~28880):**
"The six above the individual soul are related to its limitations."

**Explanation:**
Buddhi is the highest of the three internal organs (*antaḥkaraṇa*), the faculty of decisive knowledge that is the direct product of Prakṛti's disturbance.

---

### Jayaratha's Commentary

On the intellect (Vol. 5, line ~19102): Jayaratha explains that Buddhi is the principle of determination — the faculty that ascertains "this is such and such." It is the first ripple in the material ocean of Prakṛti, the initial differentiation of the undifferentiated.

On buddhi as cosmic (Vol. 5, line ~19358): "The cause of the intellect (buddhitattva) is the Unmanifest (avyakta), that is, the disturbed state of the guṇas." The entire subsequent evolution from ego to Earth is contained in Buddhi as the tree is contained in the seed.

---

### Philosophical Significance

1. **Decisive Knowledge**: Buddhi is *niścayātmikā buddhiḥ* — the nature of intellect is certainty. Unlike Manas (which deliberates) and Ahaṁkāra (which appropriates), Buddhi resolves. It is the faculty of judgment that concludes "this is true."

2. **First Product of Prakṛti**: As the first evolute, Buddhi is the most sattvic (luminous) of the products of Prakṛti. It reflects the consciousness of Puruṣa most clearly, which is why the Sāṁkhya says "As is the reflection, so is the intellect."

3. **Repository of Impressions**: Buddhi stores *saṁskāras* — the latent impressions of past experience that shape present perception. It is both the light of discernment and the storehouse of karma.
