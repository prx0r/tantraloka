# Deep-Dive: The Root, Middle and Summit of Consciousness — Triśirobhairava Teaching

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 12–15ab  
**Catalog Entry:** #292  
**Lines:** 5004–5228

---

## Topic Overview

Abhinava presents an even simpler model of ascent than the Five Causes — just three levels of consciousness taught in the Triśirobhairava. These three levels (root, middle, summit) correspond to the three states of contracted, expanded, and transcendent awareness, within which all 36 principles and their worlds are distributed.

---

## Key Verses

### Verses 12–15ab (lines 5020–5084)
> triśiraḥśāsane bodho mūlamadhyāgrakalpitaḥ
> sattriṁśattattvasaṁrambhaḥ smṛtir bhedavikalpanā
> avyāhatavibhāgo 'smibhāvo mūlaṁ tu bodhagam
> samastatattvabhāvo 'yaṁ svātmany evāvibhāgakaḥ
> bodhamadhyaṁ bhavet kiṁcid ādhārādheyalakṣaṇam
> tattvabhedavibhāgena svabhāvasthitilakṣaṇam
> bodhāgraṁ tat tu cidbodhaṁ nistaraṅgaṁ bṛhatsukham
> *"According to the Triśirobhairava, consciousness, conceived to have a root, middle and summit, is the pulse of the dynamic interaction of the thirty-six principles, whereas memory is conceptualization in terms of relative distinctions. The root of consciousness is the sense of personal existence, dominated by unobstructed division between the perceiver and objects. The middle is this condition in which all the principles are experienced collectively as a unified whole within one's own nature, without division, with the subtle characteristic of sustainer and sustained. The summit is the permanent state of abiding in one's own nature, the awakened consciousness of consciousness, devoid of the waves of manifestation, which is intense bliss."*

---

## The Three Levels

| Level | Name | Characteristic | Tattvas |
|-------|------|---------------|---------|
| **Root (Mūla)** | Asmibhāva (I-ness) | Unobstructed duality, objectivity predominates | Earth to Kalā |
| **Middle (Madhya)** | Idam-Aham (This-I) | Unity of principles, balanced subject-object | Śuddhavidyā level |
| **Summit (Agra)** | Cidbodha (Consciousness-Consciousness) | Nondual, waveless, intense bliss | Śiva-Śakti |

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Root = Duality:** The contracted perceiver identified with the intellect and senses — "I know this." Memory and conceptualization dominate.

2. **Middle = Unity-in-Diversity:** "This am I" (idam aham) — the balanced state where subject and object are on the same level, neither dual nor nondual.

3. **Summit = Pure Nonduality:** Awakened consciousness of consciousness — no waves of manifestation, only intense bliss (bṛhatsukha).

4. **The Thirty-Six Principles as Bodha-Saṁrambha:** The entire tattva hierarchy is the "pulse" (saṁrambha) of consciousness, not something separate from it.

5. **Direct Experience:** By contemplating these three levels, awakened consciousness is experienced directly.
