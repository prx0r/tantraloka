# Deep-Dive: The Regions Around Meru — Ilāvṛta, Ketumāla, Bhadrāśva, Kuru, Bhārata

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 68–92  
**Catalog Entries:** #304–308  
**Lines:** 8001–10499

---

## Topic Overview

This section describes in detail the nine regions (varṣas) of Jambudvīpa, their rulers, inhabitants, characteristics, and the unique significance of Bhārata (India) as the land of karma and liberation. The regions range from celestial paradises where beings enjoy effortless bliss to Bhārata, where suffering and effort make liberation possible.

---

## Key Verses

### Ilāvṛta (verse 68, lines 8001–8100)
The central region around Meru where the gods reside. Inhabitants live 13,000 years, free from premature death, surrounded by Gandharvas, Siddhas, Ṛṣis, and Apsarases.

### Ketumāla (verse 69)
West of Meru, beyond Gandhamādana — a land of seven mountains, abundant with beautiful women and natural pleasures.

### Bhadrāśva (verses 70–71)
East of Meru, beyond Mālyavat — with five mountains inhabited by sages and spiritual beings.

### Kuru, Hiraṇyamaya, Ramyaka (verses 72–75)
The northern regions — Kuru is shaped like a bow, 9,000 leagues long. Hiraṇyamaya and Ramyaka are progressively more blissful.

### Hari, Kinnara, Bhārata (verses 76–83)
The southern regions leading to Bhārata.

### Bhārata (verses 80–84, lines 10200–10499)
Bhārata is unique — it is the land where the four varṇas and āśramas (castes and stages of life) exist, where dharma can be practiced, and where liberation is possible. Only Bhārata produces karma that leads to heaven or liberation.

### Kanyādvīpa / Kumārīdvīpa (verses 85–92, lines 10300–10499)
The island of Kumārī (India south of the Himalayas) is the best place for karmic accumulation. It contains:
- 10 million Rudras headed by Mahākāla
- 500 sacred rivers beginning with Gaṅgā
- Mahākāla, Ekāmra, and other sacred sites
- Birth here is rare and precious

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Bhārata = Land of Karma:** Only in Bhārata can beings accumulate new karma. In other regions, they only experience the fruits of past karma.

2. **Kanyādvīpa as Supreme:** The island of Kumārī is the most sacred part of Bhārata — where liberation is most accessible and karma accumulates most efficiently.

3. **Suffering as Opportunity:** Unlike the paradises where beings enjoy effortless bliss (and cannot progress), Bhārata's suffering makes spiritual striving possible.

4. **The Rudras of Bhārata:** Ten million Rudras, headed by Mahākāla, have descended to Bhārata to assist beings toward liberation.
