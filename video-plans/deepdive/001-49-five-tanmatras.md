# THE FIVE TANMĀTRAS (SUBTLE ELEMENTS) — Śabda, Sparśa, Rūpa, Rasa, Gandha
## 10-Minute Deep-Dive Video Essay — Tantrāloka Vol. 1, Chapter 1

---

### Core Teaching

The five *tanmātras* (subtle elements) are principles 6–10 in the tattva hierarchy. They are the generic sensations — sound (*śabda*), touch (*sparśa*), sight-form (*rūpa*), taste (*rasa*), and smell (*gandha*) — that arise from the tāmasa (obscuring) aspect of Ahaṃkāra. The word *tanmātra* literally means "just that" — the pure quality of sensation before it is concretized into a specific object. They are the bridge between the inner senses and the gross elements: the subtle prototypes from which the five mahābhūtas are formed.

---

### Key Verse — The Five Elements (TĀ 1.190)

**Sanskrit:**
```
bhūtāny adhyakṣasiddhāni kāryahetvanumeyataḥ |
tattvavargāt pṛthag bhūtasamākhyāny ata eva hi || 190 ||
```

**Dyczkowski Translation:**
"(The existence) of the gross elements (bhūta) is proved by direct perception, and so they are classified separately from the (other) group of principles (tattva), (whose existence) can only be inferred by (their) effects, (of which they are) the cause."

**Explanation:**
The tanmātras are inferred, not perceived — they are inferred as the causes of the gross elements. They are the subtle essence of each sense-quality.

---

### Jayaratha's Commentary

On the tanmātras (Vol. 1, line ~28811): Jayaratha explains that the subtle elements (tanmātra) are not specific (*aviśeṣa*) because, unlike the gross elements, they cannot be perceived as particular. They are the generic sensations from which specific gross elements are compounded.

Jayaratha cites the Svāyambhuva: "The gross elements (come) from the subtle elements (tanmātra)." Space (ākāśa), for example, arises from touch alone; Earth arises from all five tanmātras, with smell predominant.

---

### Philosophical Significance

1. **The Pure Quality**: Each tanmātra is the essence of a sense-modality stripped of particularity — sound-as-such, touch-as-such, form-as-such, etc. They are the archetypal sensations from which all particular sensory experiences derive.

2. **From Tāmasa Ahaṃkāra**: The tanmātras arise from the tamas (inertia) aspect of Ahaṃkāra, making them the most "objective" of the evolutes. They are the first step toward gross materiality — the transition from cognitive to physical reality.

3. **Seed of the Gross Elements**: Each gross element (mahābhūta) is formed from specific combinations of tanmātras. Space is from śabda and sparśa, Air adds rūpa, Fire adds rasa, Water adds gandha, Earth has all five. The multiplication of qualities generates the material universe.
