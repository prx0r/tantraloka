## Central Thesis
Cidānanda (Bliss of Consciousness) and Jagadānanda (Cosmic Bliss) are the culminating states of the six-bliss progression. Cidānanda is the Great Pervasion (mahāvyāpti) where consciousness shines as all things, free of the insentient. Jagadānanda is the permanent establishment of this realization in both introverted and extroverted awareness — the state of Anupāya itself.

- "When this becomes fixed and permanent, this is Jagadānanda" — lines 4383–4385

### 6. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- Cidānanda = consciousness pervading all as one's own being
- Jagadānanda = permanent establishment of this in all states
- The ultimate fruit of the entire bliss progression is this recognition
