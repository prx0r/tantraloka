# Deep-Dive: The Subtle Body — Spheres of Elements, Eight Bodies of Śiva, and Cosmic Anatomy

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 215cd–238ab  
**Catalog Entries:** #327–331  
**Lines:** 15201–16400+

---

## Topic Overview

Having ascended through the world-systems of the outer cosmos, the yogi now turns inward to the subtle body. The spheres of the subtle elements (tanmātras), the deities of the organs of action and knowledge, the moon as the sphere of manas (mind), the Rudras of the ego, and the five groups of eight Rudras within the intellect — all form the inner cosmic anatomy.

---

## Key Verses

### The Eight Bodies of Śiva (verses 215cd–221ab)
The eight bodies (mitryaṣṭaka or aṣṭamūrti) of Śiva — Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space, the Sun, the Moon, and the Sacrificer (yajamāna/Ātman) — each represent a cosmic function embodied in the subtle body of the yogi.

### The Deities of the Organs (verses 221cd–223ab)
- **Organs of Knowledge (Jñānendriyas):** Hearing → Dik (Space/Quarter), Touch → Vāyu, Sight → Sūrya (Sun), Taste → Varuṇa, Smell → Aśvins
- **Organs of Action (Karmendriyas):** Speech → Agni, Grasp → Indra, Gait → Viṣṇu, Excretion → Mitra, Generation → Prajāpati

### The Moon and the Sphere of Manas (verses 223cd–225ab)
The Moon is the sphere of mind (manas) — the receptive, reflective faculty that mediates between the senses and the intellect.

### The Rudras of the Ego (verses 225cd–228ab)
The ego (ahaṃkāra) has eight Rudras — eight forms of self-assertion that govern the sense of individual identity.

### Five Groups of Eight Rudras in the Intellect (verses 228cd–238ab)
Within the intellect (buddhi) reside five groups of eight Rudras:
1. The eight Vasus
2. The eight Maruts
3. The eight Viśvedevas
4. The eight Sādhyas
5. The eight Aṅgiras
Each group governs a specific aspect of cognitive function.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Microcosm-Macrocosm:** The same deities that govern the outer cosmos reside within the yogi's subtle body. The outer journey is mirrored inward.

2. **The Eight Bodies of Śiva:** Śiva manifests as eight concrete forms — the elements and celestial bodies — making the divine accessible within creation itself.

3. **Sense-Deities:** Each sense organ has a presiding deity who activates and governs its function. Perceiving through the sense is worshipping the deity.

4. **The Mind-Moon:** Manas is lunar — it reflects the light of consciousness (the Sun of the Self) and waxes/wanes with attention.

5. **The Rudras of Ego and Intellect:** The sense of "I" is not monolithic — it is composed of multiple Rudra-forces that can be purified and transformed.
