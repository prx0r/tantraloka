## Central Thesis
The Trika Liṅga is the subtle body of the Trika system — the Trident (triśūla) that contains the 36 principles arranged along its axis. It serves as the inner maṇḍala for meditation, the support for mantra utterance, and the vehicle through which the yogi ascends from the lowest principle (Earth) to the highest (Śiva).

## Script Outline

### 1. The Trika Liṅga as Maṇḍala Body (0:00–2:00)
- The Trika is centred on the Trident maṇḍala (triśūlamaṇḍala) — lines 4497–4518
- The Liṅga = the subtle body projected as the Trident — lines 4522–4528
- The shaft extends from the genital region to the crown, containing the impure principles (Earth through Māyā)
- The three prongs above contain the pure principles (Śuddhavidyā through Śiva)
- The Liṅga encompasses the entire 36-tattva spectrum — lines 4522–4528

### 2. The Three Goddesses (2:00–4:00)
- The Trika: Parā, Parāparā, Aparā — lines 4514–4518
- The three prongs of the Trident correspond to the three goddesses
- Parā = the supreme (top prong)
- Parāparā = the supreme-nonsupreme (right prong)  
- Aparā = the nonsupreme (left prong)
- The shaft = the base of all three, corresponding to the impure manifestation

### 3. The Liṅga and the Breath (4:00–6:00)
- The Liṅga is the inner support for Hṛdayoccāra — lines 4520–4528
- SAUḤ pervades along the Liṅga from the base to the tip — lines 4493–4496
- The prāṇadaṇḍa (stick of the vital breath) activates the Liṅga — lines 4744–4753
- The breath's ascent through the Liṅga = the awakening of Kuṇḍalinī — lines 4744–4793
- Each centre along the Liṅga corresponds to a stage of the bliss progression

### 4. The Liṅga in Initiation (6:00–8:00)
- The Trika Liṅga is projected in the common (samaya) initiation — lines 4511–4513
- It serves as the "seat" (āsanakalpti) for the daily practice — lines 4514–4518
- The Liṅga is both the object of worship and the worshipper's own body
- "The formation of the seat" of the Trika goddesses — lines 4514–4518

### 5. The Liṅga and the Path of Principles (8:00–9:30)
- The 36 principles (tattvas) are arranged along the Liṅga axis — lines 4522–4528
- The ascent through the Liṅga = the progressive purification of each principle
- The impure principles (Earth through Māyā) occupy the shaft
- The pure principles occupy the prongs
- Beyond the prongs: the End of the Twelve, the plane of emission (visarga)

### 6. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- The Trika Liṅga is the complete inner architecture of the yogi's path
- It integrates body, breath, principles, deities, and mantras
- The Liṅga is the microcosmic form of the entire universe
