# Topic 85: Everything and All Means Are the Light of Consciousness

## Catalog Reference
- **Topic**: 85 of 161
- **Section**: Volume 2 — Chapter 3: Paropāya (The Supreme Means)
- **Verses**: 15-23ab

---

## 1. Core Teaching

The entire extent of means — external and internal — is just the Light of consciousness, Śiva's own body. All phenomena ("blue," "yellow," "pleasure," "pain"), all categories ("bondage," "liberation," "sentience," "insentience"), all philosophical positions ("duality," "difference," "nonduality") are only the Supreme Lord Whose body is Light. Even the relationship between means and goal is only the Light. Within this supreme nonduality, what else could there be?

## 2. Key Verses

**TĀ 2/15** (lines 2438-2446): *kiṁ ca yāvad idaṁ bāhyam āntaropāyasaṁmatam | tatprakāśātmatāmātraṁ Śivasyaiva nijaṁ vapuḥ*
All external and inner means are just the Light of consciousness — Śiva's own body.

**TĀ 2/16-17** (lines 2492-2506): *nīlaṁ pītaṁ sukham iti prakāśaḥ kevalaḥ śivaḥ | amuṣmin paramādvaite prakāśātmani ko 'paraḥ | upāyopeyabhāvaḥ syāt prakāśaḥ kevalaṁ hi saḥ*
Blue, yellow, pleasure — only the Light, Śiva. Within this supreme nonduality, what else could there be? Even the means-goal relationship is only the Light.

**TĀ 2/17cd-18ab** (lines 2521-2551): *idaṁ dvaitam ayaṁ bheda idam advaitam ity api | prakāśavapur evāyaṁ bhāsate parameśvaraḥ*
"This is duality," "this is difference," "this is nonduality" — all are the Supreme Lord whose body is Light.

**TĀ 2/18cd-19ab** (lines 2573-2582): *asyāṁ bhūmau sukhaṁ duḥkhaṁ bandho mokṣaś citir jaḍaḥ | ghaṭakumbhavad ekārthāḥ śabdās te 'py ekam eva ca*
On this plane, "pleasure" and "pain," "bondage" and "liberation," "sentience" and "insentience" are synonymous — all are the one reality.

**TĀ 2/19-21** (lines 2697-2714): *prakāśe 'py aprakāśāṁśaḥ kathaṁ nāma prakāśatām...*
How can what is not light shine in the Light? If it shines within it, distinctions cease. If it does not shine, how can it exist?

**TĀ 2/22** (lines 2966-2979): *prakāśamātram uditam aprakāśaniṣedhanāt | ekaśabdasya na tv arthaḥ saṁkhyā cidvyaktibhedabhāk*
Only the Light exists — because all that is not light is denied. "One" is not a number; it denies any counter-correlate.

## 3. Jayaratha's Commentary

**On the nonduality of all phenomena** (lines 2801-2819): Differences between manifestations are adventitious adjuncts (upādhi). Unlike Advaita Vedānta which sees multiplicity as illusory, Trika affirms that manifestations are real as products of consciousness's creative freedom.

**On the meaning of artha** (lines 2628-2649): "The word 'artha' means both 'meaning' and 'thing'... All 'things' are ultimately the same 'reality', all words are ultimately the one Word. The pure consciousness beyond the veil of thought resonates with the power of its reflective awareness, which is the highest form of Speech.

## 5. Philosophical Context

This teaching is part of Abhinavagupta's systematic exposition of the Supreme Means (Śāmbhavopāya) in Chapter 3 of the Tantrāloka. It integrates the Doctrine of Reflection with the alphabet mysticism of the Tantric traditions, showing how the structures of language embody the structures of consciousness.

## 6. Visual Concepts

- **The mirror of consciousness** with all phenomena reflected within it
- **The fifty letters** arranged as a maṇḍala of energies
- **AHAṀ** as the encompassing cycle of self-awareness
- **The five-fold bindu** as the pentad of divine activities
- **The four levels of speech** from transcendent silence to uttered sound
