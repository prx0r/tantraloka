## Central Thesis
The Ten States (daśadaśā), Ten Abodes (daśādhāra), and Ten Levels of Unstruck Sound (daśānāhatadhvanayas) form a threefold map of the yogi's ascent through the dvādaśānta. The states are levels of subjective awareness, the abodes are the corresponding locations in the subtle body, and the levels of unstruck sound are the auditory manifestations of consciousness at each stage.

## Script Outline

### 1. The Threefold Map of Ascent (0:00–2:00)
- The ten voids, states, abodes, and sounds are parallel frameworks — lines 372–378
- Each framework describes the same ascent but from a different angle:
  - Voids = what is dissolved
  - States = what is experienced
  - Abodes = where in the body
  - Sound = how it resonates in consciousness
- The number ten corresponds to the ten stages of the dvādaśānta ascent

### 2. The Ten States (Daśadaśā) (2:00–4:00)
- Verses 93cd–94 — pages 120–123
- Each state is a level of subjective awareness on the ascent
- From the state of deep sleep (śūnyapramātṛ) through waking to turīya
- The states mark progressive recognition of oneself as consciousness

### 3. The Ten Abodes (Daśādhāra) (4:00–6:00)
- Verses 95–96ab — pages 123–128
- The abodes are locations in the subtle body along the dvādaśānta axis
- From the Heart centre through the throat, palate, forehead, crown, to the space above
- Each abode is a "place" where consciousness dwells and from which it operates
- Not to be confused with the six cakras of Haṭha Yoga — these are unique to the Trika system

### 4. Unstruck Sound: Anāhata Dhvani (6:00–8:00)
- The breath resounds with HAṀSAḤ (or SO'HAṀ) — lines 3705–3724
- This is the unstruck sound (anāhata) — the sound that is not produced by striking
- The ten levels of unstruck sound in verses 98–100 — pages 128–133
- Each level is a progressively subtler resonance of consciousness
- The grossest level = audible breath; the subtlest = the silence beyond sound

### 5. The Ten Levels Detailed (8:00–9:30)
- Level 1: the audible sound of breathing
- Level 2: the inner resonance of HAṀ
- Level 3: the inner resonance of SAḤ
- Levels 4–10: increasingly subtle vibrations of consciousness
- The tenth level: the unstruck sound that is no sound at all — pure conscious silence
- "The practice consists of carefully listening to the sound of the breath" — lines 3720–3724

### 6. Conclusion (9:30–10:00)
- States, abodes, and sounds are three perspectives on the same reality
- The ascent is not through space but through levels of self-recognition
- The unstruck sound is the audible form of one's own consciousness

## Visual Suggestions
