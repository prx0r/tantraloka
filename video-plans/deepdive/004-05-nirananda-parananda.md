# 004-05: Nirānanda and Parānanda — Exhalation and Inhalation Bliss

- **Duration:** 10:00
- **Difficulty:** Advanced

- **Core Texts:** Tantrāloka 5.44–46ab; Viveka; Swami Lakshmanjoo's commentary

## Key Verses (with line numbers)

- TĀ 5.45 — lines 3458–3461: Parānanda from the object of knowledge
- TĀ 5.46ab — line 3461: "Adorned with the Moon of the downward moving breath"

- Commentary on Parānanda — lines 3690–3756, 4157–4168

## Central Thesis
Nirānanda (Bliss of Stillness) and Parānanda (Bliss of the Other) are the first two developed stages of bliss, arising from exhalation (prāṇa, recaka) and inhalation (apāna, pūraka) respectively. They establish the fundamental polarity — subject and object, means of knowledge and object of knowledge — that all higher blisses transcend and unite.

## Script Outline

### 1. The Two Poles (0:00–2:00)
- Nirānanda = prāṇa, the means of knowledge (pramāṇa) — lines 3678–3688
- Parānanda = apāna, the object of knowledge (prameya) — lines 3690–3696
- The breath creates the subject-object split at every cycle — lines 3680–3683
- HAṀ (outbreath) and SAḤ (inbreath) as the mantra of the breath — lines 3705–3708

### 2. Nirānanda in Detail (2:00–4:30)
- "Emerged from bliss" — nir-ānanda = the stillness that arises from innate bliss — lines 3726–3739
- The exhaled breath rises from Heart to End of Twelve — lines 3680–3683
- Swami Lakshmanjoo: "unfathomably deep emptiness" with alert awareness — lines 4136–4156
- The breath becomes extremely subtle, as if ceasing — lines 4144–4147

### 3. Parānanda in Detail (4:30–7:00)
- "Bliss brought about by another" — the object of knowledge — lines 3568–3570
- The inhaled breath descends from End of Twelve to Heart — lines 3692–3696, 3755–3756
- "Satisfied by drinking the plenitude of the infinite aspects of objectivity" — lines 3746–3751
- Swami Lakshmanjoo: frightening sounds and forms, choking sensation — lines 4305–4328
- The yogi must persevere with mantra and devotion — lines 4315–4328

### 4. The Unity of the Pair (7:00–9:00)
- These two blisses are the first movement beyond individual subjectivity
- They establish the dynamics later consumed in higher blisses — lines 3781–3820
- The Moon of apāna nourishes; the Sun of prāṇa illumines — lines 3751–3756
- Recognising the breath as Śiva's power of emission (visarga) — lines 3705–3708

### 5. Conclusion (9:00–10:00)
- Nirānanda and Parānanda teach the yogi to experience breath as consciousness
- The polarity of exhale/inhale is the basic pulse of the universal spanda
- Mastery of these two opens the door to the higher blisses
