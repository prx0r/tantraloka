# The Four Cosmic Eggs (Aṇḍa)
## 10-Minute Deep-Dive Video Essay

---

### Core Teaching

The Cosmic Egg (brahmāṇḍa) is not merely a cosmological concept in the Tantrāloka — it is an "enveloping covering" (āvaraṇa) that simultaneously obscures and structures reality. Abhinavagupta, following the *Mālinīvijayottara* (2/49), teaches four Eggs nested within each other, each corresponding to a domain of the Forces (kalā). Only the first Egg (Earth) offers resistance to penetration (*apratigha*); the higher Eggs are increasingly subtle, culminating in the Śiva principle which has no Egg at all, being utterly transparent.

---

### Key Verses

#### Verse 11.13cd-14ab — No Egg in the Śiva Principle

**Lines:** 1438–1473

> yady api prāk śivākhye 'pi tattve bhuvanapaddhatiḥ || 13 ||
> uktā tathāpy apratighe nāsminn āvṛtisaṁbhavaḥ |

**Dyczkowski Translation:**
"...even though a series of world-orders was previously said to be present in the principle called Śiva, it offers no resistance to penetration (apratigha), and so no enveloping covering is possible within it."

**Explanation:**
The Śiva principle contains world-orders (those of Vyāpinī, Samanā, etc.) but does so without any obscuring covering. It is *apratigha* — offering no resistance — because it is formless (amūrta) and void of all phenomenal characteristics. An Egg, by definition, requires a degree of opacity; where there is perfect transparency, no Egg can exist.

---

#### Verses 11.14cd-18 — The Objection and the Teachers' Response

**Lines:** 1506–1516

> nanv evaṁ dharaṇīṁ muktvā śaktau prakṛtimāyayoḥ || 14 ||
> api cāpratighatve 'pi katham aṇḍasya saṁbhavaḥ |
> atrāsmad guravaḥ prāhur yat pṛthivyādipañcakam || 15 ||
> pratyakṣam idam ābhāti tato 'nyan nāsti kiñcana |
> meyatve sthūlasūkṣmatvān mānatve karaṇatvataḥ || 16 ||
> kartṛtollāsataḥ kartṛbhāve sphuṭatayoditam |
> triṁśattattvaṁ vibhedātma tadabhedo niśā matā || 17 ||
> kāryatvakaraṇatvādivibhāgagalane sati |
> vikāsotkasvatantratve śivāntaṁ pañcakaṁ jaguḥ || 18 ||

**Dyczkowski Translation:**
"(Someone may object) that none of the Principles (that correspond to the four cosmic Eggs) except Earth, namely, Nature, Māyā and Power, offer resistance to penetration. So how is it possible for a Cosmic Egg to be within them? (14cd-15ab) Our teachers say (with regard to this objection) that apart from the group of five (gross elements), that is, Earth and the rest, that manifest directly perceived by the senses, nothing else exists. (The elements are the source of) thirty individual (vibhedātman) Principles. (Ten are at the level of) objectivity, because they are gross and subtle. (Five function as the) senses, that are the) instruments (of cognition,) and so are at the level of the means of knowledge. (The remaining fifteen) correspond to the state of the agent..."

**Explanation:**
The objection is acute: only Earth offers physical resistance — how can Nature, Māyā, or Power have an "enveloping covering"? The teachers' response is radical: the five gross elements alone are directly perceptible; everything else (30 tattvas) is derived from them through the threefold structure of object (prameya), means (pramāṇa), and subject (pramātṛ). The Egg is thus a phenomenological principle, not a physical one. It is the "covering" of ignorance that makes something appear as object rather than as consciousness.

### Puruṣa as Egg and the Fourfold Schema

Jayaratha explains that Puruṣa is identified with the Egg because, like an egg, it encloses and delimits. The four Eggs are:
1. **Pārthiva-aṇḍa** — Egg of Earth (1 principle: Earth)
2. **Prākṛta-aṇḍa** — Egg of Nature (24 principles: Water to Prakṛti)
3. **Māyīya-aṇḍa** — Egg of Māyā (7 principles: Puruṣa to Māyā)
4. **Śākta-aṇḍa** — Egg of Power (4 principles: Śuddhavidyā to Śakti)

Beyond these is the Śiva principle, which is Eggless.

### Narrative Arc

**Opening (0:00-1:30):** Why "Egg"? The cosmology of envelopment in Indian tradition. Brahmāṇḍa as the universe-egg.

**Segment 1 (1:30-3:30):** The four Eggs mapped onto the kalās and tattvas. Each Egg is a domain of experience, not a physical sphere.

**Segment 2 (3:30-5:30):** The objection and the radical reply — nothing exists but the five elements. All 36 tattvas are phenomenological derivatives.

**Segment 3 (5:30-7:30):** The Heart contracts itself into Eggs. PTv passage: "The Supreme Lord breaks through the invisible integument which is ignorance."

**Segment 4 (7:30-9:30):** Egglessness of Śiva. Transparency vs. opacity in consciousness. The initiate's journey through the Eggs.

**Conclusion (9:30-10:00):** The Eggs are not places but states of contraction. Liberation is the breaking of all shells — the return to the Eggless Heart.
