## Central Thesis
"Tying the Topknot" (sikthabandha) is the practice of gathering all the dispersed energies of the breath into a single point of focused awareness. The Sixfold Path (ṣaḍadhvan) then maps the six cosmic paths — worlds, principles, forces, phonemes, mantras, and pads — onto the breath, revealing the entire universe within each breathing cycle.

## Script Outline

### 1. Opening: The Topknot of the Breath (0:00–2:00)
- Sikthabandha = "tying the topknot" — gathering the hair/energies together — lines 417
- The "hair" is the scattered energies of the breath
- Tying them = focusing all breath-movements into the central channel
- This practice concentrates the dispersed cosmic energies into the dvādaśānta axis

### 2. The Sixfold Path (Ṣaḍadhvan) (2:00–4:00)
- Six paths: three objective (worlds, principles, forces) and three subjective (phonemes, mantras, pads) — lines 418
- The Path of Space (bhuvanādhvan) = the cosmic geography in the breath
- The Path of Principles (tattvādhvan) = the 36 tattvas in the breath
- The Path of Forces (kalādhvan) = the five energies in the breath
- These are the objective counterparts of the subjective paths

### 3. The Sixfold Path in Practice (4:00–6:00)
- Each of the six paths is traversed in a single breath cycle
- Exhalation: the ascent through increasing subtlety
- Inhalation: the descent through increasing density
- The yogi recognizes all six paths as dimensions of his own breath
- The paths are not concepts but direct experiences

### 4. Three Kinds of Terms (6:00–7:30)
- TĀ 6.31cd–33 — pages 250–253, lines 419
- The three terms refer to different ways of measuring time in the breath
- Short term: individual breaths
- Medium term: the daily 21,600 cycle
- Long term: yearly and cosmic cycles
- All three are present simultaneously in each breath

### 5. The Integration (7:30–9:00)
- Tying the topknot = recognizing that all six paths are one
- The three kinds of terms are the same reality at different scales
- The Breath of Consciousness (prāṇanā) is the single thread through all
- The yogi who masters this becomes "one with the Supreme Principle"

### 6. Conclusion (9:00–10:00)
- The topknot is a symbol of integration
- The scattered energies of time, space, and consciousness are gathered
- The six paths merge into the single path of the breath
- The yogi wears the universe as his topknot
