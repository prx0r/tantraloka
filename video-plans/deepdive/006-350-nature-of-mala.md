# 350 — The Nature of Mala (Impurity)
**Tantrāloka 9.62–88ab** | Chapter 9: Kāryakāraṇa (Causality)

## Hook
What is *mala* — impurity, bondage, original sin? Is it a substance, an absence, or something else entirely?

## Core Teaching
*Mala* is not a positive entity but the contraction of consciousness — a self-limitation of Śiva's infinite nature. It has no independent existence; it is the Lord's own power of self-obscuration.

## Key Verses

**9.62–68ab**: *Mala* is beginningless (*anādi*) but not eternal in the sense of being unchangeable. It is the limiting adjunct (*upādhi*) that makes the infinite soul appear finite.

**9.68cd–71ab — The Paralyzing Power**: *Mala* is "paralyzing" (*stambha*) — it makes the soul identify with the limited body-mind. But this paralysis is not absolute; it can be removed by grace (*śaktipāta*).

**9.71cd–84ab — Not a Real Obscuring Covering**: Against dualist Siddhānta, Abhinavagupta argues that *mala* is NOT a real covering that exists independently of consciousness. If it were:
- It would be a second reality beside Śiva (dualism)
- It could never be removed (eternal impurity)
- It would limit Śiva (contradiction of omnipotence)

## Three Types of Mala
1. **Āṇava mala** — the primal impurity of individuality (*aṇu* = atom), the sense of being a separate self
2. **Māyīya mala** — the impurity of objective experience arising from Māyā
3. **Kārma mala** — the impurity of karma, the residue of actions

## The Non-Dualist Reframing
In Trika non-dualism:
- *Mala* is not a substance but a misapprehension
- It is the Lord's own power of concealment (*tirodhāna*)
- It exists only as a functional limitation, not as an ontological reality
- When grace removes it, it disappears like a mistaken snake in a rope

## The Paralyzing Power
*Mala* "paralyzes" consciousness by:
1. Contracting infinite awareness to a finite point
2. Creating the sense of "I am this body"
3. Generating attachment to limited objects
4. Veiling the truth of one's own divinity

## Grace as the Solution
Only *śaktipāta* (descent of grace) can remove *mala*. No amount of effort can remove it, because the effort itself is part of the contraction. Grace is Śiva's own freedom re-asserting itself.

## Connection to Karma (9.88cd–141)
*Mala* generates karma, and karma reinforces *mala*. They form a recursive loop that keeps the soul bound. Breaking this loop requires an intervention from beyond — grace.
