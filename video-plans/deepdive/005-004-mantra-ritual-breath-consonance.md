# Deep-Dive: Mantra and Ritual in Consonance with the Breath

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 7, verses 38cd–40  
**Catalog Entry:** #285  
**Lines:** 2679–2797

---

## Topic Overview

After the extensive philosophical excursus on perception and time, Abhinava returns to practical application. The teaching is simple and direct: all ritual action — mantra repetition (japa), fire sacrifice (homa), worship (arcana) — must be performed in consonance with the breath. Without this alignment, no practice succeeds, no matter how many times a mantra is recited.

---

## Key Verses

### Verses 38cd–39ab (lines 2688–2695)
> yathā karṇau nartayāmīty evaṁ yatnāt tathā bhavet
> cakracāragatād yatnāt tadvat taccakraiva dhīḥ
> *"Just as by repeated effort to move the ears, they eventually do so, in the same way, by exercising the exertion applied to the movement of a cycle (of the breath and Mantra), the contemplative mind is present there within that cycle."*

### Verse 39cd (lines 2730–2746)
> japahomārcanādīnāṁ prāṇasāmyam ato vidhiḥ
> *"Therefore, the prescribed procedure is that the repetition of Mantra, the fire sacrifice, worship and the rest should be performed in consonance with the breath."*

### Verse 40 (lines 2762–2767)
> siddhāmate kuṇḍalinīśaktiḥ prāṇasamonmanā
> uktaṁ ca yoginīkaule tad etat parameśinā
> *"According to the Siddhāmata, when the energy of Kuṇḍalinī is in consonance with the vital breath, it is the Transmental (unmanā). The Lord has also said that in the Yoginīkaula."*

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **The Ear-Moving Analogy:** Just as one can learn to wiggle one's ears through repeated effort and intention (even though it seems impossible initially), the yogi can learn to fix the contemplative mind on the cycle of breath and mantra through sustained practice.

2. **Universal Applicability:** The rule applies to all ritual actions — japa, homa, arcana, etc. — because all involve mantra utterance, and all mantra must follow the breath.

3. **Kuṇḍalinī and Consonance:** When the energy of Kuṇḍalinī (the power of consciousness expressing through sound) is in consonance with the breath, it becomes unmanā — the Transmental state beyond mind, which is at one with Śiva.

4. **No Success Without Alignment:** A mantra recited even millions of times without breath-consonance yields no fruit. This is a radical claim — it prioritizes alignment over quantity.

---

## Scriptural Authorities

Abhinava cites two authoritative Tantras:
- **Siddhāmata (Siddhayogesvarīmata):** Establishes the principle of Kuṇḍalinī-breath consonance
- **Yoginīkaula:** The Lord (Parameśa) teaches the same

Citation from the Siddhāmata (lines 2777–2782):
> *"One should make the Mantra which is to be repeated consonant with the vital breath. So too, make the fire sacrifice consonant with the vital breath. In this way, Śiva's power, who is Mind Beyond Mind and is called Kuṇḍalā, is consonant with the breath."*
