# Deep-Dive: The Path of Space in the Body and Pervasive Consciousness

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 2–8  
**Catalog Entry:** #290  
**Lines:** 4129–4568

---

## Topic Overview

Chapter 8 begins the exposition of the Path of Space (deśādhvan), also called the Path of Worlds (bhuvanādhvan). Abhinava immediately establishes that the entire cosmic geography is not separate from consciousness — it is established within consciousness alone (cinmātra). The yogi who contemplates the layout of the cosmos attains Bhairava's state quickly.

---

## Key Verses

### Verse 1 (lines 4129–4140)
> atha deśādhvaṁ vakṣyāmi mūrtivaicitryajaṁ param
> *"Now I will expound the Path of Space, born from the wonderful variety of forms."*

### Verse 3 (lines 4274–4284)
> yat tatra nahi viśrāntaṁ tan nabhaḥ kuṣumāyate
> *"All this Path is established in consciousness alone, for what does not rest there is as unreal as a sky-flower."*

### Verse 4 (lines 4297–4347)
> saṁviddvāreṇa tatsṛṣṭe śūnye dhiyi marutsu ca
> nāḍīcakrānucakreṣu bahirdehe 'dhvasaṁsthitiḥ
> *"Through and mediated by consciousness, the Path is established in that which it has emitted: in the void, in the intellect, in the vital breaths, in the configurations of the channels, in the secondary wheels of the senses, and in the external body."*

### Verse 5 (lines 4371–4421)
> tatrādhvaivaṁ nirūpyo 'yaṁ yatas tat prakriyākramam
> anusandadhad eva drāg yogī bhairavatāṁ vrajet
> *"This Path merits description because the yogi who contemplates and integrates the layout of the cosmos quickly attains Bhairava's state."*

### Verse 6 (lines 4454–4462) — Citation from Spandaśāsana/Spandakārikā
> didṛkṣayā sarvārthān yadā vyāpyāvatiṣṭhate
> tadā kiṁ bahunoktena ity uktaṁ spandaśāsane
> *"It is said in the Spanda teaching: 'When the Yogi's consciousness pervades all things by his desire to perceive, then why speak much? — He will experience it for himself.'"*

### Verses 7–8 (lines 4526–4543)
> jñātvā samastam adhvānaṁ tad īśeṣu vilāpayet
> tān dehaprāṇadhīcakre pūrvavad gālayet kramāt
> tat samastaṁ svasaṁvittau sā saṁvid bharitātmikā
> upāsyamānā saṁsārasāgarapralayānalaḥ
> *"Once he has known the entire Path within his consciousness, the yogi should dissolve it into the presiding deities. As before, he should merge them into the wheel of the body, the vital breath and the intellect. Finally, all that should be merged into his own consciousness — which, full of all things and the object of constant worship, is the fire of universal destruction drying up the ocean of transmigration."*

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Cinmātra — Pure Consciousness Alone:** The Path is not separate from consciousness. What does not rest in consciousness is like a sky-flower — utterly unreal.

2. **Six-Fold Path in the Body:** The Path is established in six locations corresponding to states of consciousness: void (deep sleep), intellect (dream/waking), vital breaths, nāḍī channels, sense-wheels, and external body.

3. **Prakriyā — Cosmic Layout:** The systematic deployment of the worlds is the prakriyā. Contemplating this layout (the "how" of cosmic emanation) leads directly to Bhairava's state.

4. **The Spanda Teaching:** Abhinava cites Spandakārikā 3/11 — the yogi who pervades all things by the mere desire to perceive them experiences reality directly, without further teaching.

5. **Dissolution Method (Vilāpana):** The yogi dissolves the Path progressively — worlds into their Lords, Lords into the body, body into the breath, breath into the intellect, intellect into the void, void into consciousness.
