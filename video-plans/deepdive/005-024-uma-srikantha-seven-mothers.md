# Deep-Dive: The Worlds of Umā and Śrīkaṇṭha — Umā's Seven Forms as the Seven Mothers

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 238cd–244  
**Catalog Entries:** #332–333  
**Lines:** 16401–17000+

---

## Topic Overview

At the apex of the subtle body's ascent, the yogi encounters the worlds of Umā (the Goddess) and Śrīkaṇṭha (Śiva as "Beautiful-Throated"). Umā manifests in seven forms as the Seven Mothers (Saptamātṛkā) — the powers of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, Īśvara, Sadāśiva, and their transcendent source.

---

## Key Verses

### The Worlds of Umā and Śrīkaṇṭha (verses 238cd–241)
Śrīkaṇṭha's world is located above the intellect and below the supreme Śiva sphere. It is the world where the pure individuality (śuddha ahaṃ) resides. Umā's world is the location of divine power (śakti), inseparable from Śrīkaṇṭha.

### Umā's Seven Forms as the Seven Mothers (verses 242–244)
The Saptamātṛkās are:
1. **Brāhmī** — the power of Brahmā (creation)
2. **Māheśvarī** — the power of Maheśvara/Rudra (withdrawal)
3. **Kaumārī** — the power of Kumāra/Skanda (youthful vigor)
4. **Vaiṣṇavī** — the power of Viṣṇu (sustenance)
5. **Vārāhī** — the power of Varāha (the boar incarnation)
6. **Indrāṇī** — the power of Indra (sovereignty)
7. **Cāmuṇḍā** — the terrifying power that transcends all

Each Mother represents a specific divine function, and Umā is their collective source.

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Śrīkaṇṭha — Śiva's Grace-Headed Form:** Śrīkaṇṭha (Beautiful-Throated) is the form of Śiva who carries the poison (halāhala) in his throat, symbolizing the power to digest all duality.

2. **Umā as Śakti:** Umā is not separate from Śrīkaṇṭha — she is his power of self-awareness and activity.

3. **Seven Mothers as Universal Powers:** The Mātṛkās govern the seven levels of consciousness — from gross creation to transcendent terror (Cāmuṇḍā).

4. **The Mothers in the Body:** These goddesses reside within the yogi's body, activated through appropriate meditation and nyāsa.
