# Deep-Dive: The Thirty-Five Hells (Narakas)

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol. 5, Chapter 8, verses 24cd–30ab  
**Catalog Entry:** #296  
**Lines:** 6001–6500+

---

## Topic Overview

Within the lower half of the Egg of Brahmā, above Kālāgni, lie the thirty-five hells (naraka). These are the realms of karmic retribution where souls experience the fruits of their evil actions. The most famous include Avīci, Kumbhīpāka, and Mahāraurava.

---

## Key Verses

### Verses 24cd–30ab (lines 6001–6200+)

The hells are arranged in progressive degrees of suffering. Key hells include:
- **Avīci** (Without Waves): Constant torment without respite
- **Kumbhīpāka** (Cooked in a Pot): Souls boiled in cauldrons
- **Mahāraurava** (Great Screaming): Intense screaming from torture
- **Raurava** (Screaming): Lesser degree of Mahāraurava
- **Tāmisra** (Darkness): Total darkness and confinement
- **Andhatāmisra** (Blinding Darkness): Even worse darkness

---

## Key Doctrinal Points

1. **Temporary Realms:** In the nondual Trika view, hells are not eternal — souls experience the fruits of karma and then move on.

2. **Karmic Consequences:** Each hell corresponds to specific types of negative karma: Avīci for the gravest offenses, Kumbhīpāka for cruelty, etc.

3. **Within the Egg:** The hells are located in the lower half of the Brahmāṇḍa, below the earth but above Kālāgni.

4. **Ascent Through Hells:** In initiation (dīkṣā), the teacher purifies the disciple by ritually ascending through these worlds, consuming their karmic residues.
