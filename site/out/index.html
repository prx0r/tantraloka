प्रकाश–विमर्श

Prakāśa–Vimarśa

The two inseparable aspects of consciousness: its self-luminous radiance (prakāśa) and its reflective self-awareness (vimarśa).

foundationconsciousnessnonduality

स्पन्द

Spanda

The subtle vibration or pulsation of consciousness — the eternal self-affirmation of 'I-I'.

dynamicsconsciousnessvibration

माया

Māyā

The power through which unlimited consciousness appears as divided subject and object.

tattvacontractionbondage

विद्या

Vidyā

The contraction of unlimited knowing into partial, conditional knowledge — the fourth of the five kañcukas.

tattvakañcukaknowledge

राग

Rāga

The contraction of infinite fullness into experienced lack and object-directed desire — the third kañcuka.

tattvakañcukadesire

पञ्च कञ्चुक

Pañca Kañcukas

The five coverings through which unlimited consciousness becomes limited: Kāla (time), Niyati (destiny), Rāga (attachment), Vidyā (limited knowledge), Kalā (limited agency).

tattvacontractionlimitation

पुरुष

Puruṣa

The contracted subject — the individual experiencer who has forgotten its identity with Śiva.

tattvasubjectcontraction
