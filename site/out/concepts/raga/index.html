राग

Rāga

rāga

You feel a subtle unease, a sense that something is missing. You check your phone. You eat something. You scroll. The unease returns because it was never about the object — it was Rāga, the contraction of fullness into the feeling of lack. Rāga is the deeply familiar ache of 'I want' that seems to come from outside but actually structures your entire subjective world.
