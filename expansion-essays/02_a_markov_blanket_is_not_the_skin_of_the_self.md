# a markov blanket is not the skin of the self

A cell membrane is visible.

A Markov blanket is not.

The membrane is made of lipids and proteins.

The blanket is made of conditional independencies.

It does not stop matter.

It describes which variables can influence which others directly once a mediating set is known.

The phrase sounds biological enough to be mistaken for anatomy.

It is mathematics first.

A Markov blanket is not the skin of the self.

---

The concept comes from probabilistic graphical models.

Choose a variable or set of variables.

Its Markov blanket is the minimal collection of other variables that makes it conditionally independent from the rest of the system.

Once the blanket states are known, information from beyond them adds nothing further to predicting the internal variable, given the model.

For a node in a Bayesian network, the blanket typically includes its parents, children, and the other parents of its children.

The definition concerns statistical dependence.

No literal fabric is required.

A blanket can surround a gene-expression variable in a graph, a set of neural states, or a simulated object.

The boundary exists relative to the variables and probability structure chosen for analysis.

---

In active-inference models, the partition acquires a biological interpretation.

States are divided into four groups:

internal states;

external states;

sensory states;

active states.

Sensory states are influenced by external states and affect internal states.

Active states are influenced by internal states and affect external states.

The blanket consists of sensory and active states.

Internal and external states do not directly influence one another once blanket states are conditioned upon.

The organism therefore meets the world through a statistical interface.

External causes change sensation.

Internal dynamics change action.

The two sides remain coupled.

The blanket does not isolate them.

It specifies the route through which relation occurs.

---

A cell offers an intuitive example, but only approximately.

The membrane carries receptors, channels, pumps, and cytoskeletal machinery.

External chemistry affects receptor and membrane states.

Internal processes alter movement, secretion, and exchange.

The physical boundary can realize some of the dependencies represented by a Markov blanket.

But membrane and blanket are not automatically identical.

The Markov partition depends upon the model, timescale, coarse-graining, and variables selected.

A molecule outside the membrane may function as part of the system’s effective regulation.

An internal state may directly couple across a physical boundary in ways a simplified graph ignores.

A statistical boundary can cut across anatomical ones.

The mathematics must be demonstrated, not inferred from visual resemblance.

---

This is the first major caution.

The success of Markov blankets in Bayesian inference does not prove that every living thing possesses one unique metaphysical boundary.

Critics distinguish the original Pearl blanket from the broader “Friston blanket” used in free-energy formulations.

A Pearl blanket is an epistemic structure in a probabilistic model.

A Friston-style blanket is asked to identify a persisting physical system and support claims about autonomy, selfhood, or cognition.

The second use requires more assumptions.

One cannot move from:

this model contains a conditional-independence partition

to:

nature itself has revealed the true edge of the organism

without an argument connecting model and world.

The blanket may be discovered.

It may also be imposed by how the scientist describes the system.

---

This criticism does not make the framework useless.

It makes the question sharper.

Under what conditions do the dynamics of a real non-equilibrium system support a stable partition that behaves like a blanket?

Sparse connectivity alone is not enough.

Studies of coupled nonequilibrium systems have found that apparent component boundaries do not automatically produce the required conditional independencies in steady-state distributions.

A living system may approximate a Markov blanket at one scale and violate it at another.

Blankets may be dynamic, graded, or temporary.

The idea becomes scientifically interesting when it can fail.

A boundary that exists by definition cannot explain why some systems achieve autonomy and others dissolve.

---

The free-energy principle adds another layer.

A living system persists in a limited range of states rather than diffusing into every possible condition.

Its internal dynamics can be described as if they encode expectations about external causes through sensory states.

Its actions change the environment and sensory stream in ways that help maintain its viable organization.

Under the mathematical assumptions, internal and active states minimize variational free energy.

This does not mean the cell consciously calculates probabilities.

The language of inference interprets the dynamics.

The system behaves as though internal states parameterize beliefs about hidden external states.

“Belief” here is formal.

It may exist without any experienced conviction.

---

The phrase “self-evidencing” captures the loop.

A living system repeatedly brings about the kinds of sensory states consistent with its continued existence.

A fish seeks water.

A bacterium moves through chemical gradients.

A body regulates temperature.

The organism creates evidence for the model embodied in its organization.

If it persistently encounters states outside the range its structure predicts and can regulate, it ceases to remain that organism.

The living form is a hypothesis continually tested against the world.

Action helps make the hypothesis true.

---

But this language can become circular.

The organism persists because it minimizes surprise.

We know it minimizes surprise because it persists.

The framework gains explanatory power only when it specifies mechanisms, variables, and predictions beyond redescribing survival.

Which internal states?

Which sensory channels?

Which action policies?

Which free-energy functional?

Which perturbation should disrupt the inferred blanket?

Formal elegance does not replace empirical identification.

A universal mathematical description can be true of many systems while explaining little about the biological differences among them.

The blanket is a tool.

It is not a finished theory of life.

---

Nested blankets make the picture more complex.

Cells form tissues.

Tissues form organs.

Organs form organisms.

Each level may support a statistical boundary not reducible to the visual boundary of its parts.

The organism is not merely the sum of cell blankets.

Higher-level variables can emerge through collective dynamics.

A heart has states no single cardiomyocyte possesses.

A community has communication patterns no individual carries alone.

Markov blankets can, in principle, be blankets of blankets.

This does not prove that every scale is equally agentic.

It gives a language for asking where conditional independence and self-maintaining dynamics stabilize.

---

A 2025 preprint proposes “Markov blanket density,” treating conditional independence as graded across space rather than all-or-nothing.

Another develops dynamic blanket detection to identify macroscopic objects in simulations by learning which microscopic elements function as internal or boundary states over time.

These proposals are early.

They are useful because real organisms exchange matter, change shape, migrate, and replace components.

A fixed binary edge may be too rigid.

The living boundary is maintained through activity.

It can move while the organization persists.

A flame has no membrane.

A cell replaces molecules.

A person extends action through tools.

Objecthood may require tracking a dynamic pattern rather than drawing one permanent line.

---

This is where metaphysical inflation becomes most tempting.

If Markov blankets define things, perhaps every blanket is a self.

If nested blankets extend through the biosphere, perhaps Earth is one mind.

If internal states infer external states, perhaps all bounded systems are conscious.

None of these conclusions follows automatically.

Conditional independence is not subjectivity.

Statistical autonomy is not necessarily biological autonomy.

Biological autonomy is not necessarily consciousness.

A pendulum system can exhibit dynamics representable through a blanket-like partition without feeling its motion.

The framework may reveal prerequisites or structural analogies.

It does not settle the hard problem by changing vocabulary.

---

The skin remains important.

It is a physical achievement through which the body regulates exchange, prevents uncontrolled mixing, hosts sensation, and marks vulnerability.

But the lived self is not coextensive with skin.

Tools can become parts of action.

Language extends memory into culture.

Other people regulate emotion and physiology.

Microbes participate in metabolism.

A Markov blanket framework can help formalize such distributed coupling.

It can also oversimplify it.

The true boundary depends upon the question.

Metabolic self.

Legal self.

Experiential self.

Immunological self.

Each may cut the world differently.

---

The precision of the boundary therefore matters more than the romance of the metaphor.

A blanket is scientifically useful when it exposes a testable dependency structure, clarifies intervention, or predicts which variables screen one another off. It becomes decorative when every interesting object is declared blanketed after the fact.

---

A cell membrane is visible.

A Markov blanket is not.

The membrane can help realize a blanket.

The blanket can help model the membrane’s role.

Confusing them makes both less precise.

The living boundary is not a mystical aura around the organism.

It is a pattern of controlled dependence.

Inside and outside continue affecting one another, but only through channels whose organization lets the system remain distinguishable.

A Markov blanket is not the skin of the self.

It is the grammar describing how a provisional inside can exist without ever becoming independent of what lies beyond it.
