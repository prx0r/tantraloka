# Expansion Essay 14 — Ibn 'Arabi: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "god is incomplete without you"
Total: 12 scenes, ~92 seconds
Palette: Deep luminous dark / Gold / Rose-gold / Mirror-silver / Theophany-light

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "What if God needs you as much as you need God?"
- Visual: Two arcs facing each other — neither complete alone. They form a circle when they meet.

**Scene 2** (8s) — "There is only one act of being — one ocean, many waves."
- Visual: One ocean surface. Many waves rising and falling. Different forms, same water.

**Scene 3** (8s) — "I created perception in thee only that I might become the object of my perception."
- Visual: An eye that looks outward. The gaze curves back and sees itself looking. The seer, the seeing, and the seen are one.

**Scene 4** (8s) — "You are the mirror in which the divine can see itself."
- Visual: A mirror reflecting a luminous source. The reflection is the mirror's gift to the source.

**Scene 5** (8s) — "God has no like, but has an image — the formless takes form in the heart."
- Visual: A heart-shaped space. Formless light begins to take shape within it — the most beautiful form.

**Scene 6** (8s) — "The perfection of contingent being is to know its own unknowingness."
- Visual: A figure holding a concept of God. The concept dissolves. The hand opens. Empty. Full.

**Scene 7** (8s) — "Perfect Man — the being in whom divine self-disclosure reaches completion."
- Visual: A human figure gradually becoming more luminous, more transparent. The divine shining through.

**Scene 8** (6s) — "An individual who is his own species — like the Angel."
- Visual: A single figure standing alone — not lonely, but complete. A category of one.

**Scene 9** (8s) — "Every scripture is a letter addressed to you personally."
- Visual: A book opens. The words rearrange themselves into a personal address. "You" is the recipient.

**Scene 10** (8s) — "God is love requires a beloved — the mirror in which love sees itself."
- Visual: Two mirrors facing each other. Infinite reflections of love reflecting love.

**Scene 11** (6s) — "You are an organ of the divine body — necessary for self-completion."
- Visual: A cosmic body missing one part. You are that part. When you arrive, the body is whole.

**Scene 12** (8s) — "The only question is whether you will know it — and complete the circle."
- Visual: The circle from scene 1 closes. The two arcs become one. The light flows through.

---

Total: 12 scenes, ~92 seconds
