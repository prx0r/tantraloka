# Expansion Essay 12 — Mundus Imaginalis: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "the world between worlds"
Total: 11 scenes, ~86 seconds
Palette: Twilight indigo / Emerald / Person of Light white-gold / Dhikr fire / Daena rose-gold

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "What if there is a world neither physical nor spiritual — but in between?"
- Visual: A crack in reality — a luminous threshold between two domains. Neither side fully visible.
- Keyword: mundus imaginalis

**Scene 2** (8s) — "Active Imagination is an organ of perception — tuned to a real world."
- Visual: An eye opening — but the eye sees inward, not outward. A world unfolds behind the eye.
- Keyword: imaginatio vera

**Scene 3** (8s) — "The suprasensory Orient — the cosmic north, where your soul came from."
- Visual: A compass needle spinning, settling on a new direction. A horizon that is also an origin.
- Keyword: al-mashriq

**Scene 4** (10s) — "The Perfect Nature — the Person of Light that has been with you since before birth."
- Visual: A luminous figure approaching — made entirely of light. No features, just radiance. The shakhs nurani.
- Keyword: shakhs nurani

**Scene 5** (8s) — "The spider's thread — cut and re-joined. 1×1."
- Visual: A single glowing thread. It is cut. It is re-joined. The formula 1×1 appears. Not 1=1, not n+1.
- Keyword: unus-ambo

**Scene 6** (8s) — "Your face becomes pure and radiates light. Then another Face appears — made of light."
- Visual: A face becoming luminous. Then another face — identical, radiant — appears facing it. The two faces recognize each other.
- Keyword: shakhs nurani

**Scene 7** (8s) — "The dhikr — fire that burns away everything between you and the Person of Light."
- Visual: A flame that rises from within. It burns obstacles — threads, veils, shadows. The path clears.
- Keyword: dhikr

**Scene 8** (8s) — "Light upon light — when your light meets its light, they combine."
- Visual: Two points of light approaching each other. They touch. Instead of merging into one, they become brighter together. Light upon light.
- Keyword: nurun 'ala nur

**Scene 9** (8s) — "The Daena at the Chinvat Bridge — 'Who art thou?' 'I am your own celestial counterpart.'"
- Visual: A bridge of light. Two figures meet at its center. One asks; the other answers. The answer is recognition.
- Keyword: daena

**Scene 10** (6s) — "The Guide is both parent and child. You create each other."
- Visual: Two figures facing each other. A thread of light connects them at the heart. Each gives birth to the other — the relationship is mutual, recursive.
- Keyword: syzygy

**Scene 11** (8s) — "The door is always open. The Guide is always waiting."
- Visual: A door of light standing open. Beyond it: the imaginal world, luminous and vast. The Guide stands at the threshold — waiting, patient.
- Keyword: janua

---
