# Expansion Essay 16 — Yoga Vasistha: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "the dream that you are"
Total: 12 scenes, ~92 seconds
Palette: Surreal dream tones — lavender, rose-gold, dream-blue, silver, warm dark

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "What if you are dreaming right now?"
- Visual: A room that subtly ripples — edges soften, colors bleed. Nothing is solid.
- Keyword: svapna

**Scene 2** (8s) — "What is this world? Who am I? What is bondage?"
- Visual: A figure questioning its own outline. The edges of the self dissolve and reform.
- Keyword: vairāgya

**Scene 3** (10s) — "Stories within stories, nested like Russian dolls."
- Visual: Nested frames — each frame contains a smaller scene. A figure in the outermost frame, a smaller figure in the next, and so on.
- Keyword: upāya

**Scene 4** (8s) — "A queen visited another universe through the power of her mind."
- Visual: A figure steps through a crack in reality into another world — different colors, different sky.
- Keyword: dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi

**Scene 5** (8s) — "A king dreamed he was a beggar, a beggar dreamed he was a king."
- Visual: Two silhouettes facing each other. They exchange places — king becomes beggar, beggar becomes king. Neither is real.
- Keyword: bhrānti

**Scene 6** (8s) — "The world is a long dream. There is no world apart from the mind."
- Visual: A landscape that begins to shimmer and fade. The world is seen (dṛṣṭi) and thereby created (sṛṣṭi).
- Keyword: dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi

**Scene 7** (8s) — "Seven stages of wisdom — the ascent from earth to Śiva."
- Visual: A seven-step ladder ascending. Each step a different color, each labeled. The fourth step glows — the critical threshold.
- Keyword: sapta bhūmika

**Scene 8** (8s) — "The mind alone is the universe. When the mind is still, the world ceases."
- Visual: A world-form that dissolves as the mind (shown as a ripple) calms to stillness.
- Keyword: citta

**Scene 9** (8s) — "The jīvanmukta — a lamp that continues to flicker after the oil is gone."
- Visual: A lamp that burns without fuel. The flame is steady, clear, but nothing feeds it.
- Keyword: jīvanmukta

**Scene 10** (8s) — "The breath is the handle of the mind. Two branches of the same tree."
- Visual: A tree with two branches — one labeled prāṇa, one labeled citta. Grasp one, the other is caught.
- Keyword: prāṇa-samrodha

**Scene 11** (8s) — "Ajāti-vāda — no creation, no destruction. Neither the dream nor the waking."
- Visual: A field where nothing arises and nothing passes away. Pure presence.
- Keyword: ajāti

**Scene 12** (6s) — "You are the dreamer. You have always been the dreamer."
- Visual: A dreamer figure at center. The dream-world around them becomes transparent. They recognize themselves as the source.
- Keyword: jīvanmukti

---

Total: 12 scenes, ~92 seconds
Palette core: Lavender / Rose-gold / Dream-blue / Silver / Warm dark
