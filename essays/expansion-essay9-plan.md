# Expansion Essay 9 — Daimon: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "the angel you've been ignoring"
Total: 12 scenes, ~90 seconds

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "There is something with you that has always been with you."
- Visual: A subtle luminous presence beside or behind a human-like form. Always there, never noticed.
- Keyword: daimōn

**Scene 2** (8s) — "Assigned at birth — your life is a shared project."
- Visual: A birth moment — a point of light enters a new form. The daimon and the child, together from the first breath.
- Keyword: synamphoteros

**Scene 3** (8s) — "The daimon is a function — the bridge between divine and human."
- Visual: Two realms (above light, below dark). A luminous bridge/thread connects them. The daimon is the bridge.
- Keyword: mesitēs

**Scene 4** (8s) — "Plotinus: the undescended part of your own soul."
- Visual: A figure with a luminous upper half that remained above while the lower half descended into body. The undescended part.
- Keyword: ameres psychē

**Scene 5** (8s) — "Iamblichus: a superior being requiring ritual."
- Visual: A luminous being standing apart. Steps/ritual preparations needed to approach it. The gap between human and daimon.
- Keyword: theourgia

**Scene 6** (6s) — "Proclus: both are right — at different levels."
- Visual: A vertical hierarchy — daimon as middle term between One and matter. The complete chain of being.
- Keyword: taxis

**Scene 7** (8s) — "Corbin: the Man of Light — a being of pure luminosity."
- Visual: A silhouette made entirely of light — no features, just radiance. The celestial Witness.
- Keyword: shahid

**Scene 8** (6s) — "Ficino's music — planetary songs attuned the soul."
- Visual: A lyre/lute with glowing strings. Planetary symbols around it. Sound waves traveling upward.
- Keyword: harmonia

**Scene 9** (8s) — "The PGM systasis — five stages of preparation."
- Visual: Five sequential chambers/gates. Each a different color, each labeled: skinning, purifying, charging, inflaming, communion.
- Keyword: systasis

**Scene 10** (6s) — "Acher's four-stage cyclical path."
- Visual: A circle divided into four quadrants: trust, joy, darkness, encounter. A point moving through them in sequence.
- Keyword: kyklos

**Scene 11** (8s) — "The daimon also wants the meeting."
- Visual: Two forms facing each other across a divide. Both reaching. Not just human reaching for daimon — both reaching.
- Keyword: synantēsis

**Scene 12** (10s) — "The voice that said 'look up' — that was it. The meeting can happen now."
- Visual: A moment of sudden intuition — light breaking through. The companion, finally seen. A face of light turning toward you.
- Keyword: epistrophē

---

Total: 12 scenes, ~90 seconds
