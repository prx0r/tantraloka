# Expansion Essay 1 — Spanda: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "the engine of consciousness"
Total: 17 scenes, ~129 seconds (~2 min)

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "There is a pulse happening in you right now that is not your heartbeat"
- Visual: Slow gold pulse ring expanding from center. A familiar heartbeat/EKG shape appears and fades — contrast shows spanda is NOT the heart.
- Text on field: "not your heartbeat"

**Scene 2** (5s) — "Most people never notice it. They called it Spanda."
- Visual: The pulse ring continues, now labeled. The word SPANDA appears in Devanāgarī (स्पन्द) and transliteration.
- Text on field: "spanda — the hidden pulse"

**Scene 3** (8s) — "yasyonmeṣa-nimeṣābhyām — expansion is arising, contraction dissolution"
- Visual: Concentric rings expand outward (arising of the world) then contract back (dissolution). The cycle repeats. Sanskrit verse on field.
- Keyword: unmeṣa (opening) / nimeṣa (closing)

**Scene 4** (6s) — "A wheel. Hub = awareness. Spokes = will, cognition, action."
- Visual: A wheel with hub and 3 main spokes labeled icchā (will), jñāna (cognition), kriyā (action). The hub is labeled "awareness." The wheel rotates slowly.
- Keyword: śakti-cakra

**Scene 5** (8s) — "Kallata: your own true nature gives sovereignty over this wheel"
- Visual: The same wheel, but now the viewer is placed at the hub. First-person perspective looking out along the spokes. The spokes radiate from "you."
- Keyword: svātantrya

**Scene 6** (10s) — "Six names: prāṇanā, spanda, sphurattā, viśrānti, jīva, hṛdaya"
- Visual: Six concentric rings, each a different color, each labeled with its Sanskrit name. Each ring has a slightly different motion quality (one vibrates quickly, one slowly, one shimmers, etc.)
- Keyword: ṣaḍ-nāma

**Scene 7** (6s) — "When a word arrives before you speak it — that pulse"
- Visual: A Devanāgarī syllable that shimmers into existence BEFORE a mouth/voice appears. The gap between intention and articulation made visible.
- Keyword: anāgatā vāk

**Scene 8** (8s) — "Say a word out loud. Feel the breath. Mantra is the pulse shaped into sound."
- Visual: A breath wave (sine) rises. At its crest, a Sanskrit syllable crystallizes (हं / सौः / ॐ). The syllable and the wave are the same form — one continuous movement.
- Keyword: mantra-spanda

**Scene 9** (8s) — "Mantra works because it IS the pulse — riding the breath"
- Visual: The breath wave from scene 8 transforms into a pulse ring seen from above. Same motion, different angle. Shows identity between breath-mantra-pulse.
- Keyword: spandanātmatā

**Scene 10** (8s) — "Every perception a pulse — unmeṣa opening, nimeṣa closing"
- Visual: A field of circles that open (revealing a point of light) and close in sequence. The open-close wave propagates across the field. Labels: unmeṣa / nimeṣa.
- Keyword: unmeṣa-nimeṣa

**Scene 11** (8s) — "Tuning fork resonance — mantra meets spanda by resonance"
- Visual: Two tuning forks. One vibrates. The other begins to vibrate without physical contact — a sympathetic wave passes between them. "Your voice makes a glass tremble without touching it."
- Keyword: anuranana

**Scene 12** (10s) — "The fish-belly throb — the whole body one single pulse"
- Visual: Full-frame pulsation. A fish silhouette throbs, pulled out of water (shown as drips/ripples falling from it). Every part alive in the same rhythm. Raw, shaking.
- Keyword: matsyodara-spanda

**Scene 13** (8s) — "Time → Breath → Spanda → Void → Consciousness"
- Visual: Five vertical layers labeled kāla, prāṇa, spanda, śūnya, citi. A pulse-point travels through them from top to bottom. Each layer glows when the pulse passes through it.
- Keyword: krama

**Scene 14** (6s) — "Every pulse is real. The universe is a drum."
- Visual: Ten small pulsing points scattered across the field, each at its own rhythm. They're all happening simultaneously, independent but connected. Text: "a drum that has never stopped."
- Keyword: bherī

**Scene 15** (8s) — "All pulses are waves of one ocean that never moves"
- Visual: Multiple wave forms rising from a single smooth horizontal surface (the ocean). The surface remains completely still while waves rise and fall from it. Text: "the one ocean that never moves."
- Keyword: sāmānyaspanda

**Scene 16** (10s) — "Krīḍā — play is vibration seeking out joy. A king pretends to be a soldier."
- Visual: A wheel that dances — asymmetrical, joyful, not mechanical. A small crowned figure transforms into an unadorned figure — same person, two roles. The whole scene is light, playful.
- Keyword: krīḍā

**Scene 17** (10s) — "When you know yourself as the pulse, desire becomes creative power"
- Visual: A radiant center. A gold orb (sun) and a silver orb (moon) rise from it on opposite sides. Everything emanates from one point. Text: "the sixth bliss — complete expansion — pūrṇavikāsa."
- Keyword: pūrṇavikāsa

---

Total: 17 scenes, ~129 seconds
