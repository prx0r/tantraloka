# Expansion Essay 13 — Alchemy: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "the secret life of matter"
Total: 10 scenes, ~78 seconds
Palette: Furnace dark / Molten gold / Quicksilver / Emerald / Flame

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "The alchemists were trying to turn matter into light."
- Visual: Lead crumbling, then glowing from within. Matter becoming luminous.

**Scene 2** (8s) — "The Emerald Tablet — that which is below is like that which is above."
- Visual: Emerald text on a dark ground. Above and below mirroring each other.

**Scene 3** (8s) — "Virgin Mercury — yields to nothing but love."

**Scene 4** (8s) — "Body, Soul, and Spirit — one thing, equally present."
- Visual: Three interlocking rings forming one figure. Body, Soul, Spirit all present.

**Scene 5** (8s) — "Blake's Printing House — six chambers of transformation."
- Visual: Six chambers in sequence. Each a different color. A figure passing through.

**Scene 6** (8s) — "Make Water of Earth, Earth of Water — dissolve the fixed, fix the volatile."
- Visual: Solid becoming liquid, liquid becoming solid. The rhythmic exchange.

**Scene 7** (8s) — "Without Contraries, no progression — good and evil, reason and energy."
- Visual: Two opposites facing each other. A third thing emerging between them.

**Scene 8** (8s) — "Supercelestial Marriage — soul and body reconciled."
- Visual: Two forms approaching, merging into one luminous figure.

**Scene 9** (6s) — "The gold of the sun — matter purified enough to shine."
- Visual: Dark matter becoming radiant gold. The Philosopher's Stone glowing.

**Scene 10** (8s) — "You are the furnace. The fire is already lit."
- Visual: A human silhouette containing a furnace fire within. The work is underway.
