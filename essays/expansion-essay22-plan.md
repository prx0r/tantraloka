# Expansion Essay 22 — Poimandres: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "the mind that's thinking through you"
Total: 11 scenes, ~86 seconds
Palette: Primordial white-gold / Serpent darkness / Watery teal / Star-silver

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "What if the universe is a mind?"
- Visual: A vast field of stars that are also neurons. The cosmos as a thinking being.
- Keyword: nous

**Scene 2** (8s) — "An enormous being appeared — Poimandres, mind of sovereignty."
- Visual: A vast luminous figure appearing before a small human. "I am with you everywhere."
- Keyword: Poimandres

**Scene 3** (8s) — "Everything became light — clear and joyful. Then darkness arose — coiling sinuously like a snake."
- Visual: The primordial light. Then darkness coiling from below like a serpent. Light and dark as two movements.
- Keyword: phōs / skotos

**Scene 4** (8s) — "The light-giving word who comes from mind is the son of god."
- Visual: A word of light emerging from the light-source. The Logos as a ray.
- Keyword: logos

**Scene 5** (8s) — "The mixing bowl — immerse yourself if your heart has the strength."
- Visual: A great bowl of light descending. A herald's voice. Some step in and remember.
- Keyword: krater

**Scene 6** (6s) — "A boy sees his reflection in a pond and dives in — forgets he was ever dry."
- Visual: A reflection on water. A figure dives. The water closes. The memory of air fades.
- Keyword: katabasis

**Scene 7** (8s) — "A room with a thousand mirrors — the god with all names and no name."
- Visual: Mirrors reflecting each other. Different faces. One presence.
- Keyword: panonymos

**Scene 8** (8s) — "A man climbs a tower in the dark, leaving a garment on each step."
- Visual: Seven spheres or steps. At each, a garment falls away. At the top, nothing remains.
- Keyword: anodos

**Scene 9** (6s) — "Stretch your arms — one hand touches the first morning, the other the last evening."
- Visual: A figure expanding to cosmic scale. Containing all time and space.
- Keyword: apeiros

**Scene 10** (8s) — "Sunlight on a window — made of the same light as the sun."
- Visual: Light streaming through a window. The glass realizes it is made of the same light.
- Keyword: homoiosis

**Scene 11** (8s) — "You are a thought in the mind of the universe thinking itself."
- Visual: The cosmos as a mind. A thought within it becomes self-aware. The thought recognizes itself as the thinker.
- Keyword: gnōsis

---

Total: 11 scenes, ~86 seconds
