# Expansion Essay 2 — Kañcukas: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "you're wearing armor"
Total: 17 scenes, ~126 seconds (~2 min)

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "You can only do so much. You can only know so much."
- Visual: A luminous central form gradually enclosed by five colored rings appearing one by one. Each ring is a limit being applied.
- Keyword: pariccheda (limitation)

**Scene 2** (8s) — "Five pieces of armor between you and freedom."
- Visual: The five rings fully wrapped. Each labeled: kalā, vidyā, rāga, kāla, niyati. The central light is dimmed but present.
- Keyword: kañcuka

**Scene 3** (6s) — "A knot pulls tighter the more you wrestle it."
- Visual: A knotted loop that tightens when pulled from both ends. When released with relaxed attention, it loosens one loop at a time.
- Keyword: māyāgranthi

**Scene 4** (8s) — "Kalā — limited agency. Beneath it: the power to lift anything."
- Visual: A reaching/ lifting form that meets a ceiling. The same form then lifts freely — showing what lies beneath the limit.
- Keyword: kiñcitkartṛtva

**Scene 5** (8s) — "Vidyā — limited knowledge. You only see a sliver."
- Visual: A beam of light (knowing) that narrows to a slit. Beyond the slit, the full field remains. Vidyā as the capacity to see through.
- Keyword: paricchinnajñāna

**Scene 6** (8s) — "Rāga — craving. You reach for what you lack."
- Visual: A reaching form stretching toward something just out of reach. The form feels its own shape through the stretch, through the ache.
- Keyword: abhilāṣa

**Scene 7** (8s) — "Kāla — time. You move from A to B."
- Visual: A line from A to B. A point moves along it. But a still point (the present) remains unmoved at the center.
- Keyword: krama

**Scene 8** (8s) — "Niyati — necessity. You reap what you sow."
- Visual: A chain of linked circles (cause → effect). Each link connected to the next. Beneath the chain: a hand that sows freely.
- Keyword: niyati

**Scene 9** (6s) — "An eye cannot see itself without a mirror. Vidyā is that mirror."
- Visual: An eye form facing its own reflection. In the mirror, it sees not the eye but the awareness behind it.
- Keyword: vimarśa

**Scene 10** (6s) — "The words 'just now' are a cage. The cage is made of time."
- Visual: The phrase "just now" appears. It dissolves into cage bars. The bars are made of tick marks. The cage holds nothing but the moment.
- Keyword: kālapañjara

**Scene 11** (8s) — "The fist that closes also brings blood to the palm."
- Visual: An open hand gradually closing into a fist. At the center of the closed fist, a glow appears. Contraction generates warmth.
- Keyword: saṃkoca

**Scene 12** (8s) — "The seven perceivers map onto the states you pass through every night."
- Visual: A vertical column of seven states (waking, dreaming, deep sleep, turiya, beyond). Each state shows a different configuration of the five rings.
- Keyword: saptapramātṛ

**Scene 13** (6s) — "Māyā is the night of the Lord's consciousness."
- Visual: A dark field. Stars begin to appear — not despite the dark but because of it. The turning that makes stars visible.
- Keyword: rātrī

**Scene 14** (6s) — "No two souls wear the same armor. Your limits are your signature."
- Visual: Several different configurations of the five rings, each arranged differently. Each unique. The differences are what make each configuration recognizable.
- Keyword: svakīya

**Scene 15** (8s) — "Time is a gesture consciousness makes — a slicing of the eternal into manageable pieces."
- Visual: An undivided luminous field. A slicing gesture passes through it. Segments appear — moments. The field remains undivided beneath the slicing.
- Keyword: kṣaṇa

**Scene 16** (8s) — "The body: a garment woven from the same thread as the armor."
- Visual: A body silhouette filled with the five colors woven together. The armor is not something worn — it IS the body. Karma spun into form.
- Keyword: puryaṣṭaka

**Scene 17** (8s) — "They hide the infinite. They also reveal the finite as beautiful."
- Visual: The five rings dissolve from the inside out. The central light blazes. But the rings don't disappear — they become translucent, jewel-like. Limits as the condition for beauty.
- Keyword: saundarya

---

Total: 17 scenes, ~126 seconds
