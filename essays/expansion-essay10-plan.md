# Expansion Essay 10 — Bardo: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "you died already"
Total: 15 scenes, ~118 seconds (~2 min)

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "No one remembers dying. You have died before."
- Visual: A faint outline of a figure. Multiple shadowy after-images behind it — past lives, previous deaths. A sense of repetition.
- Keyword: punarmṛtyu

**Scene 2** (8s) — "Four independent witnesses describe the same river."
- Visual: Four frames or circles, each with a symbol: Tibetan (vajra), Egyptian (ankh), Tantric (trident), Steiner (rose cross). A river flows through all four.
- Keyword: catur-āgama

**Scene 3** (10s) — "Earth dissolves — heaviness. Water — fluids dry. Fire — warmth cools. Wind — breath stops."
- Visual: Sequential element dissolution. A cube crumbling (earth). Liquid draining (water). Flames fading (fire). Air spiraling away (wind). Each in sequence.
- Keyword: bhūta-kṣaya

**Scene 4** (6s) — "Redness. Whiteness. Blackness. Then radiance."
- Visual: Three full-field colors in sequence — red field, white field, black field. Then a crack of light in the blackness.
- Keyword: raktādi

**Scene 5** (10s) — "The Clear Light dawns — subtle, clear, luminous, radiant."
- Visual: Full-field radiance brightening from center. Soft, overwhelming, peaceful. Text: "Your own true nature."
- Keyword: prabhāsvara

**Scene 6** (6s) — "The entire life unfolds in a single instant."
- Visual: A panoramic strip of images — faces, places, moments — rushing past. Not as memory, as presence.
- Keyword: jīvita-smṛti

**Scene 7** (10s) — "Forty-two peaceful deities — center and four directions."
- Visual: A luminous five-pointed mandala. Center = blue (Vairocana). East = white. South = yellow. West = red. North = green. Each a radiant form.
- Keyword: śānta-devatā

**Scene 8** (8s) — "Fifty-eight wrathful deities — crowned with skulls, drinking blood."
- Visual: The same five directions, but now the forms are darker, more intense. Flames. Weapons. Fierce expressions.
- Keyword: krodha-devatā

**Scene 9** (8s) — "Every being you encounter is a part of yourself."
- Visual: The peaceful and wrathful forms approach the center — they merge into the viewer. The distinction between self and vision dissolves.
- Keyword: svātmāvabhāsa

**Scene 10** (8s) — "The heart weighed against the feather of Ma'at."
- Visual: A scale. On one side: a heart (glowing). On the other: a feather (delicate). Forty-two small lights watch.
- Keyword: psychostasia

**Scene 11** (8s) — "Yama holds up a mirror. You judge yourself."
- Visual: A mirror surface. In it: reflections of actions, intentions, words. The viewer sees themselves seeing.
- Keyword: ādarśa

**Scene 12** (8s) — "The Twelve Kālīs consume objectivity, knowing, and subjectivity."
- Visual: Three waves of dissolution — outer objects vanishing, then thoughts/ perceptions, then the sense of self. What remains: pure awareness.
- Keyword: kalī-saṃhāra

**Scene 13** (8s) — "Six lights — six realms. The soul gravitates toward its tendency."
- Visual: Six colored orbs in a circle. Dull white, red, blue, green, yellow, smoky. A point drifts toward one of them.
- Keyword: gati

**Scene 14** (6s) — "The soul boards a boat. Crosses water. Emerges at sunrise."
- Visual: A small boat on dark water. It moves from darkness toward a horizon where light is breaking.
- Keyword: tarī

**Scene 15** (8s) — "The radiance of your own true nature. Recognize it."
- Visual: The Clear Light returns — expanded, stable. A figure recognizes it as itself. The figure and the light are one.
- Keyword: pratyabhijñā

---

Total: 15 scenes, ~118 seconds
