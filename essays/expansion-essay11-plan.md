# Expansion Essay 11 — Proclus: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "one thing looking at itself"
Total: 10 scenes, ~76 seconds
Palette: Diamond crystal / Silver / Gold / Deep luminous dark

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "What if the entire universe is a reflection of a single reality?"
- Visual: A mirror reflecting a luminous source. Everything in the room is a reflection.

**Scene 2** (8s) — "The One is the mirror itself, not the image in it."
- Visual: A mirror surface. Images pass across it. The mirror itself is untouched.

**Scene 3** (8s) — "A diamond has many facets — each shows the entire diamond."
- Visual: A rotating diamond form. Each facet catches light differently. Each shows the whole.

**Scene 4** (8s) — "Every god is a self-complete henad — the whole from a specific angle."
- Visual: Multiple facets radiating from one center. Each facet labeled as a perspective.

**Scene 5** (8s) — "Remaining, proceeding, returning — the triadic structure."
- Visual: A point that stays still (remaining). A ray that goes out (proceeding). A curve that comes back (returning).

**Scene 6** (8s) — "Intellect is being and knowing simultaneously."
- Visual: A form that is both the eye and the light — seeing and being seen are the same act.

**Scene 7** (8s) — "Soul has forgotten it is also Intellect. It descends into time and space."
- Visual: A point descending through levels, forgetting at each level. A memory trace remains.

**Scene 8** (6s) — "The hierarchy — gods, daimons, souls, matter — same thing at different compression."
- Visual: A vertical hierarchy. Each level contains the levels above in compressed form.

**Scene 9** (8s) — "The daimons translate the will of the gods into the experience of souls."
- Visual: A middle figure receiving light from above and transmitting it below. The hands of the divine.

**Scene 10** (8s) — "You are one facet. Polish it. The whole shines more brightly."
- Visual: A facet being refined — becoming clearer, more transparent. The whole diamond brightens.

---

Total: 10 scenes, ~76 seconds
