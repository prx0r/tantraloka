# Expansion Essay 15 — Suhrawardi / Ishraq: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "the light that illuminates itself"
Total: 12 scenes, ~90 seconds
Palette: Emerald / Dawn-gold / Luminous white / Deep night

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "Light is visible by itself — it needs nothing else to see it."
- Visual: A point of light that illuminates itself. No external source. Self-luminous.
- Keyword: nūr

**Scene 2** (8s) — "Between the world of pure Lights and the sensory universe — the mundus imaginalis."
- Visual: A threshold between two realms. One side: pure radiance. The other: dark shapes. Between them: a shimmering corridor.
- Keyword: mundus imaginalis

**Scene 3** (8s) — "The emerald cities of Hurqalya on the cosmic mountain Qaf."
- Visual: Green crystalline city on a mountain peak. Translucent, glowing from within.
- Keyword: Hurqalya

**Scene 4** (6s) — "Na-koja-abad — the land that is nowhere. The cosmic north within you."
- Visual: A compass that points inward. The "land that is nowhere" as the center of consciousness.
- Keyword: Na-koja-abad

**Scene 5** (8s) — "Perfect Nature — the Guide of Light who has been with you since before birth."
- Visual: A luminous figure emerging. Not external — it is the viewer's own higher nature.
- Keyword: al-tiba' al-tamm

**Scene 6** (8s) — "Thou art the Spirit who gave birth to me, and the Child to whom my spirit gives birth."
- Visual: Two figures facing each other, connected by a thread of light. Each gives birth to the other.
- Keyword: syzygy

**Scene 7** (8s) — "The midnight sun — at the darkest point of the night, the sun shines."
- Visual: Profound darkness. A point of brilliant light appears and expands. The dawn that comes without external source.
- Keyword: nisf al-layl

**Scene 8** (8s) — "Every being has a fravarti — a counterpart above. Light upon light."
- Visual: Two identical points of light, one above the other. A vertical column of paired lights ascending.
- Keyword: fravarti

**Scene 9** (8s) — "Morning knowledge and evening knowledge — the two ways of knowing."
- Visual: Two figures: one looking at a landscape from outside, one becoming the landscape. Observer vs participant.
- Keyword: cognitio matutina

**Scene 10** (6s) — "The Emerald Rock — translucent, luminous, the goal of the journey."
- Visual: A crystalline rock form, glowing from within. The seeker approaches it.
- Keyword: zarqā'

**Scene 11** (8s) — "The Chinvat Bridge — you decide which self you will be."
- Visual: A bridge of light crossing from darkness into radiance. A figure at its center.
- Keyword: Chinvat

**Scene 12** (8s) — "You are already light — a candle that has forgotten it is fire."
- Visual: A candle whose flame is it. The candle and the flame are one. The light recognizes itself.
- Keyword: ishrāq

---

Total: 12 scenes, ~90 seconds
Palette core: Emerald / Dawn-gold / Luminous white / Deep night
