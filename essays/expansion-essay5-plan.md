# Expansion Essay 5 — Rasa: Beat-by-Beat Scene Plan

Essay: "pain is juice"
Total: 12 scenes, ~82 seconds

---

**Scene 1** (6s) — "A song you should turn off but can't. A movie that wrecks you."
- Visual: A music note or film frame that draws you in despite knowing it will hurt. A form that is both beautiful and painful.
- Keyword: vedanā

**Scene 2** (8s) — "A philosopher called it rasa. The juice. Nine of them."
- Visual: Nine colored droplets/cups, each a different color, each labeled with a rasa. The nine flavors of experience.
- Keyword: nava-rasa

**Scene 3** (8s) — "The same thing that makes you cry at a film makes flowers grow."
- Visual: A tear falling and, as it lands, a flower blooms. The same vitality in both.
- Keyword: saṃvit

**Scene 4** (6s) — "Grief — let it sit in your chest like a stone."
- Visual: A dark, heavy stone form at the chest. The transition from clenched fist to open cup.
- Keyword: śoka

**Scene 5** (8s) — "The goddess who is the Absolute is aesthetic rapture — camatkāra."
- Visual: A goddess-like luminous form at center. Radiating wonder in all directions.
- Keyword: camatkāra

**Scene 6** (6s) — "Tasting versus consuming. The emotion is wine, the witness is tasting."
- Visual: A cup being tasted slowly vs gulped. The witness as the tongue that savors.
- Keyword: āsvādana

**Scene 7** (8s) — "The savouring of one's own self — beyond objects, beyond poetry."
- Visual: A form tasting itself — closed loop of self-delight. The subject and object are one.
- Keyword: ātmananda

**Scene 8** (6s) — "Rest in the feeling. Not fighting. Not holding. Not analyzing."
- Visual: A form resting in warm water. Floating. Not struggling. Just being held.
- Keyword: viśrānti

**Scene 9** (8s) — "When 'I am sad' becomes 'there is sadness' — grief becomes rasa."
- Visual: The phrase "I am sad" transforms into "there is sadness." A shift from identity to witness. The grief becomes golden.
- Keyword: sākṣin

**Scene 10** (8s) — "The one who rests in the power of bliss becomes the bliss itself."
- Visual: A form dissolves into its own golden light. The witness and the witnessed become one.
- Keyword: tadātmya

**Scene 11** (6s) — "Grief still hurts. The witness is made of something grief cannot touch."
- Visual: Fire burning around a clear, untouched center. The center is the witness.
- Keyword: avikriya

**Scene 12** (8s) — "Ride it. Let it crest. Let it crash. Let it dissolve. Another wave is already forming."
- Visual: A series of waves — cresting, crashing, dissolving, reforming. Each wave a different color (a different rasa). You are the ocean, not the wave.
- Keyword: ūrmi

---

Total: 12 scenes, ~82 seconds
